## Overview

**Schedule** sets two things that apply across every booking calendar in the business: the **timezone** all times are shown and stored in, and each team member's **working hours**.

You'll find it under **Appointments → Settings → Schedule**.

This page is the foundation the rest of the booking system sits on. Get the timezone wrong and every calendar in the business offers the wrong times. Get someone's working hours wrong and they'll be booked outside them, or never booked at all.

<Note>
  **Before you start**

  - Create at least one booking calendar first. See [1) Creating Your Booking Calendar](/cal-calendars). Working hours only matter once something is bookable.
  - Invite your team members. Only people who are already members of the business appear in the working-hours list.
</Note>

---

## 1. Set the Business Booking Defaults

The **Availability** panel at the top controls booking defaults for the whole business: *the timezone slots are shown in and the default slot length*.

<Frame caption="Settings → Schedule. Business timezone and slot length at the top, per-member working hours below.">
  <img src="/images/appointments/schedule/01-schedule.png" alt="The Schedule sub-tab of Appointments Settings, showing an Availability panel with a Timezone dropdown and a Slot length field with a Save button, and a Working hours section listing a team member with their weekly hours." />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the timezone">
    Pick the timezone your business books in, for example `(UTC+05:30) Sri Jayawardenepura`.

    Every time in the product is interpreted in this timezone: the working hours below, the hours on each calendar, and the times shown on **Business Cal**. Invitees still see slots converted to *their* own timezone on the booking page, so this is not what your customers see. It is the reference clock everything else is measured against.

    Click **Save**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the default slot length">
    **Slot** is the default meeting length, in minutes, applied to new booking calendars.

    Changing it does **not** alter calendars that already exist. Each calendar keeps its own **Duration** under **Details → Scheduling Rules**. Set this to whatever length you create most often so you're not editing every new calendar.

    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Change the timezone before you take real bookings, not after.** Existing appointments keep the time they were booked at, so shifting the business timezone later changes how already-booked meetings read on your calendar. If you must change it, do it when nothing is on the books.
</Warning>

## 2. Set Each Team Member's Working Hours

Below the defaults, **Working hours · per team member** lists everyone in the business. Each person has their own weekly grid.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a team member">
    Select the person you want to edit. Their week is listed Sunday through Saturday.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set their hours for each day">
    For each day, set a start and end time, for example `9:00am` to `5:00pm`. Mark a day **Unavailable** to close it.

    New members default to **9:00am–5:00pm, Monday through Saturday**, with Sunday unavailable.

    Click **Save** when you're done with that person.

    <Frame caption="Working hours are per person, and separate from each calendar's own hours.">
      <img src="/images/appointments/schedule/02-working-hours.png" alt="The Working hours section showing a team member with Sunday marked unavailable and Monday through Saturday set to 9:00am to 5:00pm, with a note explaining the hours are in the business timezone." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

### How working hours combine with calendar hours

This is the single most important thing on the page, and the product states it directly underneath the grid:

> These hours are in the business timezone. You are bookable at these hours ∩ each event type's own hours.

In plain terms: **a slot is only offered when both sets of hours agree.**

| Your working hours | The calendar's hours | What invitees can book |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mon–Fri 9–5 | Mon–Fri 9–5 | Mon–Fri 9–5 |
| Mon–Fri 9–5 | Mon–Fri 9–12 | Mon–Fri 9–12 |
| Mon–Fri 1–5 | Mon–Fri 9–12 | **Nothing**. The two never overlap |
| Saturday 9–5 | Mon–Fri only | **Nothing on Saturday** |

If a calendar is showing no times at all, an accidental non-overlap here is the usual reason.

<Note>
  A team member with **no working hours configured at all** is treated as unrestricted, so the calendar's own hours decide. A member who *has* been configured but has no hours on a given day is unavailable that day. Removing all of someone's hours is not the same as never setting them.
</Note>

<Warning>
  **There is no per-person timezone.** Everyone's hours are read in the business timezone set at the top of this page. A team member in another country needs their hours entered as the business-timezone equivalent. Someone working 9am to 5pm in London, with the business set to Sri Lanka time, should be entered as 1:30pm to 9:30pm.

  **There is also no holiday or time-off setting here.** A day off only removes slots if it's a busy event on that person's connected Google or Outlook calendar, or if you close the date on the individual calendar under **Availability → Date overrides**.
</Warning>

## Settings Reference

| Setting | What it does | Default | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Timezone** | The reference clock for all working hours, calendar hours and dashboard times. | Set when the business was created | Set once, before taking bookings. Use the timezone the team actually works in. |
| **Slot** | Default meeting length applied to newly created calendars. | 30 minutes | Match your most common call length. Doesn't affect existing calendars. |
| **Working hours** | When each team member can be booked, per day. | 9:00am–5:00pm, Mon–Sat; Sunday unavailable | Trim to real hours. Most teams should close Saturday. |
| **Unavailable** | Closes a day entirely for that person. | Sunday only | Use for regular non-working days, not one-off time off. |

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A calendar shows no available times at all">
    Almost always a non-overlap. Compare the member's working hours here against that calendar's hours under **Calendars → Availability**. A slot only appears where both agree. Also check the calendar actually has team members assigned. With none, bookings fall to the business owner, so it is the *owner's* hours that matter.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Someone is getting booked outside their working hours">
    Check you edited the right person. The grid is per team member and it is easy to save changes against the wrong one. Then confirm their hours are entered in the **business** timezone, not their own local time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Times on Business Cal look an hour or two out">
    The business booking timezone here is what **Business Cal** renders in. If times look shifted, this dropdown is the first place to check.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A team member doesn't appear in the working-hours list">
    They aren't a member of this business yet. Invite them under **Settings → Users** first, then come back. The list only shows existing members.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I changed the slot length but my calendars didn't change">
    That's expected. **Slot** only applies to calendars created after the change. Existing ones keep their own **Duration**, editable per calendar under **Details → Scheduling Rules**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do invitees see times in my timezone or theirs?">
    Theirs. The booking page detects their timezone and converts every slot into it, with a picker so they can change it. The timezone here is the reference your team's hours are measured in.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can two people have different working hours?">
    Yes. That is the point of the per-member grid. Each person's hours are independent, and a booking is only offered when someone who can take it is free.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I take a day off?">
    Not from this page. Either block the time on your connected Google or Outlook calendar, or close that specific date on the individual booking calendar under **Availability → Date overrides**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I work split hours, like mornings and late afternoons?">
    Working hours here are one window per day. For split availability, set the wider window here and use multiple time slots on the individual calendar under **Calendars → Availability**, where **Add time slot** lets you add a second window per day.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to set working hours at all?">
    No. Someone with no hours configured is unrestricted, and the calendar's own hours decide. Set them when you want to protect personal time across every calendar at once.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
