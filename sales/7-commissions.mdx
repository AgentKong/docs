Commissions turn closed deals into what you owe each rep. Set a rate once and Cortana calculates commissions automatically as payments complete, then tracks each one from pending to paid.

## Open Commissions

Go to the **Commissions** tab. Four cards summarize the range, and the **Commissions by Rep** table breaks it down.

## Set commission rates first

If everything is zero, no rates are set yet. Click **Set commission rates** (or **Settings**). For each rep, **Pick a rep**, choose a **Commission type**, and enter the rate as a percentage. Until a rep has a rate, their deals generate no commission.

## How commissions flow

Commissions **auto-generate when a tracked payment completes**, then move through three states:

- **Pending**: earned, not yet approved for payout.
- **Ready to Pay**: approved and waiting to be paid.
- **Paid**: paid out.

The **Total Liability** card is everything you still owe (pending plus ready).

## Pay reps

When you are ready to pay, use **Mark commissions as paid**. Add a **Payment reference** (for example the transfer id) so the payout is recorded. Use **Export CSV** to hand a payout run to finance or to a payroll tool.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Commissions by Rep columns">
    - **Rep**: the person earning.
    - **Rate**: their configured commission rate.
    - **Pending / Ready / Paid**: their commission in each state.
    - **Total Revenue**: revenue attributed to the rep in the range.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What a single commission records">
    Each commission line carries the **Date**, **Contact**, **Product**, **Platform**, **New Revenue Generated**, **Renewal Revenue**, the **Commission** amount, and its **Status**. New business and renewals are tracked separately so you can pay them differently if you choose.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Voiding a commission">
    If a payment is refunded or a deal is reversed, void that commission so it is removed from what you owe. Voided commissions drop out of the pending and ready totals.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I have revenue but commissions are zero.">
    No rate is set for that rep. Open Set commission rates and give them a rate. Commissions only generate for reps who have one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A rep was paid but the card still shows the amount as ready.">
    Mark those commissions as paid and add a payment reference. Until then they stay in Ready to Pay.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do renewals pay commission?">
    Renewal revenue is tracked separately from new business on each commission line, so you can see and handle it on its own.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
