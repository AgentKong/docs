The Call Recordings tab is where every sales call lands, connected to the deal it belongs to and the rep who ran it. Use it to review how deals were won or lost and to coach your team.

## Open Call Recordings

Go to the **Call Recordings** tab. Recordings flow in from your connected call recorder and are matched to the rep and the deal outcome.

## Filter to the calls you want

Four controls narrow the list:

1. **Date range** (top-left): the period to show.
2. **Deal outcome**: filter to a stage of the pipeline. Options are **Won deals**, **All calls**, **Verbal commit**, **Proposal requested**, **Follow-up scheduled**, **Under review**, **Stalled**, **Lost**, and **No decision**.
3. **Reps**: show one rep or **All reps**.
4. **Search titles**: type to find a call by its title.

## Group the view

With **All calls** selected, switch between **By Meeting Type** and **By Rep** to organize the list either by the kind of call or by who ran it. This makes it easy to review, for example, every discovery call, or every call one rep took.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The deal-outcome stages">
    Each recording is tagged with where the deal stands: **Won deals** closed, **Verbal commit** agreed but not yet paid, **Proposal requested**, **Follow-up scheduled**, **Under review**, **Stalled**, **Lost**, or **No decision**. Filtering by a stage shows only the calls at that point, so you can review, for example, every deal that stalled.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where recordings come from">
    Recordings come from your connected call recorder and are matched to the rep and the contact. If a rep's calls are missing, check that their recorder is connected and that the date range covers the calls.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No recordings are showing.">
    Confirm the date range covers days with calls, widen the deal-outcome filter to All calls, and check that your call recorder is connected. A stage filter like Won deals shows nothing if there were no won deals in the range.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I find one specific call?">
    Use Search titles to match the call's title, and set the rep filter if you know who took it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I see every call for one rep?">
    Yes. Select the rep in the reps filter, or choose All calls and switch to By Rep.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
