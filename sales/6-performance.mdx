The Performance tab shows how your team is closing over the selected date range. Read it top to bottom: headline numbers, then the funnel, then each rep, then the trend.

## Stat cards

Four cards summarize the range, each with a sparkline and a change versus the previous period:

- **Links Sent**: payment links your reps shared.
- **Payments Completed**: links that resulted in a paid checkout.
- **New Revenue Generated**: revenue from first-time payments in the range.
- **Total Revenue**: all revenue in the range, including recurring.

## The funnel

The funnel shows **Taken**, **Showed**, and **Closed**, with your **show rate** and **close rate** on the right, plus totals for the range (booked, upcoming, and amount closed). Click any bar to see the people behind that stage.

## The per-rep table

Each rep gets a row with their calls and their links side by side:

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Calls / Taken / Showed / No-show / Unmarked | The rep's call outcomes |
| Show % | Showed divided by taken |
| Sent / Opened | Links the rep sent and how many were opened |
| Closes / Close % | Deals closed and the rate |
| Revenue | Revenue attributed to the rep |

A dash in a column means that rail does not apply to that rep. For example an AI agent that only sends links has dashes in the call columns. Click a call number to drill into the calls behind it, or click a rep to see their deal history.

## The trend chart

The **Trend** chart plots per-rep activity over the range. Toggle between **Links sent**, **New payments**, and **New revenue** to see who is driving each.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How show rate and close rate are calculated">
    Show rate is showed divided by taken. Close rate is closed divided by showed. Both use the sales-call event types and the unique-contact setting from Funnel setup, so if these look off, check Funnel setup first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Reps, AI agents, and Unknown">
    Human reps show both calls and links. AI agents (for example a DM setter) send links but take no calls, so their call columns are dashes. Unknown groups activity that could not be matched to a rep.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What the drill-downs show">
    Clicking a funnel bar or a rep's call number opens the list of calls or deals behind that number, so you can see the actual contacts and outcomes, not just the totals.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why does a rep show 3 sends but 0 revenue?">
    Their links were sent but not yet paid, or the payments were attributed to a different rep. Open the rep to see their deal history.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My revenue looks low compared to Stripe.">
    The Performance tab is scoped to your date range and to closes counted by Funnel setup. Widen the range, or check whether closes outside payment links need a conversion event added in Funnel setup.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is a whole column dashes for one rep?">
    That rail does not apply to them. AI agents that only send links have no call numbers, so those columns are dashes.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
