# Video audiences

A video audience is a list of the people who watched one of your videos, for example *everyone who watched 75% of your sales video in the last 45 days*. Cortana keeps it up to date on its own: people join as they watch and leave when they no longer match.

Each audience holds two kinds of people:

- **Contacts**: viewers you know, with a name, an email or a phone number.
- **Visitors**: viewers who watched but have not filled a form or booked yet.

Turn on **Sync with ads** and both reach Meta, so you can retarget everyone who watched, not only the people who gave you their email.

<Note>
  **Before you start**

  - **Video Tracking is in beta.** If you don't see **Video Tracking** in your sidebar, it isn't switched on for your account yet.
  - Your videos need viewers. Audiences count what people watched in your Cortana videos.
  - To send an audience to Meta: Meta connected and your ad account selected in **Integrations**.
  - To also send the viewers who never filled a form: your Meta pixel connected in [AI Ad Optimization](/6-ai-ad-optimization), and that pixel shared with the same ad account in Meta Events Manager.
</Note>

## Create a video audience

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Audiences tab">
    In your business, open **Video Tracking** and click the **Audiences** tab, next to **Library** and **Analytics**.

    The first time, you see **No video audiences yet**. Click **New audience**.

    <Frame caption="Video Tracking, Audiences tab, before your first audience.">
      <img src="/images/video-tracking/video-audiences/01-open-audiences-tab.png" alt="The Video Tracking page with the Audiences tab selected (1) and the New audience button in the empty state (2)." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Answer the three questions">
    The **Watched a video** panel asks, in order:

    1. **Which video?** Pick one video, or leave **Any video**.
    2. **How much of it?** **25%**, **50%**, **75%** or **95%**. A person qualifies when they watched at least that much.
    3. **When?** **Any time**, **In the last 7 days**, **In the last 30 days**, **In the last 90 days**, **In the last year**, or **Custom number of days**.
    4. With **Custom number of days**, type the number in **How many days back?**: any whole number from 1 to 3,650.
    5. Click **Add to filter**.

    <Frame caption="The video questions: Sales VSL, 75% or more, in the last 45 days.">
      <img src="/images/video-tracking/video-audiences/02-answer-the-video-questions.png" alt="The Watched a video panel with Which video set to Sales VSL (1), How much of it set to 75% (2), When set to Custom number of days (3), How many days back set to 45 (4), and the Add to filter button (5)." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Name it and save">
    **Name this audience** opens with a name written from your answers, for example *Watched 75%+ of Sales VSL, last 45 days*. Keep it or type your own, then click **Save audience**.

    <Frame caption="Name the audience. The line under the title repeats the rule.">
      <img src="/images/video-tracking/video-audiences/03-name-the-audience.png" alt="The Name this audience dialog with the name field (1) and the Save audience button (2)." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for the first count">
    The new audience shows **Counting** while Cortana finds everyone who matches. This takes up to 15 minutes.

    <Frame caption="A new audience counting its members.">
      <img src="/images/video-tracking/video-audiences/04-counting.png" alt="The Audiences tab listing the new audience, Sales VSL: watched most of it, with its rule Watched 75%+ of Sales VSL, last 45 days and the status Counting." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read your audience

Each audience shows its rule and how many people are in it.

1. The count: contacts first, then visitors, for example **5 contacts · 27 visitors**.
2. **See who** opens Contacts with this audience already chosen.
3. **Sync with ads** sends it to Meta. Once it is on, the button reads **Syncing to Meta**.

The **⋯** menu holds **Archive audience**.

## See who is in it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contacts">
    Click **See who**. Contacts opens with the audience chosen in the audience menu (1). The switch next to it (2) shows **contacts** first: the people you know, in the usual contacts table.

    <Frame caption="Contacts, filtered to the audience. The switch moves between contacts and visitors.">
      <img src="/images/video-tracking/video-audiences/07-see-who-contacts.png" alt="The Contacts page with the audience Sales VSL: watched most of it chosen in the audience menu (1) and the switch reading 5 contacts and 27 visitors (2), above a table of five contacts." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the visitors">
    Click **visitors** in the switch. Each visitor shows as **Anonymous visitor** with a short code, their device, browser and system, up to three videos with how far they got in each, and when they were first and last seen.

    <Frame caption="The visitors: people who watched but have not been identified yet.">
      <img src="/images/video-tracking/video-audiences/08-see-who-visitors.png" alt="The visitors list of the audience, opened with the visitors side of the switch (1). Each row reads Anonymous visitor with a short code, a device such as Desktop, Chrome, macOS, video chips such as Sales VSL 95%, and Last seen and First seen times." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

When a visitor fills a form or books, they move to the contacts side at the next daily count.

## Send it to Meta

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on the Meta switch">
    Click **Sync with ads**, then turn on **Meta** (1). If you have more than one ad account, pick the one to use.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check both lines">
    Two lines tell you how it stands:

    - **Synced … ago** (2): your contacts, sent to Meta as a customer list. Meta can take up to a day to show changes.
    - **Anonymous viewers** (3): Cortana also creates a second Meta audience, named after yours with *(anonymous viewers)* at the end, that fills from your video events. Meta keeps people in it for up to 180 days.

    <Frame caption="The Meta sync with both lines: the contact list and the anonymous viewers.">
      <img src="/images/video-tracking/video-audiences/06-sync-with-meta.png" alt="The Send this audience to your ad accounts panel with the Meta switch on (1), the line Synced 6 minutes ago (2), and the line Anonymous viewers: a second Meta audience fills from your video events, Meta keeps them up to 180 days (3)." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

In Ads Manager you then target the two audiences together, or each on its own.

## Details

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How the video rule works">
    - Cortana notes when each viewer first passes **25%, 50%, 75% and 95%** of a video. These are the only amounts a rule can ask for.
    - **Any video** counts a viewer who passed the amount on any of your videos.
    - The window counts back from today. Change it later and the audience re-counts within 15 minutes: people come back in or leave.
    - The same question, **Watched a video**, is in the **Filter** on the Contacts page. There you can combine it with webinars, conversions and tags, then click **Save as audience**. Every window there (video, webinar, conversion) takes the same **Custom number of days**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Contacts and visitors: who counts as what">
    - A **contact** is in the audience when one of their own watches passed the amount in the window.
    - A **visitor** is a person Cortana knows only by their browsers. Someone who watched on their phone and their laptop counts once when Cortana could tie the two together.
    - A person who is already a contact is never also counted as a visitor. When a visitor becomes a contact, they move sides at the next daily count, so for up to a day the same person can show on both.
    - Tags, conversions and webinars only exist for contacts. An audience that needs one of them with **Match all** has contacts and no visitors. With **Match any**, visitors are judged on the video questions alone.
    - Bots never count.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What reaches Meta, and when">
    - **Your contacts**: hashed emails, phone numbers and names, as a customer list. Only when **Sync with ads** is on for that audience.
    - **Video events**: every time a viewer passes 25, 50, 75 or 95% of a video, Cortana sends a video event to each Meta pixel connected in [AI Ad Optimization](/6-ai-ad-optimization), within about 5 minutes. An event is sent only when Meta can recognize the viewer: their browser carries Meta's cookie, or they are a contact with an email or a phone. These events are sent for all your videos, whether or not an audience uses them.
    - **The anonymous viewers' audience**: built in Meta from those video events, on a pixel that your ad account can use. Cortana updates it when you change the rule, the window or the video. A window longer than 180 days is capped at 180 there, Meta's limit.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When there is no anonymous viewers' audience">
    The **Anonymous viewers** line says why, in words:

    - *Anonymous viewers are not sent: this audience's rule needs something only contacts have (a tag, a conversion or a webinar).* The rule uses **Match all** with a tag, conversion or webinar.
    - *Anonymous viewers are not sent: Cortana does not send two video rules joined by "match all" to Meta yet, so only contacts are sent.* Use **Match any**, or one video question.
    - *Anonymous viewers are not sent yet: none of this workspace's Meta pixels is shared with this ad account.* Share your pixel with the ad account in Meta Events Manager. Cortana tries again an hour later.

    When Meta refuses a change (for example, the connection lost access), the line turns amber with Meta's words and Cortana tries again an hour later.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When the numbers change">
    | What | When |
    |---|---|
    | A new or edited audience | counted within 15 minutes |
    | New viewers | added every 15 minutes |
    | A window running out, a visitor becoming a contact | the daily count |
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Archive, pause and limits">
    - **Archive audience** (in the **⋯** menu) stops counting and syncing. The contact list stays in Meta as it was; remove it in Ads Manager if you no longer need it. The anonymous viewers' audience is removed from Meta once no ad set uses it.
    - If a video named in a rule is deleted, the audience shows **Paused: a video in its rule was deleted**. Nobody joins or leaves until you change the rule. **Any video** never pauses.
    - Up to 50 audiences per workspace, video or not.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why does my video audience say 0 visitors?">
    Either nobody unidentified watched that far in the window yet, or the rule also asks for a tag, conversion or webinar with **Match all**. Those only exist for contacts, so the audience has contacts only. Use **Match any** if you want visitors judged on the video alone.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My audience has said Counting for a long time">
    A new or edited audience is counted within 15 minutes. If it still says **Counting** after half an hour, contact support from the chat in the app.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why don't contacts plus visitors match my video's view count?">
    An audience only holds people who passed the amount you chose, within the window you chose. A person counts once even with several watches or devices, bots never count, and someone who watched without reaching the milestone is not in it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The anonymous viewers audience in Meta is empty">
    Check the **Anonymous viewers** line in **Sync with ads** first: it says if the audience could not be made, and why. If it says the audience fills from your video events, check that your pixel is connected in [AI Ad Optimization](/6-ai-ad-optimization) and shared with that ad account. Meta also needs to recognize the viewers: people who watched in a browser Meta knows. Give it time; Meta shows a size once enough people are in it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use 60% instead of 75%?">
    No. Cortana notes 25%, 50%, 75% and 95%, so a rule picks one of those. Pick the one just below what you had in mind: 50% includes everyone who watched 60%.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can one audience use two videos?">
    Yes, from the **Filter** on the Contacts page: add a **Watched a video** question for each video, then **Save as audience**. With **Match any**, both your contacts and your visitors go to Meta. With **Match all**, only the contacts go for now.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens in Meta when I archive an audience?">
    The contact list stays in Meta as it was, so a running ad keeps its audience. The anonymous viewers' audience is removed from Meta once no ad set uses it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
