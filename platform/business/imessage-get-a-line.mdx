# Get an iMessage line

An iMessage line is a brand-new phone number, set up on a real iPhone and Mac, that texts your leads as blue bubbles. You buy it from the iMessage page in your business. The flow is four steps, in this order: four things to know, the rules, your line, payment. It takes about five minutes, and the number is usually live within 24 to 48 hours.

<Note>
  **Before you start**

  - **A real person to text from.** The line must carry a real first and last name and a real photo of the person who handles the conversations. Have that photo ready (PNG, JPEG, WebP or HEIC, up to 25 MB).
  - **Your legal company name and street address.** The carrier registers the number to a business at an address.
  - **Opted-in leads only.** Ad responders, form fills, booked calls, customers, people who gave you their number. A cold or purchased list will get the number retired.
  - **Payment.** The setup fee comes from your wallet if it covers it, otherwise from your saved card or a card you enter.
</Note>

## Where to start

1. Open your business and click **Settings** in the left menu.
2. Open the **Phone / iMessage** tab and pick **iMessage / Blue Bubble**.
3. The card reads **No iMessage line yet**. Click **Get an iMessage line**.

A progress bar labelled **Line setup progress** shows the four steps: **Before you buy**, **The rules**, **Your line**, **Payment**. You can go **Back** at any step; what you typed is kept.

## Step 1: Before you buy

*Four things to know about your new line. Tick each to continue.* Each one is a checkbox. All four must be ticked before **Continue** lights up.

| What you tick | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| **Your line takes about two days to go live** | A real iPhone, Mac and eSIM are set up for your number; we email you the moment it is live. |
| **Opted-in contacts only** | Three "Report Junk" taps retire a number. Cold or purchased lists end the line. |
| **The first message to a new contact is plain text** | No links, no attachments, no voice notes until the lead replies. |
| **A brand-new number** | It cannot be ported in or out and should never be printed where strangers will text it. |

## Step 2: The rules

Seven rules, each with its own checkbox, written in the first person because you are the one agreeing to them. Then one master checkbox: **I've read and agree to all seven rules, and I understand setup takes 24–48 hours**. The button **Agree & continue** lights up when all eight are ticked. The moment you tick each one is recorded with your order.

1. **Opted-in leads.** I'll only message opted-in, warm leads: ad responders, form fills, booked calls, customers, people who gave me their number. Never cold or purchased lists.
2. **A real person.** My sender is a real person: a real first and last name and a real photo of the person who handles the conversations. Never a business, brand, logo or role name.
3. **Human texts.** My messages read like one person texting another. No links until the lead replies, nothing that reads like a promo blast.
4. **Daily caps.** Each line reaches at most 50 new contacts a day and 150 messages a day. The caps protect the number and are not lifted.
5. **Attachments from the second message.** Attachments (voice notes, images, videos) are allowed from the second message on. Any attachment in a first message is rejected.
6. **iMessage first, SMS as a fallback.** iMessage is sent natively. SMS is a fallback through a carrier route and its delivery is not guaranteed.
7. **Replacements and refunds.** If Apple restricts the number, the first replacement is free, the second and third cost \$50 each, a fourth ends the line. Setup and subscription are non-refundable.

## Step 3: Your line

*The person, business and address behind this number.* Three short groups of fields.

**Who texts from this line?**
- **First name** and **Last name**: the real person your leads will see.
- **Headshot**: click **Upload photo** and choose a PNG, JPEG, WebP or HEIC photo up to 25 MB. *A real photo of this person; leads see it.* You can **Replace** it before you continue.

**What business is behind it?**
- **Legal company name**
- **Company phone**

**Where is it registered?**
- **Address line 1**, **Address line 2 (optional)**, **City**, **State** and **ZIP**. The number's area follows the state and ZIP you give.

**How should it ring?**
- **Forward calls to (optional)**: the number that rings when someone calls your iMessage line. *Leave blank for no forwarding.*

Click **Continue**.

## Step 4: Payment

**How many lines?** Pick **1** or **2**. Two lines means two numbers, each with its own daily caps.

The price is shown as **setup + \$297/month per line**. The setup fee is **\$497**, charged once. The monthly fee runs as its own subscription on your card from the first bill date.

How you pay depends on your wallet:

- **Wallet covers the setup fee.** The button reads **Pay \$0 and order my line** and the note says *The setup fee will be deducted from your wallet.*
- **Saved card.** The note says *Your saved card will be charged for setup.*
- **No card on file.** The button reads **Pay \$497 and order my line** (or **lines**). Click it, and a **Secure card details** form opens. Enter your card and click the button again.

If the card is declined you see *Your card was declined. Please use another card.* and nothing is ordered. If your connection drops after the charge, come back to this step: the flow shows *Payment received. Your order details are saved; continue to Payment to finish your order.* and finishes without charging you again.

Click **Finish ordering my line**.

## Done: your line is ordered

The last screen reads **Your line is ordered.** with *Your number is being ordered now.* and *Usually live within 24–48 hours, we email you the moment it is.* Click **See your line** to open [Your iMessage line](/platform/business/imessage-line). The card shows **Setting up** until the carrier activates the number, then **Ready** while we move your agent onto it, then **Live**.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What the setup fee and the monthly fee pay for">
    The **\$497 setup fee** covers the real device, the eSIM and the number, once. It is non-refundable.

    The **\$297 a month per line** is a subscription on your card. The first bill comes on your first bill date, 28 days after the line is ordered. If you buy two lines, the subscription carries a quantity of two.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why the first message must be plain text">
    Apple treats a first message with a link, a photo or a voice note as spam from an unknown number. Cortana enforces it for you: any file or voice note on a first message is held and the words go out as text. From the lead's first reply on, anything goes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why the sender must be a real person">
    Leads report numbers that text like a brand. Three **Report Junk** taps retire a number. A first and last name and a real photo keep your number alive, and they are also what makes iMessage work: it is one person texting another.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why there is a daily cap">
    New numbers that send to many new contacts in a day get flagged. **50 new contacts and 150 messages a day per line** is the volume a healthy number sustains. The caps reset at midnight UTC and are not lifted. Need more? Buy a second line.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if Apple restricts the number">
    The first replacement number is free. The second and third cost \$50 each. A fourth restriction ends the line. The card shows **Getting a new number** while a replacement is in flight. Your threads stay where they are.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is recorded with your order">
    The form you filled in and the time you ticked each of the eleven boxes (four things to know, seven rules) are stored with your order, together with your IP address and browser. That record is what lets us vouch for how the number is used.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long until my line is live?">
    Usually 24 to 48 hours. We email you the moment it is. Until then the card shows **Setting up**, and then **Ready** for a short while as your agent is moved onto it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I pick my area code?">
    No. The number's area follows the state and ZIP you register the line at. There is no area code field.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I move my existing number onto iMessage?">
    No. The line is a brand-new number and it cannot be ported in or out.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I paid but the flow crashed. Was I charged twice?">
    No. Come back to the **Payment** step: it finds your existing payment and finishes the order without a second charge.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I get a refund?">
    No. Setup and subscription are non-refundable, and you agreed to that as rule 7.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the sender be my company name?">
    No. The sender must be a real first and last name with a real photo of the person who handles the conversations. A company, brand, logo or role name gets the number reported.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
