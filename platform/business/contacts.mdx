# Managing Your Contacts in Cortana

The Contacts page is your central database or Customer Relationship Management (CRM) hub within each business account. This is where every individual your AI interacts with is stored and managed. Here you can view contact details, add new contacts, manage their information, and create custom data fields and tags to enhance your AI's knowledge.

## Navigating the Contacts Page

The Contacts page is divided into three main tabs: **Contacts**, **Contact Custom Fields**, and **Contact Tags**.

## 1. The Main Contacts List

This is the default view and it displays all the contacts associated with the selected business.

- **Viewing Contacts:** All your contacts are listed in a table format, showing their name and other key details.
- **Searching and Loading:**
  - Use the **Search bar** at the top to quickly find a specific contact.
  - The list loads 50 contacts at a time. To see more, simply click the **Load More** button at the bottom of the list.
- **Adding a New Contact:**
  - Click the **+ Add Contact** button in the top-right corner.
  - A form will appear where you can manually enter the contact's information, including basic details, business information, location, and any custom fields.
  - Click "Create" to save the new contact.
- **Viewing a Contact's Conversation:**
  - Clicking directly on a contact's name in the list will take you to the **Conversations** page, filtered to show only the interaction history with that specific individual. This allows you to quickly review all communications.

## Actions for Individual Contacts

On the far right of each contact's row, there is a **three-dot menu** that provides several actions:

- **Update:** This opens a pop-up window where you can edit all the information for that contact, including their name, email, phone number, company, address, tags, and any custom fields you have created.
- **View Session Logs:** This takes you to a detailed log of every interaction and event the AI has had with the contact. It shows the type of interaction (e.g., message, event, tool call), the input message, the agent's reply, and other technical data. This is extremely useful for troubleshooting and understanding the AI's decision-making process, especially if an agent isn't responding as expected.
- **Copy ID:** This copies the contact's unique ID to your clipboard.
- **Delete:** This allows you to permanently remove the contact and all their associated data from the system.

## 2. Contact Custom Fields

Custom fields allow you to store additional, specific information about your contacts beyond the standard fields. This data is crucial for personalizing your AI's conversations.

- **Automatic Sync with Your CRM:** If you have integrated a CRM like GoHighLevel, Cortana will automatically sync and display all the custom fields from that platform. The sync is two-way, meaning updates in either Cortana or your CRM will be reflected in the other.
- **Manual Creation:**
  - Navigate to the **Contact Custom Fields** tab.
  - Click **Add Custom Field**.
  - A pop-up will appear. Define your new field:
    - **Label:** Give the field a descriptive name (e.g., "Monthly Revenue," "Deal Size," "Follow-up Status").
    - **Type:** Choose the type of data this field will hold (Text, Number, Date, URL, Single-Select, Multi-Select).
  - Click **Create Field**.
- **Viewing Contacts with Custom Fields:** In the Contacts column, you can click the number to see a list of all contacts who have that specific custom field filled out.

## 3. Contact Tags

Tags are simple labels you can apply to contacts to segment and organize them.

- **Automatic Sync:** Like custom fields, tags have a two-way sync with integrated CRMs like GoHighLevel. Adding or removing a tag in one system will update the other.
- **Source:** The Source column shows where a tag originated (e.g., GHL for GoHighLevel, MANUAL, or SYSTEM).
- **System Tags:**
  - Cortana uses a special, non-deletable system tag called **Agent-Off**.
  - This tag is automatically applied to a contact when you manually turn off the AI for them in the Conversations page.
  - You can also add this "Agent-Off" tag to a contact within your CRM (like GoHighLevel) to disable the AI for that contact, and the change will sync to Cortana.

## 4. Merged contacts

Cortana keeps one contact per person. When two contacts turn out to be the same human (the same email or phone on both, or a payment, form, or tracked link that ties them together), the newer one is folded into the older one automatically: sessions, conversions, bookings, deals, notes, and chat history all move across. Nothing is deleted.

Every merge is written down, and the contact tells you why.

1. Open the contact (click its row, or the **Update** action in the three-dot menu).
2. In the right-hand column, click the **Merges** tab.
3. Each entry names the contact that was absorbed and says in one sentence why the two were merged. For example: **Merged into Sam Carter because both had the phone +1 (415) 555-0199. Jordan got it on Sep 10, 2026 from a booking form. Sam's contact is older, so it was kept.**
4. Below the sentence you see when it happened and what came across, for example **Brought over 6 deals, 3 notes, 13 appointments**.

### Undoing a merge

Click **Undo** on an entry to put the absorbed contact back as its own record. Everything that came across in that merge moves back. Anything added since the merge stays on the current contact.

If the merge happened because one contact had picked up the other's main email or phone as an extra identifier (a booking form or a CRM can do that), Undo removes that identifier from the restored contact so the two do not merge again. The confirmation says exactly which one, for example **Undo removes +1 (415) 555-0199 from Jordan Lee: it is Sam Carter's main phone.**

Some entries show **Can’t undo** with a lock instead of the button. Hover it to read why. The two reasons:

- **Both contacts have the same main email or phone.** That is a true duplicate: separating them would not stick, they would be merged again within minutes. The message names the value. Change it on one of them first, then undo.
- **The merge moved more records than it logged.** Very large contacts (tens of thousands of sessions) cannot be replayed in reverse automatically. Split them by hand instead.

### FAQ

**Why does my contact have a phone number nobody typed here?**
Open the **Merges** tab. If a contact with that phone was absorbed, the entry says so and says where that contact got the number.

**Two different people got merged into one. How do I split them?**
Open the **Merges** tab and click **Undo** on the entry. If one contact had picked up the other's main phone or email, Undo removes it and tells you so. If it says **Can’t undo**, hover it to read why: usually both contacts have the same main email or phone. Change it on one of them, then undo.

**Will undoing a merge bring back everything?**
Everything the merge moved goes back: sessions, conversions, bookings, deals, notes, chat messages, tags, and custom fields the absorbed contact owned. Custom fields the surviving contact already had, and anything added after the merge, stay where they are.

**Does the explanation use AI?**
No. The sentence is written from facts recorded at the moment of the merge, so it is the same every time you open it.
