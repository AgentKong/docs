## Overview

Once a week your rep, Diego or Sophia, posts your weekly attribution report in your Slack channel. In one message you see:

- every conversion that came in over the last 7 days, next to the week before,
- what is tracking well,
- payments that came in with no ad behind them, and the fix,
- anything in your setup that still needs a fix, with the fix.

When there is something to fix, it ends with "want me to fix these?". Reply in the thread and Cortana takes it from there.

## When it arrives

- **On Monday**, between 9am and 5pm in your business's time zone. That is the first business on your account, or Eastern time if it has no time zone set.
- **Never before 8am Toronto time on Monday.** In Europe that means Monday afternoon. In Asia and Australia, it arrives Tuesday morning.
- **Sometimes later in the week**, up to Friday 5pm your time. For example, it waits until a day has passed since someone on our team last wrote in your channel.
- **Once a week** at most.

## What's in it

Here is a full report, with made-up names:

```text
hey, here's your weekly attribution report for the 7 days to Nov 17

41 conversions came in, 34 the week before:
• "Lead": 25 (22 the week before)
• "Booked Call": 12 (9 the week before)
• "Purchase": 4 (3 the week before)

what looks good:

4 payments ($1,988) came in with no tracked visit:
• Jane D. $497 on Nov 12
• Sam K. $97 on Nov 11
• and 2 more
the fix, either one:
• send every payment through a Cortana payment link
• or add the email a buyer paid with to their GoHighLevel contact as a second email
how to send a payment link: https://help.usecortana.ai/sales/3-send-payment-links

still open on your setup:
• your "Free Audit" calendar's bookings aren't counted yet: connect it to a conversion
```

A part with nothing to say is left out.

## Want me to fix these?

Reply in the report's thread. Cortana picks it up there.

For a change she can make herself, like a form or calendar that is not counted yet, or URL parameters on a Meta ad, she shows you exactly what will change and waits for your yes. Then her message carries an **Undo** button. See [Cortana does it for you](/platform/cortana-vip#cortana-does-it-for-you).

Meta reviews an ad again after its URL parameters change, and it may not deliver until Meta approves it. So she does one ad at a time.

## When conversions drop to zero

If nothing came in this week after 10 or more the week before, your rep sends a short question instead of the full report:

```text
hey, here's your weekly attribution report for the 7 days to Nov 17

0 conversions came in, 34 the week before
Did you guys change anything, or is this expected?
```

Reply in the thread. The rest of the report (payments, open fixes, ads) comes with next week's.

## Weeks with no report

No report goes out that week when:

- nothing came in and nothing needs fixing,
- your subscription is paused, or set to cancel,
- your account has no Slack channel with us.

It waits, and skips the week if it still cannot go by Friday 5pm, while:

- someone on our team wrote in your channel in the last day,
- you have an open conversation with us about your tracking or your billing (we keep talking there),
- something on your account is with our engineers,
- we already sent you a same-day heads-up that day.

## FAQ

**Who sends it?** Your rep, from their own Slack account. If your rep cannot post in your channel, Cortana sends it.

**Why don't the numbers match my dashboard?** The report covers the 7 days to the date in its first line, in your business's time zone, and leaves out conversions named as tests. Your dashboard may be set to other dates.

**Does a person read my reply?** Cortana answers in the thread, and real people on our team watch every conversation and can jump in.

**Will I get reminders to answer it?** No. The report is never followed by a reminder.
