## What is Business Sharing?

Business Sharing lets you give **another Cortana account** access to one of your businesses. The classic use case: you run your own Cortana account, and your marketing agency runs theirs. Share the business with the agency's account and they can work inside it — dashboards, ad tracking, contacts, AI agents, Control Centre analytics — exactly as if it were one of their own.

You stay in control the whole time:

- **You keep ownership.** The business stays in your account, on your subscription.
- **You can revoke at any time.** Access ends immediately.
- **They can't touch your money.** Billing, subscriptions, and wallet purchases stay with your account only.

## Who gets access on the other side

A share targets an **account**, not a person. Once accepted:

- The other account's **owner** and their **Admin** users can access the shared business.
- Their **Manager** users cannot see it at all.
- They cannot re-share the business or invite anyone else into it.

## Requirements

- The invite must go to the **account owner's email** of the receiving account.
- The receiving account must be an **active, paid Cortana account**. No subscription, no share.

## How to share a business

1. Open the business you want to share from the dropdown at the top of the left-hand navigation bar.
2. Click **Settings**, then the **Users** tab.
3. Open the **Sharing** sub-tab.
4. Enter the **email address of the other account's owner**.
5. Tick the acknowledgement checkbox. This confirms you understand that every admin user of that account will be able to see and work inside your business, and that activity they run (including AI) is billed to this business.
6. Click **Send invite**.

The invite email goes out immediately and the share appears in your Sharing table with a **Pending** status. Invites expire after 7 days — you can resend from the same screen.

## How the other side accepts

1. The account owner opens the invite link from their email.
2. They log in to Cortana **as the account owner** — the email on their account must match the email you invited. Managers can't accept, and a mismatched email is rejected.
3. They review what accepting means and click **Accept**.

That's it. The business appears in their account right away.

## What the recipient sees

- **Business selector:** the shared business sits in its own **"Shared with you"** group, marked with a link icon and a "Shared by" tooltip.
- **Control Centre:** the business shows a **Shared** badge in the Businesses table, appears in the business selector, and flows through the **Unified Attribution** table with the same numbers you see. They can also add it to their business groups.
- Shared businesses never count against the recipient's own plan limits.

## What the recipient can and can't do

| Can do | Can't do |
| --- | --- |
| Use everything in the business — dashboards, tracking, contacts, conversations, AI agents | Delete the business |
| See it in their Control Centre and Unified Attribution | Manage your billing, subscriptions, or wallet |
| Use it through the API and MCP integrations | Re-share it or invite others |
| Leave the share on their own | Manage the share itself |

<Note>
Usage inside the business (AI runs, messages, and similar) is billed to the business it runs on — that's your account. This is stated in the consent acknowledgement when you share.
</Note>

## Revoking access

1. Go back to **Settings → Users → Sharing** on the business.
2. Click **Revoke** next to the share and confirm.

Access ends on the recipient's very next request — the app, the API, and any connected MCP tools all lose the business immediately.

The recipient can also walk away on their own from **Control Centre → Settings → Account → Shared with me → Leave**.

## Subscription rules

- If **your** subscription lapses, the recipient sees a "subscription inactive" wall on the shared business until your account is active again. Your data is never exposed through a lapsed account.
- If **their** account stops being active, they lose access to shared businesses until they're back.
