Business Sharing gives **another Cortana account** access to one of your businesses. No shared passwords, no extra seat. The business stays in your account on your subscription, and you can revoke any time.

It works one way: **the business owner sends the invite**. If you are an agency that needs into a client's business, the client shares it with you. There is no way to request access from your side.

## Share a business

1. Pick the business in the dropdown at the top of the left nav.
2. Go to **Settings → Users → Sharing**.
3. Enter the **email of the other account's owner**. Not a manager or teammate. The owner.
4. Tick the acknowledgement and click **Send Invite**.

The invite shows as **Pending** and expires after 7 days. The other owner opens the email, logs in **as owner with that same email**, and clicks **Accept**. The business appears in their account at once under **Shared with you**. Their account must be active and paid.

## Who gets in

A share goes to an **account**, not a person. Their **owner and Admin users** get full working access. Their **Manager** users see nothing.

| They can | They cannot |
| --- | --- |
| Use everything in the business, including the API and MCP | Delete the business |
| See it in their Control Centre and Unified Attribution | Touch your billing, subscription, or wallet |
| Leave the share themselves | Re-share it or invite anyone else |

Activity they run inside the business, including AI, is billed to the business, which means to you.

## Revoke or leave

- **You revoke:** **Settings → Users → Sharing**, then **Revoke**. Access ends on their next request, in the app, the API, and MCP.
- **They leave:** **Control Centre → Settings → Account → Shared with me → Leave**.

If either subscription lapses, access pauses until it is active again.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I get access to my client's business?">
    Ask the client to share it with you. In that business they go to **Settings → Users → Sharing** and enter **your account owner's email**. You accept from the invite email, logged in as owner. It then shows under **Shared with you**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I share my account with another Cortana user?">
    You share a **business**, not the account. Follow the steps above with the other account owner's email. If the person is on your own team and just needs a login, invite them under **Settings → Users** instead. See [Users](/platform/control-centre/users).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="They clicked the invite but were rejected.">
    They are logged in as a Manager, or their account email does not match the one you invited. They must log in as the owner with that exact email. If the invite is over 7 days old, resend it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I share with one person instead of a whole account?">
    No. Every Admin on the other account gets in. They control who is an Admin.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
