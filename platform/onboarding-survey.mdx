## Overview

Twice during onboarding, Cortana asks you how it is going. The question appears as a popup the next time you log in. Answering takes under a minute, and your answers go straight to the founders.

## When it appears

1. **A few hours after your onboarding call.** One question: **How was your onboarding experience?**
2. **When your four-week onboarding is complete.** Two questions: **How was your onboarding experience?** and **How was working with your account manager?**

The popup only appears on your main dashboard pages. It never appears on billing, checkout or setup pages, and never inside an embed on your own website.

## How to answer

1. Pick a score from **1** (Not great) to **5** (Loved it).
2. Click **Next**.
3. If you are asked about your account manager, pick a second score and click **Next**.
4. Add anything you would change in the text box. This is optional.
5. Click **Send feedback**.

The popup cannot be closed until you send an answer. Once sent, click **Back to the app**.

## What happens with your score

- **1 to 3:** a manager is alerted the same minute. You can expect someone to reach out. If it would help, you can ask for a different account manager.
- **4 or 5 at the end of onboarding:** you are offered a link to leave a review on Trustpilot. Clicking **Leave a review** opens Trustpilot in a new tab. You can skip it with **Maybe later**.

Your account manager never sees your individual score. The founders do.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why did I get a Slack message about the survey?">
    If the popup was shown but not answered within 48 hours, Cortana posts one message in your Slack channel with a link to the same survey. That is the only reminder. Answering in either place closes it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I answered in Slack and the popup still appeared">
    Both places record the same survey, so answering once is enough. If you see the popup after answering, it closes as soon as you send, and a second answer is not stored.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who sees my answers?">
    The founders see every score and comment. Managers see low scores so they can help. Account managers see how many customers answered, never a score or a comment.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

**Why can't I close this popup?**
It stays until you send a score. One tap and Send is enough; the text box is optional.

**I gave a 5, why was I asked for a review?**
At the end of onboarding, a high score on both questions offers a Trustpilot link. It is optional.

**Can I change my answer?**
No. Each survey is answered once. Tell your account manager or reply in your Slack channel if something changed.
