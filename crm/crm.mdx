The CRM gives every business a deal board that reads like your CRM and knows what your ads did. Every deal carries the ad, campaign or channel that created the contact, so the board is the one place where pipeline and attribution meet. By the end of this section you will have your pipelines imported, your GoHighLevel team invited and matched, your custom fields on every deal, and two-way sync running.

## Open the CRM

1. In the sidebar, click **CRM / Pipelines**.
2. The first pipeline opens. To switch, click the pipeline name at the top left and pick another one. Grab the grip on the left of a pipeline and drag it up or down to arrange the list.
3. To start a fresh one, open the same menu and click **New pipeline**. Give it a name and click **Create pipeline**. It starts with sensible stages you can rename anytime.

The number next to the pipeline name is the count and total value of the deals you can see right now. Filters change it.

## Import your deals

The import brings pipelines, stages and deals across as native Cortana pipelines, with the dates your CRM carried, so age and time in stage are real from day one.

### 1. Open the import

Click **Import** at the top right of the board.

<Frame caption="The Import button, top right of the board.">
  <img src="/images/crm/02-import-button.png" alt="The CRM board with a numbered highlight on the Import button at the top right" />
</Frame>

### 2. Choose where your deals live

Pick **GoHighLevel**, **HubSpot**, **Close** or **A CSV file**. A CRM that is not connected yet says **Connect it in Integrations first**.

<Frame caption="Where do your deals live today: a connected CRM, one to connect first, or a CSV file.">
  <img src="/images/crm/03-import-source.png" alt="The Import pipelines dialog on its Source step: GoHighLevel connected, HubSpot and Close saying Connect it in Integrations first, A CSV file with an Upload button, and a Continuous sync switch" />
</Frame>

### 3. Import from a CRM

1. Turn on **Continuous sync** for the CRM if you want changes to keep flowing both ways after the import. You can change this later in CRM settings.
2. Tick the pipelines you want and click **Import**.
3. Watch the tally: **Imported**, **Already here**, **Needs contact** and **Failed**. Click **Stop after this page** if you need to pause. Everything that already landed stays, and re-running skips it.
4. The **Done** step states the outcome in numbers and lists anything that needs a hand. Click **View the board**.

<Frame caption="Tick the pipelines to bring across. Ones already here re-run without duplicating.">
  <img src="/images/crm/04-import-pipelines.png" alt="The Pipelines step listing each GoHighLevel pipeline with its stage count, two ticked, and an Import 2 pipelines button" />
</Frame>

### 4. From GoHighLevel: match your users

Deals arrive owned by GoHighLevel users. Until a GoHighLevel user is matched to a Cortana teammate, their deals show that person's name with a **GHL** badge on the board, and the **Done** step counts them.

1. Open CRM settings, click **Sync**, then **Match users**, then **Manage users** next to the location.
2. Users whose email already matches a Cortana account are matched for you.
3. For the rest, pick a teammate from the dropdown, or click **Invite** and **Send invite**. Cortana creates their seat, emails their login, and hands them every deal and booking that user owns.

From then on, an owner change in either system lands in the other. The full walkthrough is under **Sync and your team** below.

### 5. Import from a CSV file

Any spreadsheet export works: one row per deal, one column per property.

1. Click **Import**, then **A CSV file**, and upload the file. The steps are **Upload**, **Map**, **Preview**, **Import** and **Done**.
2. On **Map**, every column gets a target: the contact's email, phone, name or company; the deal's name, value, cash collected, stage, owner, status, created date or source; one of your custom fields (or a new one you name right there); or **Skip this column**. Cortana guesses the usual headers for you.
3. For **Stage**, **Owner** and **Status** columns, the values found in the file are listed so you can point each one at a stage, a teammate or **open**, **won**, **lost** or **abandoned**.
4. Leave **Create contacts that are not here yet** on if the file holds people Cortana has not met.
5. **Preview** shows the first rows as they will land. Click **Import** and watch the tally; **Stop after this page** pauses it.

Rows are matched by email, phone, deal name and created day, so importing the same file twice never duplicates a deal.

<Frame caption="The CSV wizard on Map: every column gets a target, and a Stage column lists its values to point at your stages.">
  <img src="/images/crm/05-csv-map.png" alt="The CSV import wizard on the Map step, each column with a target dropdown and a Stage column showing its values mapped to stages" />

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Deal age and dates after an import">
    Imported deals keep the dates your CRM carried, so their age and time in stage are real. When a CRM does not carry a full date history, the age starts from the nearest date it did carry, or from the import itself, and the Done step tells you how many deals that affected. Those approximate births never count as conversions in your attribution.

The first 500 field definitions come over per sync; run Sync fields now again for more. Deals whose contact is not in Cortana yet are counted under Needs contact and picked up by re-running the import once the contact syncs in. A CSV import handles up to 200 columns and imports in pages of 250 rows.
  </Accordion>

## Work the board

The board is one column per stage. Everything a rep does all day happens here without opening a deal.

- **Move a deal**: drag its card to another stage. While you drag, **Won**, **Lost** and **Abandoned** drop zones appear at the bottom. Dropping on Lost or Abandoned asks for a reason.
- **Read a card**: the name and who the deal is with, the value with its billing period and any cash collected, one line for where the contact came from, then an action row with the conversation, notes, tasks and appointments counts. Click an icon to open the deal on that section.
- **Assign an owner**: click the owner chip at the bottom of a card and pick a teammate. A deal synced from GoHighLevel whose owner is not matched to a teammate yet shows that person's name with a **GHL** badge instead of Unassigned; see **Invite and match your GoHighLevel team** below. On a synced deal a teammate GoHighLevel does not know is greyed out with the note **not matched to a GoHighLevel user yet**.
- **Read the age**: every card shows how long the deal has been open. An orange dot means it has gone stale.
- **Search and filter**: type in **Search deals**, or click **Filters**. The sheet narrows by **Owner** (**Me**, a teammate or **Unassigned**), **Attention** (**Deals I follow**), status, **Value**, **Billing** (**One-time**, **Monthly**, **Annual**), **Source**, the campaign or ad that acquired the contact, **Created** dates, and the value of any custom field. Click **Saved views** to keep a filter set for the whole team, or pick one of the automatic views: your deals, each teammate's deals, or unassigned deals.
- **Change the view**: the view menu next to Filters switches between **Board**, **List** and **By source**, which rolls your deals up by the ad source or campaign that created them.

<Frame caption="Filters: status, owner, deals you follow, value, billing, source, created dates, your fields, and the ad that acquired the contact.">
  <img src="/images/crm/06-filters.png" alt="The Filters sheet with Status, Owner, Deals I follow, Value, Billing, Source, Created, Fields, Attention and Attribution sections" />
</Frame>

### Work several deals at once

1. Hover a card and tick the circle in its corner, or tick the circle at the top of a column to select every deal in it.
2. The bar at the bottom says how many deals are selected. Click **Move to** and pick a stage; **Mark as** and pick **Won**, **Lost** or **Abandoned** (one reason for all of them); **Set a field** to write one value on every selected deal; or **Delete**.
3. Drag any selected card and the whole selection moves as one stack.

A deleted deal that is synced from your CRM comes back on the next check; delete it in the CRM to keep it gone.

## Open a deal

Click a card. The drawer slides in from the right with three columns:

- **Left**: every property, editable in place.
- **Middle**: the conversation with the contact, live.
- **Right**: a rail with **Deal activity**, **Contact**, **Notes**, **Tasks**, **Appointments** and **Recordings**.

On a narrow screen the same three become the **Details**, **Chat** and **Activity** tabs.

### Properties

Every field saves as soon as you leave it.

- **Deal**: name, value with its billing period (**One-time**, **Monthly**, **Annual**), **Cash collected**, source.
- **Pipeline**: stage, and status **open**, **won**, **lost** or **abandoned**. Lost or abandoned asks for a reason.
- **People**: the **Deal owner** and the followers. Hover the followers to see who they are.
- **Opportunity custom fields**: your fields, grouped by folder. **Manage fields** opens CRM settings.

### The rail

- **Deal activity**: the timeline. Stage moves, status changes, conversions, calls.
- **Contact**: their details, custom fields and customer journey, the same view as the Conversations page.
- **Notes**: write in **Write a note** and press Cmd+Enter (Ctrl+Enter on Windows) or click **Add note**. Notes stay on the deal, newest first.
- **Tasks**: type in **Add a task** and press Enter. Tick a task when it is done; the open count shows on the card.
- **Appointments**: every call with this contact, past and next. Click one to edit it (below).
- **Recordings**: the contact's recorded calls, each with its outcome and AI summary. Play one right there, or open it in Fathom.

### Reschedule or update an appointment

You do not have to leave the board to move a call.

1. Click the appointments icon on a card, or open **Appointments** in the drawer, then click the appointment.
2. Click the pencil next to the time. Set **Date and time** and **Duration**, then click **Done**.
3. Change the status at the bottom: **Booked**, **Rescheduled**, **Completed**, **Showed**, **No-show** or **Cancelled**.
4. Edit the title, the location or meeting link, the notes or what the meeting is about. Everything saves automatically.

## CRM settings

Click the settings button at the top right of the board (or open the pipeline menu and click **CRM settings**). The tabs are **Board**, **Pipelines**, **Fields**, **Lost reasons** and **Sync**. Fields, lost reasons and sync are shared by every pipeline.

### Board

What every card shows: switch **Value**, **Source** and **Acquired from** on or off, and choose whether the age counter shows **Days old**, **Days in stage** or is **Hidden**.

### Pipelines

The order your pipelines appear in the switcher. Drag a pipeline to arrange the list, click a name to rename it. New pipelines are created from the switcher.

<Frame caption="CRM settings, Fields tab: every field with its type, its folder and the CRM it is kept in sync with.">
  <img src="/images/crm/10-settings-fields.png" alt="CRM settings on the Fields tab: the tabs Board, Pipelines, Fields, Lost reasons and Sync, a list of fields each with a type pill and a GoHighLevel sync pill, and an Add a field button" />
</Frame>

### Fields

Fields are the questions your team answers on every deal. Imported fields arrive with their type, their folder and a **GoHighLevel** pill when they are kept in sync.

1. Click **Add a field**.
2. Answer **What's the field called?**
3. Answer **What kind of answer?**: **Text**, **Pick one**, **Pick many**, **Number**, **Date**, **Email**, **Phone** or **Link**.
4. For Pick one and Pick many, type each choice and press Enter.
5. Choose the folder, then click **Add field**.

Click a field name to rename it. Click **choices** under a pick field to add or remove options. The three dots on a row move the field to another folder or archive it. Archiving hides the field from every deal card; values already saved stay on their deals.

### Lost reasons

When a deal is marked lost, the rep picks one of these. Type a reason in **Add a reason** and click **Add**. Drag to reorder, click a reason to rename it, and archive the ones you no longer use. Keep the list short enough to answer honestly.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Field types and how they map">
    Text, Number, Date, Email, Phone, Link, Pick one and Pick many map to the matching GoHighLevel field types. Renaming a field or editing its choices in Cortana updates GoHighLevel, and vice versa. Folders are managed in GoHighLevel while sync is on. A value that arrives for a field Cortana does not have yet counts as waiting for a field on the Sync tab; click Sync fields now, and it lands on the next check. A value in a shape Cortana cannot read counts as could not be read and is left untouched in your CRM.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Sync and your team

### The Sync tab

One row per CRM. The switch turns two-way sync on or off for that CRM; while it is off, nothing moves in either direction. Under the status line, three gauges show the last check, when fields were last synced and when users were last matched. The line under them says how often the full check comes round for your account.

<Frame caption="CRM settings, Sync tab: the switch, the status line and the three gauges for a connected GoHighLevel account.">
  <img src="/images/crm/11-settings-sync.png" alt="CRM settings on the Sync tab: a GoHighLevel row with the two-way sync switch on, a status line, gauges for Last check, Fields and Users with a Match users link, the cadence line, and a Sync fields now button" />
</Frame>

Click **Sync fields now** to pull field definitions from your CRM immediately. Click **Match users** under the Users gauge to invite and match your GoHighLevel team without leaving the board.

### Invite and match your GoHighLevel team

Each GoHighLevel connection is one location, and every location has its own list of users. A deal or booking owned by a GoHighLevel user becomes a teammate's the moment that user is matched to a Cortana account. Three doors open the same list:

- Right after you paste a private integration token, the **Invite your team** step shows that location's users.
- On the Integrations page, the three dots on a GoHighLevel subaccount, then **Manage users**.
- On the board, CRM settings, **Sync**, **Match users**, then **Manage users** next to the location.

For each user:

1. If they already have a Cortana account with the same email, they are matched automatically.
2. Otherwise pick a teammate from the dropdown, or click **Invite**. The email is filled in from GoHighLevel and you can change it. Click **Send invite**. Cortana creates their seat, emails them their login, matches them to the GoHighLevel user, and hands them every deal and booking that user owns.
3. **Default GoHighLevel owner** is where bookings for an unmatched user land, so nothing is ever ownerless. It is set per location.

  <img src="/images/crm/12-users-dialog.png" alt="The Users on a location dialog with the default GoHighLevel owner, a Team members list showing matched teammates and Invite buttons, and one row with its invite email field and Send invite button open" />

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What syncs with GoHighLevel, and how fast">
    With two-way sync on, deals, stages, status, value, name, owner and custom field values flow both ways. Stage and status changes made in GoHighLevel arrive in seconds. Owner, value and field edits made there are picked up by the next full check; the Sync tab says how often that check comes round for your account, and the Last check gauge shows when it last ran. Changes you make in Cortana are pushed within about a minute. If GoHighLevel refuses a push, the row keeps retrying every 15 minutes until it goes through.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="HubSpot and Close">
    Both import pipelines, stages and deals, and both keep deals in sync. Neither carries a deal owner, so owner changes on those deals stay in Cortana and the Unassign option remains available.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How owners are matched to your teammates">
    A GoHighLevel user is matched to a Cortana teammate per location: automatically when the emails match, by picking a teammate, or by inviting them. Until then a deal that user owns carries their name with a GHL badge on the board and says who owns it in GoHighLevel in the drawer, and the Users gauge on the Sync tab counts those deals. Matching hands the teammate every deal and booking that user owns, right away. Reassigning a synced deal in Cortana updates GoHighLevel; to unassign a synced deal, do it in GoHighLevel for now.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can invite">
    Inviting creates a Cortana seat, so it follows the same rule as adding a user under Team: the account owner, an admin, or a manager the owner allowed to manage users on that business, with an active plan. The invited person joins as a manager of that business.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What the Sync tab numbers mean">
    Received and sent are the last time a change came in and went out. Waiting to push is how many deals have edits queued for your CRM. Retrying every 15 minutes means your CRM rejected those pushes and Cortana keeps trying. The last check failed means the periodic check could not reach your CRM: reconnect it under Integrations. The line under the gauges is how often the full check reaches your account; it depends on how many accounts share the sweep.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I re-run the import without duplicating deals?">
    Yes. Deals already here are skipped and counted under Already here. Re-running is the way to pick up deals that were waiting for a contact. The same is true for a CSV file.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My imported deals show a name with a GHL badge instead of a teammate.">
    That is the deal's owner in GoHighLevel, not matched to a Cortana teammate yet. Step 4 above fixes it: match or invite them, and their deals become theirs here right away.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is a teammate greyed out when I try to assign a deal?">
    The deal is synced with GoHighLevel and that teammate is not matched to a GoHighLevel user, so the assignment could not be pushed. Match or invite them first: CRM settings, Sync, Match users.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I deleted deals from the board and they came back.">
    They are synced from your CRM, and the next check brought them back. Delete them in the CRM as well, or turn sync off for that CRM first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I added a field here. Does it show up in GoHighLevel?">
    Yes. With sync on, a new field is created in GoHighLevel with the same name, type and choices, and it shows a GoHighLevel pill in CRM settings once it is there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does the Sync tab say values are waiting for a field?">
    Your CRM sent values for a field Cortana has not created yet. Click Sync fields now to bring the field definitions over; the values land on the next check.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I changed a deal in GoHighLevel and it still shows the old value here.">
    Stage and status changes arrive in seconds, but owner, value and field edits wait for the next full check. Open CRM settings, Sync tab, and read the Last check gauge and the line under it. If it says the last check failed, reconnect GoHighLevel under Integrations.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I invited someone and they say they got no email.">
    The seat and the match are made even when the email does not go out, and the toast tells you when that happened. Resend their login from Team.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I turn sync off and edit deals?">
    Nothing moves in either direction while sync is off. When you turn it back on, the next check reads your CRM as the source of truth for the deals it holds, so make edits in the CRM while paused.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
