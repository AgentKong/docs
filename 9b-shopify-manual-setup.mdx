<Warning>
  **Legacy path.** New stores should [install the Cortana app from the Shopify App Store](/9-how-to-set-up-shopify): the pixel, the webhooks and the conversions are set up for you. The manual setup below keeps working for stores that already use it and is never switched off on you. Hand-made webhooks were created on Shopify API version **2025-10**, which Shopify retires on **16 October 2026**; move to the app before then.
</Warning>

## Move to the app (recommended)

You can install the app while your manual setup keeps running. Nothing double-counts: an order that reaches Cortana on both your hand-made webhook and the app is booked once.

1. Install the **Cortana** app from the Shopify App Store. On the **What do you want to track?** screen, your existing Purchase conversion is kept and switched on; nothing is created twice.
2. Wait for the chips under **Your store** to read **Pixel installed**, **Webhooks live** and **First event received**.
3. Open the **Tidy up your old setup** card at the bottom of the screen. It lists your hand-made webhooks (by the name Shopify's admin shows) and your custom pixel, each with an **Open** link into the right Shopify settings page.
4. Delete them at your own pace: **Settings** → **Notifications** → **Webhooks** for the webhooks, **Settings** → **Customer events** for the custom pixel. Once the app has delivered every one of your old topics, the card says so.

<Note>
  One case is not optional: a custom pixel pasted **before 17 July 2026** is an older build the app cannot tell apart from its own, so product views count twice until it is deleted. The card says **Delete the custom pixel "Cortana"** when this applies to you.
</Note>

Shopify has a specific manual process to track every visit and purchase: the theme snippet, the custom pixel, then one webhook per conversion.

### Step 1: Connect your store

First, head over to the **Integrations** tab and find **Shopify**.

1. Click **Connect**.
2. Enter your **Shopify Store URL** (e.g., admin.shopify.com/store/your-store-name).
3. Click **Continue**.

### Step 2: Install the header snippet

The system generates a script snippet. Add it to your theme's code:

1. Copy the snippet provided in the dashboard.
2. Click **Theme Editor** (or navigate to **Online Store \> Themes \> Edit Code** in Shopify).
3. Open the theme.liquid file.
4. Search (Cmd/Ctrl \+ F) for `</head>`.
5. Paste your snippet on a new line just **before** the `</head>` tag.
6. Hit **Save**, then return to our dashboard and click **I pasted the snippet**.

### Step 3: Set up the custom pixel

Next, use Shopify's "Customer Events" to track cart additions and checkouts:

1. Copy the **Pixel Code** provided in the second step.
2. Click the **Events** button in the dashboard to jump to the right spot in Shopify.
3. Click **Add custom pixel**, give it a name (e.g., "Cortana"), and click **Add pixel**.
4. Paste the code into the editor.
5. **Customer Privacy Settings:**
   - **Permission:** Select **Not required**.
   - **Data sale:** Select **Data collected does not qualify as data sale**.
6. Click **Save**, then click **Connect** at the top.
7. Return to the dashboard and click **Verify your site**. This opens your store in a new tab to confirm the pixel is firing.

### Step 4: Map your conversions (webhooks)

Now that the tracking is live, map specific events like purchases back to the system.

1. Go to **Conversion Setup** and select the event you want to track (e.g., **Purchase (Paid)**).
2. Click **Add Source**, select **Shopify**, and choose the corresponding **Shopify Event**.
3. Copy the **Webhook URL** provided.
4. Click **Open Shopify Webhooks** (or go to **Shopify Settings \> Notifications \> Webhooks**).
5. Click **Create Webhook**:
   - **Event:** Select the matching event (e.g., "Order payment").
   - **Format:** JSON.
   - **URL:** Paste the Webhook URL you copied.
   - **API Version:** the version the dialog tells you to pick.
6. Click **Save**.

<Tip>
  **Verify your webhook:** After saving, click the three dots next to your new webhook in Shopify and hit **Send test notification**. You should see a "Received" status with a green checkmark in our dashboard.
</Tip>

### Standard Shopify events

Most stores map these core events:

- **View Product:** a customer views a product page.
- **Add to Cart:** an item is added to the shopping bag.
- **Initiate Checkout:** the customer reaches the first stage of the checkout.
- **Purchase (Paid):** the big one. It triggers once the payment is successfully processed, and refunds are booked against it.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How a manual store and the app coexist">
    - Each conversion books on one source per event. When both a hand-made webhook and the app deliver the same order, the older source books it and the newer delivery is recorded as a duplicate.
    - Page views come from one sender only: the theme snippet while it is installed, the app pixel once it is.
    - Commerce events (checkouts, adds to cart, product views) are de-duplicated on Shopify's own ids for 90 seconds, so the pasted pixel and the app pixel never book the same event twice, except for product views from a pixel pasted before 17 July 2026 (see the note above).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="API version retirement">
    Hand-made webhooks were created on Shopify API version 2025-10. Shopify retires it on 16 October 2026; after that date the payload shape may change without notice. Cortana watches for a changed version on every delivery and the team reaches out to stores still on the manual path before then.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
