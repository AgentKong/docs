## Overview

A conversion is something Cortana counts and can send to your ads, like a sign-up. Webinars give you seven of them, from signing up to seeing your pitch. WebinarJam webinars add three more: two for the replay, and one for buying in the room.

Nothing is counted on its own. You choose, per webinar, or once for all your webinars.

This page shows both ways, how to add a show rate to your reports, and what goes to your ad platforms.

<Note>
  **Before you start.** Make a webinar first: see [2) Create Your Webinar](/webinars-create).

  To send results to your ads, connect your ad platforms. See [Connect integrations](/1-connect-integrations).
</Note>

## The seven webinar events

Each event can count into a conversion. The panel shows the short name on the left. The conversion Cortana makes for it has the full name.

| In the panel | Conversion name | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Registered** | Webinar Registered | *Someone registered for a webinar through its form.* |
| **Accepted the calendar invite** | Webinar Invite Accepted | *A registrant accepted the calendar invite.* |
| **Attended** | Webinar Attended | *A registrant joined the live event.* |
| **Watched 25%** | Webinar Watched 25% | *Watched at least a quarter of the live event.* |
| **Watched 50%** | Webinar Watched 50% | *Watched at least half of the live event.* |
| **Watched 75%** | Webinar Watched 75% | *Watched at least three quarters of the live event.* |
| **Saw the pitch** | Webinar Saw The Pitch | *Was still in the room at the pitch minute.* |

### Three more on WebinarJam

A WebinarJam webinar also offers these three. A Zoom webinar does not.

| In the panel | Conversion name | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Watched the replay** | Webinar Replay Watched | *Watched the replay.* |
| **Watched 50% of the replay** | Webinar Replay Watched 50% | *Watched at least half of the replay.* |
| **Bought in the room** | Webinar In-Room Purchase | *Bought from the webinar room, live or on the replay.* |

Cortana checks WebinarJam for these every 6 hours for 7 days after each session, or within minutes with WebinarJam's webhook. **Webinar In-Room Purchase** has no dollar value: your checkout stays the record of the money. See [Use WebinarJam](/webinars-webinarjam).

Missing a webinar is never a conversion. No-shows only show on the **Registrants** tab.

## 1. Choose conversions for one webinar

You can do this on the **Done** step of **New webinar**, under **Which conversions count**. Or at any time on the webinar's **Settings** tab:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Settings tab">
    Open the webinar, then click the **Settings** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Conversions">
    Scroll to **Conversions**. It says *Which conversion each of these counts into.*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an event's dropdown">
    Each event has a dropdown on the right. Click the one for **Registered**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a conversion">
    Pick **Create “Webinar Registered”**, one of your own conversions, or **Do not track**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do the same for each event">
    Pick a conversion, or **Do not track**, for each event: seven on a Zoom webinar, ten on a WebinarJam webinar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save conversions**. Nothing counts until you save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Every choice in the dropdown">
    - **Create “Webinar Registered”** (and so on): shows until Cortana has made that conversion. Saving makes it.
    - **Webinar Registered** (and so on), on its own: Cortana's conversion, once it exists. Your next webinar uses it too, so you never get two.
    - **Your conversions**: any other conversion in your business.
    - **Counted for every webinar**: only shows when your business already counts that event for every webinar.
    - **Do not track**: nothing is counted for that event on this webinar.

    A conversion marked *(a Cortana Webinars source already counts this)* is greyed out. That stops one event counting twice. See section 2.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is picked for you">
    Until Cortana has made a webinar conversion, every event starts on **Create**. Once it has, a new webinar starts on Cortana's existing conversions.

    Once a webinar has saved, an event you left alone starts on **Do not track**.

    The line above **Save conversions** tells you where you stand. It reads *Nothing is tracked for this webinar until you save.*, then a count of changes not saved yet, then **Saved.**
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The note about Meta">
    With Meta connected, you may see *A conversion created here is sent to Meta as well.* It shows while an event is on **Create**.

    It means Cortana sets up the Meta event for the conversion it makes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Notes that can show under an event">
    - *Paused in Settings > Conversions, so nothing counts until it is on again.* The conversion is switched off.
    - *Its source on that conversion was removed. Save to connect it again.*
    - *Its conversion was deleted. Pick another, or leave it untracked.*
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## 2. Count every webinar at once with Cortana Webinars

The panel above works one webinar at a time. A **Cortana Webinars** source counts one event for every webinar and meeting in your business.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Ad Tracking">
    Click **Ad Tracking** in the menu on the left.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Conversion Setup">
    Click the **Conversion Setup** tab. **Conversion Events** opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a conversion">
    Click the name of the conversion you want to count into. **Edit Conversion** opens. No conversion yet? Click **Add Custom Event** to make one first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Source">
    Under **Sources**, click **Add Source**. **Create Conversion Source** opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick Cortana Webinars">
    Under **Integration**, pick **Cortana Webinars**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the event">
    Under **What counts as a conversion?**, pick one event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick which ones count">
    Under **Which webinars and meetings count?**, pick an answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Submit">
    Click **Submit**. The message *Cortana Webinars source created* confirms it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The helper says *Each source tracks one event. Registrations come from the form that registers people for the webinar.*

| Choice | What counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Webinars and meetings** | Every webinar, on Zoom or WebinarJam, and every Zoom meeting. This is the default. |
| **Webinars only** | Every webinar, on Zoom or WebinarJam. No meetings. |
| **Zoom meetings only** | Only Zoom meetings. |

The three WebinarJam events (replay and in-room purchase) are not offered here. A source counts across all your webinars, and most cannot produce them. Pick them per webinar instead, as section 1 shows.

To count a second event, add a second source. You can change **Which webinars and meetings count?** later, but not the event.

## 3. Add Webinar show rate to your Ad Tracking table

The **Webinar show rate** metric is **Attended** divided by **Registered**. Your business has one at most.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the metric">
    In the **Conversions** panel, tick *Also create a “Webinar show rate” metric (Attended / Registered)*. Then click **Save conversions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Ad Tracking">
    Click **Ad Tracking**. The **Attribution Table** tab opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your preset's menu">
    Click the button with your column preset's name, above the table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the preset">
    Click the pencil next to a preset. **Customize Columns** opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Custom Metrics">
    Click **Custom Metrics**. **Webinar show rate** is in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it">
    Press **+** next to it, or drag it into place.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** at the bottom of the box.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/webinars/conversions/25-show-rate.png" alt="The Customize Columns box. The Custom Metrics tab is marked 5, Webinar show rate with Format: percent is marked 6, and the Save button is marked 7." />

## 4. Send webinar conversions to your ad platforms

A webinar conversion goes to Meta, Google Ads and TikTok, like any other conversion. Each platform must be connected on the **AI Ad Optimization** tab first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Ad Optimization">
    Click **Ad Tracking** in the menu on the left. Then click the **AI Ad Optimization** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the platform">
    If a platform is not connected yet, connect it there. [AI Ad Optimization](/6-ai-ad-optimization) walks you through each one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open its event mapping">
    On the platform's card, click **Manage**. You see your conversions in a list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on your webinar conversions">
    Find your webinar conversions, such as **Webinar Registered**. Turn on each one you want to send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick what it counts as">
    For Meta, pick the event type. For Google Ads, pick the conversion action. For TikTok, pick the event.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* TODO: verify live: these mapping steps follow the AI Ad Optimization page and the source code. They were not walked through with a webinar conversion. */}

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Meta: Cortana sets it up for you">
    When Cortana makes a webinar conversion and Meta is connected, it also adds it to your Meta mapping. You see it turned on under **Manage**.

    It goes to Meta as a custom event named after the conversion, for example `WebinarRegistered` or `WebinarAttended`. Meta only lets your ads use a custom event once you confirm it. See **Step 4: Creating & Confirming Custom Events** under Meta CAPI on [AI Ad Optimization](/6-ai-ad-optimization).

    A conversion you picked from **Your conversions** keeps its own Meta mapping. Cortana does not change it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Google Ads and TikTok: you map it">
    Cortana does not map webinar conversions to Google Ads or TikTok for you.

    For Google Ads, the conversion action must exist in Google Ads first. [AI Ad Optimization](/6-ai-ad-optimization) shows where to make it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

LinkedIn and Microsoft Ads do not get webinar conversions.

## Settings Reference

| Setting | What it does | Default | We recommend | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Event dropdown (one per event) | Picks the conversion an event counts into. | **Create** at first; later Cortana's conversion | Count **Registered** and **Attended** at least. | Nothing counts until you save. |
| **Do not track** | Counts nothing for that event on this webinar. | After a first save, for events you left alone | Use it for events you do not care about. | Hidden when every webinar is already counted. |
| *Also create a “Webinar show rate” metric* | Makes the **Webinar show rate** metric. | Ticked on the **Done** step's first save | Keep it ticked once. | One per business. |
| **Save conversions** | Saves the choices for this webinar. | Greyed out until something changed | Save before you leave. | The **Settings** tab's **Save changes** does not save these. |
| **Integration**: **Cortana Webinars** | Adds a source that counts every webinar. | **Webhook** | Use it to count all webinars the same way. | One event per source. |
| **What counts as a conversion?** | The one event the source counts. | Nothing picked | One source per event. | Cannot be changed later. |
| **Which webinars and meetings count?** | Webinars, meetings, or both. | **Webinars and meetings** | Keep the default. | Can be changed later. |
| **Custom Metrics** (Customize Columns) | Lists your custom metrics, including **Webinar show rate**. | Not applicable | Add it to your main preset. | Click **Save** in the box. |
| **Webinars** (Add a webhook endpoint) | Sends webinar events to your endpoint. | Off | Turn it on for your automation tools. | Events include the personal join link. Keep the endpoint private. |

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My webinar conversions stay at zero">
    Check the webinar's **Settings** tab, **Conversions**. If the line says *Nothing is tracked for this webinar until you save.*, pick conversions and click **Save conversions**.

    Also check the people signed up through the webinar's own link. See [3) How People Sign Up](/webinars-registration).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A conversion in the list is greyed out">
    It reads *(a Cortana Webinars source already counts this)*. A source already counts that event into it for every webinar. Pick another, or leave the event on **Do not track**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says a Cortana Webinars source already counts this event">
    The full message is *A Cortana Webinars source already counts this event into that conversion. Pick another, or remove that source in Settings > Conversions.* The source lives under **Ad Tracking**, **Conversion Setup**, on that conversion.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says every name for the conversion is taken">
    The message starts *Every name for the "Webinar Registered" conversion is taken by another conversion.* Rename one of your own conversions, then save again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says I do not have permission">
    The message is *You do not have permission to manage webinars.* Your role cannot change conversions. Ask an owner.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Cortana count webinar conversions on its own?">
    No. Nothing is counted until you choose a conversion for an event and save.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I set this up again for every webinar?">
    For the panel, yes, but it is quick. A new webinar starts on the conversions Cortana already made. Or use a **Cortana Webinars** source to count every webinar at once.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will my next webinar make duplicate conversions?">
    No. Cortana makes each webinar conversion once. Later webinars count into the same one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I count no-shows as a conversion?">
    No. A no-show is never a conversion and is never sent to your ads. You see no-shows on the **Registrants** tab.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is Settings > Conversions mentioned when I cannot find it?">
    Some messages say *Settings > Conversions*. Your conversions live under **Ad Tracking**, then **Conversion Setup**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
