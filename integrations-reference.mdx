This page lists every integration Cortana supports and what each one does. To connect any of them, head to **Settings → Integrations** — the step-by-step walkthrough is in [Connect Integrations](/1-connect-integrations).

<Note>
Don't see your tool? Any platform that can send a webhook (natively or via Zapier, Make, or n8n) can still feed conversions into Cortana — see [Conversion Setup](/4-conversion-setup).
</Note>

## CRMs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="GoHighLevel">
    Two-way CRM sync: contacts, tags, pipelines, and calendars. Send and receive messages through GHL channels and track conversions with full attribution. Supports multiple sub-accounts, each connected separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="HubSpot">
    Two-way contact sync with attribution for the events that matter: meetings booked, deal stage changes, deal values, form submissions, and custom property changes can all become conversions. Custom fields sync both ways.

    **Household associations** — when two HubSpot contacts are associated with each other (for example a parent booking on behalf of a student), Cortana automatically merges them into one contact: sessions, conversions, and the whole timeline unify, and a booking made by the untracked person inherits the tracked person's ad attribution — retroactively too. The customer journey shows a "Household merged" entry explaining exactly what happened, and the Merge log keeps the undo. On by default; turn it off per business with the **Household associations** toggle on the HubSpot integration card.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Close.io">
    Real-time contact sync (secondary emails and phones included), with opportunity wins, meetings, and calls tracked as conversions with full attribution.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Salesforce">
    Contact sync plus opportunity stage changes tracked and attributed as conversions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
