## Overview

The **Webinars** page lists every webinar in your business. **New webinar** first asks **Where does it run?** For Zoom, it then asks four short questions: **Basics**, **Host**, **Gate** and **Done**.

Cortana then makes the Zoom webinar or meeting for you. It also turns on Zoom sign-ups, so each person gets their own join link.

In Cortana, a webinar can be a Zoom webinar or a Zoom meeting. Both work the same way here.

<Note>
  **Before you start.** Connect Zoom first: see [1) Connect Zoom](/webinars-connect-zoom).

  Have a form ready for people to sign up with. It must ask for an email, and it must be turned on. See [Create your form](/forms-create) and [Share your form](/forms-share).
</Note>

<Tip>
  **Hosting on WebinarJam?** Follow [Use WebinarJam](/webinars-webinarjam) instead. It walks the WebinarJam side of **New webinar**.
</Tip>

  - **The name.** Click it to open the webinar.
  - **The switch.** On means the webinar takes sign-ups. See [6) Manage Your Webinars](/webinars-manage).
  - **The type line.** The host, then how people register. For example *Zoom meeting · Register once, every event*, or *WebinarJam webinar · Register for each event*.
  - **The next event.** For example *Next: Oct 21, 2026, 10:00 AM*. It reads **No upcoming event** when nothing is left.

  - **Edit**, a link icon that opens the share box, and a three-dot menu.

  The **Search webinars** box finds a webinar by name.
</Accordion>

## 2. Basics: name, day and time

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Type a name in **What is the webinar called?** People see this name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the day">
    Pick a date in **Which day?** It starts on a week from today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the time">
    Pick a start time in **What time?** It starts on 10:00.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick how long it runs">
    Pick a length in **How long?** It starts on 60 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the timezone">
    Check **Which timezone?** It starts on your business's timezone. Type a city to find another.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say if it repeats">
    Leave **Does it repeat?** off for a single event. Turn it on for a series.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Next">
    Click **Next** to go to **Host**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Basics: the name, the day, the time, the length and the timezone.">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/webinars/create/08-new-basics.png" alt="The New webinar box on its Basics step, with What is the webinar called marked 1, Which day marked 2, What time marked 3, How long marked 4, Which timezone marked 5, Does it repeat marked 6, and the Next button marked 7." />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/webinars/create/08-new-basics-dark.png" alt="The New webinar box on its Basics step, with What is the webinar called marked 1, Which day marked 2, What time marked 3, How long marked 4, Which timezone marked 5, Does it repeat marked 6, and the Next button marked 7." />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  **Pick the day, the time and the repeat rule with care.**

  You cannot change them in Cortana once the webinar is made. You change them in Zoom, and Cortana follows.
</Warning>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Lengths you can pick">
    **How long?** offers 30, 45, 60, 90, 120 and 180 minutes. Pick **Custom length** to type your own, from 5 to 1440 minutes.

    Lengths your business used before also show, under **Yours**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Set up a webinar that repeats">
    Turn on **Does it repeat?** The helper says *Each event in the series is tracked individually.* More boxes appear:

    - **Repeats**: **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly**. It starts on **Weekly**.
    - **Every**: how many days, weeks or months apart. At most every 90 days, 12 weeks or 3 months.
    - **On**: the days of the week, for **Weekly**.
    - **Day of the month**: for **Monthly**.
    - **Ends**: **Never**, **After a number of events**, or **On a date**. It starts on **Never**.

    **After a number of events** starts on 8, and allows up to 50. **On a date** asks for **Last event on**.

    A repeating webinar asks one more question on the **Gate** step: how people register. See section 4.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Frame caption="Does it repeat? turned on. A series starts weekly and never ends.">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/webinars/create/09-new-repeats.png" alt="The Basics step with Does it repeat switched on, showing Repeats set to Weekly, Every, the On weekday chips, and Ends set to Never." />

## 3. Host: Zoom account, type and host

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the Zoom account">
    **Which Zoom account hosts it?** shows your default Zoom user. Pick another if you have more than one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick webinar or meeting">
    Pick **Webinar** or **Meeting** under **Webinar or meeting?** Nothing is picked for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let Cortana make it in Zoom">
    Keep **Create it for me, with registration set up**. Cortana makes it in Zoom for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick who hosts">
    Pick a team member under **Who hosts?** Calendar invites come from this person's calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Next">
    Click **Next** to go to **Gate**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Host: which Zoom user, webinar or meeting, and which team member sends the invites.">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/webinars/create/10-new-host.png" alt="The New webinar box on its Host step: Which Zoom account hosts it marked 1, Webinar or meeting marked 2 with Meeting picked, Should Cortana create the Zoom meeting marked 3 with Create it for me picked, Who hosts marked 4 showing a team member with No calendar connected and the Connect now and Check again buttons, and Next marked 5." />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/webinars/create/10-new-host-dark.png" alt="The New webinar box on its Host step: Which Zoom account hosts it marked 1, Webinar or meeting marked 2 with Meeting picked, Should Cortana create the Zoom meeting marked 3 with Create it for me picked, Who hosts marked 4 showing a team member with No calendar connected and the Connect now and Check again buttons, and Next marked 5." />
</Frame>

The helper under **Webinar or meeting?** says *Both work the same in Cortana: registrations, attendance and conversions.*

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Webinar or meeting: which one?">
    Both work the same in Cortana. The difference is in Zoom.

    A Zoom webinar needs the Webinars plan on that Zoom account. Without it, Cortana says *This Zoom account has no Webinars plan, so it cannot hold Zoom webinars. Choose Meeting instead, or add the Webinars plan to this account in Zoom.*
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Use a Zoom webinar or meeting you already made">
    Pick **Pick an existing one on this account**. Pick **Webinar** or **Meeting** first, then your upcoming ones appear in a list.

    A row marked **already in Cortana** is greyed out. A row marked **archived in Cortana, open to restore** links to that old webinar.

    If you left the name empty, Cortana uses the name from Zoom.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who hosts, and calendar invites">
    Each team member shows **Calendar connected** or **No calendar connected**.

    If the host has no calendar, the box says *Until Casey connects a calendar, registrants get no calendar invite from the host. Each person connects their own.* It uses the host's first name.

    The host connects their own calendar, signed in as themselves:

    1. Click **Connect now**. **Settings** opens on the **Users** tab, in a new tab.
    2. Open **My Profile**. Find **Calendar & Meeting Integrations**.
    3. Click **Connect Google Calendar** or **Connect Outlook Calendar**, and follow the sign-in.
    4. Come back to **New webinar** and click **Check again**. The host now shows **Calendar connected**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="No Zoom account shows">
    The box says *No Zoom account is connected yet.* Click **Connect Zoom** under it. It opens the same **Connect your own Zoom app** box as in [1) Connect Zoom](/webinars-connect-zoom).

    **Connect another** does the same when you already have one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## 4. Gate: the form people sign up with

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the form">
    Under **What form feeds into this?**, pick one Cortana form. Use **Search forms** to find it fast.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say how people register">
    For a repeating webinar only, pick an answer under **How do people register?**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the pitch minute">
    In **When does the pitch start?**, type the minute your offer starts. Leave it empty if there is none.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create webinar">
    Click **Create webinar**. Cortana makes it in Zoom and sets up sign-ups.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Gate: the form that signs people up, how far one sign-up goes, and the pitch minute.">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/webinars/create/11-new-gate.png" alt="The New webinar box on its Gate step: What form feeds into this marked 1, with a list of Cortana forms and a Cortana calendars group, a Create a form button, How do people register marked 2 with Register for each event individually picked and an orange note under it, When does the pitch start marked 3, and the Create webinar button marked 4." />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/webinars/create/11-new-gate-dark.png" alt="The New webinar box on its Gate step: What form feeds into this marked 1, with a list of Cortana forms and a Cortana calendars group, a Create a form button, How do people register marked 2 with Register for each event individually picked and an orange note under it, When does the pitch start marked 3, and the Create webinar button marked 4." />
</Frame>

The helper says *Anyone who sends one of these forms is registered, with their own personal join link.*

<Warning>
  **Create webinar makes a real webinar or meeting in Zoom.**

  Cortana cannot delete it from Zoom later. If you made it by mistake, delete it in Zoom yourself.
</Warning>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which forms can sign people up">
    The list groups your forms by where they live:

    - **Cortana forms**: pick one.
    - **Cortana calendars**: a booking calendar's form.
    - **GoHighLevel forms**: forms and surveys from GoHighLevel.
    - **Typeform forms**, **Tally forms**, **HubSpot forms**, **ClickFunnels forms**.

    You can tick forms from other tools as well as, or instead of, a Cortana form. Pick **No Cortana form (use a connected source below)** to use only those.

    A form from another tool shows here only once it is a conversion source in Cortana. Set that up under **Ad Tracking**, **Conversion Setup**. See [Conversion tracking](/4-conversion-tracking).

    Tags, stages, appointments and custom webhooks cannot sign people up.

    A form marked **draft** is still off. You can pick it, but it has no link to share until you turn it on in the form builder.

    The form must ask for an email, because Zoom signs people up by email. Without one, Cortana says *That form has no email question. Zoom registers people by email, so add one first.*

    **Create a form** makes a new form named after your webinar, and opens it in a new tab. Come back and the list refreshes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Register for each event, or register once?">
    This only shows for a webinar that repeats. Nothing is picked for you.

    - **Register for each event individually**: *One registration is for the next event only.*
    - **Register once, invited to every event**: *One registration covers the whole series, with an invite to each event.*

    A series that never ends, with a sign-up for each event, stops after 50 events. Cortana warns you: *With a registration for each event, this series stops after 50 events (about a year if weekly), because adding more in Zoom would drop people’s registrations. Pick “Register once” to keep it going.*
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What the pitch minute does">
    The helper says *Minutes from the real start. Cortana fires “Saw The Pitch” for everyone still in the room then. Leave empty if there is none.*

    "The real start" is when the event really began in Zoom, not the planned time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## 5. Done: your link and your conversions

The box now reads **Your webinar is ready**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy your webinar's own link">
    Under **Your opt-in (registration form)**, click **Copy**. Share this link, and no other.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or copy the code for your website">
    Under **Embed on your site**, click the code box. It copies all of it. Paste it into a custom HTML block.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what counts as a conversion">
    Under **Which conversions count**, pick a conversion for each event. Then click **Save conversions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the webinar">
    Click **Open webinar** to see its page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  *You must use this link, or this embed, for Cortana to know who signed up, who joined, who watched, and for how long. The form's plain link, or the form on your site without this embed, signs nobody up for this webinar.*
</Warning>

The box also says *Don't worry, you can grab this later from the webinar page too.* [3) How People Sign Up](/webinars-registration) explains the link in full.

Nothing counts as a conversion until you choose and save. [5) Count Webinars As Conversions](/webinars-conversions) walks you through it.

| **Does it repeat?** | Makes the webinar a series. | Off | Off for a one-off. | You cannot change the repeat rule in Cortana later. |
| **Repeats** / **Every** / **On** / **Day of the month** | How often the series runs. | **Weekly**, every 1 week | Match how you really run it. | At most every 90 days, 12 weeks or 3 months. |
| **Ends** | When the series stops. | **Never** | **Never** with **Register once**. | **After a number of events** allows up to 50. |
| **Which Zoom account hosts it?** | The Zoom user that owns it in Zoom. | Your default Zoom user | Your main host. | Only connected users show. |
| **Webinar or meeting?** | Makes a Zoom webinar or a Zoom meeting. | Nothing picked | **Meeting** if you have no Webinars plan. | Webinars need Zoom's Webinars plan. |
| **Create it for me, with registration set up** | Cortana makes it in Zoom. | Picked | Keep it. | It makes a real Zoom item Cortana cannot delete. |
| **Pick an existing one on this account** | Uses one you already made in Zoom. | Not picked | Use it to bring in one you made earlier. | One already in Cortana is greyed out. |
| **Who hosts?** | The team member whose calendar sends invites. | Nothing picked | Someone with a connected calendar. | No calendar means no calendar invites. |
| **What form feeds into this?** | The form people sign up with. | Nothing picked | One Cortana form. | It must ask for an email. |
| **How do people register?** | How far one sign-up goes in a series. | Nothing picked | **Register once, invited to every event**. | Only shows for a repeating webinar. |
| **When does the pitch start?** | The minute your offer starts. | Empty | Set it if you pitch. | Counted from the real start. |
| **Create webinar** | Makes it in Zoom and sets up sign-ups. | Not applicable | Click it once. | Cannot be undone in Zoom from Cortana. |

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Next does nothing and a message pops up">
    A message at the screen's edge names what is missing. For example *Give the webinar a name*, *Pick a day and a time*, *Pick webinar or meeting*, *Pick who hosts*, or *Pick the form that registers people*. Fix that box, then click **Next** again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says the time has passed">
    The messages are *That time has passed. Pick a later one.* and *That start time has already passed. Pick a later one.* Pick a later time or day.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says my Zoom account has no Webinars plan">
    The message is *This Zoom account has no Webinars plan, so it cannot hold Zoom webinars. Choose Meeting instead, or add the Webinars plan to this account in Zoom.* Go back to **Host** and pick **Meeting**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says the form has no email question">
    The message is *That form has no email question. Zoom registers people by email, so add one first.* Add an email question to the form, then try again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says Zoom no longer accepts this account">
    The message is *Zoom no longer accepts this account. Reconnect Zoom in Settings → Integrations.* You may also see *Reconnect this Zoom account before using it for a webinar*. Reconnect the Zoom user, as [1) Connect Zoom](/webinars-connect-zoom) shows.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says Zoom is busy or did not answer">
    The messages are *Zoom is busy right now. Try again in a minute.* and *Zoom did not answer as expected. Try again in a minute.* Wait a minute and try again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says Zoom refused this">
    The message starts *Zoom refused this:* and then gives Zoom's own reason. Fix what Zoom names, then try again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says Zoom created it, but Cortana could not finish">
    An orange note starts *Zoom created the webinar, but* and gives the reason. The button reads **Retry**.

    Fix what the note says, then click **Retry**. It uses the same Zoom webinar, so nothing is made twice.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Other messages">
    *That Zoom webinar is already in Cortana* means you picked one that is already here.

    *The host must be a member of this workspace* means that person left your team. Pick another host.

    *You do not have permission to manage webinars.* means your role cannot change webinars. Ask an owner.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the webinar time in Cortana?">
    No. Change the day, the time or the repeat rule in Zoom. Cortana follows Zoom.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need the Zoom Webinars plan?">
    Only for a Zoom webinar. Pick **Meeting** and it works the same in Cortana.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use a Zoom meeting I already made?">
    Yes. On the **Host** step, pick **Pick an existing one on this account**, then pick it from the list.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can one form sign people up for more than one webinar?">
    Yes. Each webinar gets its own link to the same form. Only that link signs people up for that webinar. See [3) How People Sign Up](/webinars-registration).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I made a webinar by mistake. How do I remove it?">
    In Cortana, archive it. See [6) Manage Your Webinars](/webinars-manage). In Zoom, delete the meeting or webinar yourself. Cortana cannot delete it there.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
