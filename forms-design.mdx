Two jobs live here, and both change what a visitor sees.

The **Form** tab on the right sets the look: colours, writing, how pages move, and whether a progress bar shows. The **Ending rules** card at the bottom left sets what happens after someone presses the button.

Everything changes in the middle column as you set it, so you never have to guess.

<Note>
  **Before you start.** You need a form with at least one question. See [Build your form](/forms-build).

  Ending rules can only test answers, so write your questions before you write your rules. Sending people to a page of your own? Have that page's web address ready. It has to start with `http://` or `https://`.
</Note>

## 1. Pick a look

The right column has two tabs. Click **Form**, the left one. Four sections appear, one under the other: **Theme**, **Colors**, **Typography** and **Behaviour**. The panel scrolls, so the last two are below what you see at first.

**Theme** gives you twelve ready-made looks, in two rows.

Six light ones: **Sage**, **Snow**, **Cream**, **Azure**, **Blush** and **Lavender**. Six dark ones: **Midnight**, **Royal**, **Obsidian**, **Forest**, **Ember** and **Rose**.

Each tile is a tiny drawing of your form in that look, so you can pick by eye rather than by name. Click one and the middle column repaints straight away.

<Note>
  **A brand new form has no theme picked.**

  No tile is ringed until you click one, and until then the form uses the **Sage** colours.

  So pick a tile even if you like what you see. It makes the choice yours instead of a leftover.
</Note>

## 2. Set your own colours

**Colors** has five rows: **Background**, **Text**, **Accent**, **Button** and **Button Text**. Each row has a small box and a coloured square.

Type a colour code into the box, such as `#2563EB`, or click the square and pick a colour by eye.

A theme tile fills all five in for you. Change one afterwards and the tile stops being ringed, because the look is now yours.

<Frame caption="Five colours. A theme fills them all in, then you can change any of them.">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/design/03-colors.png" alt="The Colors section of the Form tab highlighted, showing five rows labelled Background, Text, Accent, Button and Button Text, each with a small colour code box and a coloured square." />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/design/03-colors-dark.png" alt="The Colors section of the Form tab highlighted, showing five rows labelled Background, Text, Accent, Button and Button Text, each with a small colour code box and a coloured square." />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  **The card, the lines and the answer boxes follow your Background.**

  Cortana works them out for you, so a light background gets a white card and a dark one gets a raised card.

  That does mean a dark **Background** with dark **Text** is unreadable. Watch the middle column after every change.
</Warning>

## 3. Pick the writing

**Typography** sits under **Colors**. It sets the font and the size for the whole form.

**Font** offers seven: **Inter**, **Roboto**, **Open Sans**, **Lato**, **Poppins**, **Montserrat** and **System**. **System** is the visitor's own device font, the one their phone or computer uses everywhere. A new form uses **Inter**.

**Size** is a slider. It runs from 12px to 24px, and a new form sits at 16px. Its hint reads *Sets the base size for the whole form. Default is 16px. Inputs never go below 16px, so phones do not zoom on them.*

This is the base size, not the size of one heading. Questions stay bigger than their help text as you drag, and the middle column moves with the handle.

<Tip>
  **A theme tile never changes your writing, and Reset puts the shipped look back.**

  Clicking a tile sets the five colours only. Your font and your size stay exactly as you left them, so pick the font once and try as many looks as you like.

  **Reset theme and typography**, under the slider, returns the five colours, the font and the size in one click. A light form comes back to **Sage** and a dark one to **Midnight**. **Transition** and **Progress band** are left alone. A message appears with **Undo** on it, and you have ten seconds to change your mind.
</Tip>

## 4. Set how it behaves

**Transition** decides how one page gives way to the next. **Vertical** *slides up from the bottom*, **Horizontal** *slides in from the right*. It changes nothing about what you collect.

**Progress band** is *the form name and how far along they are, above the questions*. It is on to start with. Turn it off for a one-page form, where a progress bar tells people nothing.

## 5. Set the default ending

The ending is the screen after someone presses the button. Every form has one, and you can change it.

The **Ending rules** card sits at the bottom of the left column, under **Endings**. Its second line is a summary: **No rules · Message** means no rules and a message at the end, **2 rules · Redirect** means two rules and a redirect.

Click the card. A panel slides in from the right, headed **Ending rules**, with the line *Rules run top to bottom; the first match wins. If none match, the Default screen shows.*

<Frame caption="The Ending rules card. Its second line summarises what you have set.">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/design/06-endings-card.png" alt="The bottom of the builder's left column with the Ending rules card highlighted, under an Endings heading, showing a summary line reading one rule and Message." />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/design/06-endings-card-dark.png" alt="The bottom of the builder's left column with the Ending rules card highlighted, under an Endings heading, showing a summary line reading one rule and Message." />
</Frame>

Scroll to the bottom of the panel. **Default** is *what people see after submitting when no rule matches*. Under **Screen after submitting** you pick one of two things.

- **Show a message**: a **Headline** and a **Body**. A new form starts with `Thank you` and `Your answers have been received.`
- **Redirect to a URL**: your own page. Section 6 covers that.

Click **Save** at the bottom. **Cancel** throws away everything you changed in the panel.

<Frame caption="The Default screen catches everyone no rule matches. It cannot be removed.">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/design/07-endings-default.png" alt="The Ending rules panel with the Default block highlighted at the bottom, showing the heading Screen after submitting, the Redirect to a URL and Show a message choices, and Headline and Body boxes filled in with a thank you message." />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/design/07-endings-default-dark.png" alt="The Ending rules panel with the Default block highlighted at the bottom, showing the heading Screen after submitting, the Redirect to a URL and Show a message choices, and Headline and Body boxes filled in with a thank you message." />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  **The Default screen is what most people will see.**

  A new form thanks people in wording we wrote, not you. Rewrite it before you share the link.

  A message needs a **Headline** or a **Body**. Empty both and saving stops with **A message screen needs a headline or body.**
</Warning>

## 6. Send different people to different endings

A rule looks at an answer. If it matches, that rule's screen shows instead of the Default.

**Add rule** sits above the **Default** block. Each rule you add is numbered, so you can see the order. Rules run from the top down and the first match wins, so put your narrowest rule first. The bin at the top right of a rule removes it.

Under **When**, pick the question, then how to compare it, then type the value. Click **Add condition** for a second test. Two or more tests give you **Match all** and **Match any** to choose between.

A rule with no test cannot be saved. You see **Rule 1: A rule needs at least one condition.**

<Frame caption="A rule reads left to right: the question, the test, the value.">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/design/08-endings-rule.png" alt="The Ending rules panel with Rule 1's When row highlighted, showing a question chosen from a dropdown, the test Equals, a value box, and an Add condition button underneath." />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/design/08-endings-rule-dark.png" alt="The Ending rules panel with Rule 1's When row highlighted, showing a question chosen from a dropdown, the test Equals, a value box, and an Add condition button underneath." />
</Frame>

Under **Then show**, pick **Show a message** or **Redirect to a URL**. Pick **Redirect to a URL** and a **Redirect URL** box appears. It must start with `http://` or `https://`.

Underneath sits **URL parameters**, folded up, with a count of how many are on. Open it to pass answers over to your own page. Each row shows what it sends and the `?name=` it uses. There is also **Add custom parameter** for a fixed pair of your own.

Click **Save**.

<Frame caption="A redirect can carry answers over to your own page as URL parameters.">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/design/09-endings-redirect.png" alt="The Ending rules panel with the Default screen set to Redirect to a URL, a Redirect URL box below it, and the URL parameters row highlighted showing how many are switched on." />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/design/09-endings-redirect-dark.png" alt="The Ending rules panel with the Default screen set to Redirect to a URL, a Redirect URL box below it, and the URL parameters row highlighted showing how many are switched on." />
</Frame>

A rule can use fourteen tests.

**Equals** · **Not Equals** · **Contains** · **Does Not Contain** · **Greater Than** · **Less Than** · **Greater or Equal** · **Less or Equal** · **Is One Of** · **Is Not One Of** · **Starts With** · **Ends With** · **Is Empty** · **Is Not Empty**

The last two need no value.

<Note>
  **The middle column never shows a rule's ending.**

  Finish the form in the middle column and you always get the **Default** screen, even when a rule would have matched.

  Open the form's own page to see a rule really fire. [Share your form](/forms-share) shows you how.
</Note>

  <Accordion title="Troubleshooting">
    **My theme tile is no longer ringed.** You changed one of the five colours by hand, so the look is now yours rather than the tile's. Click the tile again to put all five back.

    **My form is unreadable after I changed a colour.** Almost always a dark **Background** with dark **Text**, or the other way round. Click a theme tile to reset all five, then change one at a time.

    **The size slider will not go any smaller or bigger.** It stops at 12px and at 24px. To make one question stand out beyond that, use **Text size** in **Field settings**.

    **My answer boxes look bigger than the size I picked.** Below 16px the answer boxes stay at 16px, on purpose. A phone zooms in on any box under 16px, which throws the visitor out of the form.

    **I clicked Reset theme and typography by mistake.** A message appears with **Undo** on it. Click that within ten seconds and everything comes back.

    **Saving says A message screen needs a headline or body.** One of your message screens is completely empty. The message names which one, either a rule number or **Default**.

    **Saving says A redirect screen needs a URL.** You chose **Redirect to a URL** and left the box empty. Type the page's address, starting with `https://`.

    **Saving says The redirect must be an http or https address.** Copy the address out of your browser's address bar rather than typing it.

    **Saving says A rule needs at least one condition.** You added a rule and then removed its only test. Add one back, or delete the rule with the bin at its top right.

    **My rule never fires.** Three things to check, in order. 1) You are testing in the middle column, which always shows the Default: open the form's own page. 2) The value does not match exactly: `Sales` is not `Sales question`. 3) An earlier rule matched first: drag your narrower rule higher.

    **I clicked Cancel and lost my rules.** **Cancel** throws away everything you changed while the panel was open, and there is no undo. Add the rules again, and click **Save** this time.
  </Accordion>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I make the form look like my brand?">
    Set the five colours under **Colors**, starting from whichever **Theme** tile is closest. **Accent** and **Button** carry your brand colour furthest. Then pick your **Font** under **Typography**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I make the writing bigger?">
    Yes. Drag **Size** under **Typography** and the whole form grows together. To change one question on its own, use **Text size** in **Field settings**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What do visitors see if their phone is in dark mode?">
    Exactly what you picked. Your form looks the same to everyone, so choose the look you want them to see.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I send someone to a different page depending on their answer?">
    Yes. Add a rule, set **When** to the answer you care about, and set **Then show** to **Redirect to a URL**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I pass an answer to my own thank-you page?">
    Yes, on a redirect. Open **URL parameters** and switch on the answers you want. Each row shows the `?name=` it will use, so whoever built your page knows what to read.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I turn people away politely?">
    Yes. Add a rule for the answer you do not want, and give it a message screen that says so. Their answers are still recorded. The rule only changes what they see.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
