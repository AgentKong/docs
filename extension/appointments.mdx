## What this tab does

The **Appointments** tab mirrors your Cortana booking calendar inside the popup, so you can check what is booked, mark whether someone showed up, and book someone in without opening the web app.

It has two sub-tabs: **Calendar** and **Booking Links**.

This page documents extension version **4.20**.

<Note>
  **Before you start**

  - The extension **connected**, with a **business selected**. See [Install and connect](/extension/install-and-connect).
  - A **booking calendar** set up for that business in Cortana.
  - An **All Access** key if you connected by key. Creating bookings and changing status are writes.
</Note>

## Work with an appointment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Click an appointment to see its details: who it is with, when, which calendar, and its current status.

    <Frame caption="An appointment's details.">
      <img src="/images/ad-tracking/chrome-extension-appointments/05-appointment-details.png" alt="The appointment details view in the extension popup showing the attendee, date and time, calendar and status, with the attendee's personal details blurred." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Change its status">
    Open the status control and pick the new state. There are six:

    | Status | Means |
    |---|---|
    | **Booked** | Scheduled, not yet happened |
    | **Completed** | The meeting took place and is finished |
    | **Rescheduled** | Moved to another time |
    | **Cancelled** | Called off |
    | **Shown** | The person turned up |
    | **Not shown** | The person did not turn up |

    <Frame caption="Changing an appointment's status.">
      <img src="/images/ad-tracking/chrome-extension-appointments/06-status-dropdown.png" alt="The appointment details view with the status dropdown open, listing Booked, Completed, Rescheduled, Cancelled, Shown and Not shown." />
    </Frame>

    <Tip>
      Marking **Shown** and **Not shown** honestly is what makes your show-rate reporting worth reading, and those statuses feed the conversion columns in Ads Manager when you track booking events.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create a booking

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the new booking form">
    Start a new booking from the calendar. Choose the contact, the calendar and the time.

    <Frame caption="Creating a booking from the popup.">
      <img src="/images/ad-tracking/chrome-extension-appointments/07-new-booking.png" alt="The new booking form in the extension popup with fields for the contact, the calendar, and the date and time, with entered values blurred." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="When the provider is fixed">
    Some calendars are tied to one provider. In that case the provider field is locked and shows who the booking will be with.

    <Frame caption="A new booking on a calendar with a fixed provider.">
      <img src="/images/ad-tracking/chrome-extension-appointments/08-new-booking-locked-provider.png" alt="The new booking form with the provider field locked to a single person and a numbered badge marking it, with the provider name blurred." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Creating a booking here creates a real appointment**, the same as booking in the web app. The attendee is notified according to that calendar's settings.
</Warning>

## Settings reference

| Setting | What it does | Default | Our recommendation | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Month, week, day** | Calendar view | Month | Day view when working a list of calls | View only |
| **Filter** | Narrows what the calendar shows | Unfiltered | Filter by calendar on busy weeks | Affects display only |
| **Status** | Marks what happened | **Booked** | Update the same day | Feeds show-rate and conversion reporting |
| **New booking** | Creates a real appointment | Not applicable | Confirm the time zone before saving | Notifies the attendee |
| **Booking Links** | Shareable link per calendar | One per calendar | Send instead of booking manually | Needs a booking handle |

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The tab says I have to select a business first">
    Pick a business in the popup header.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The calendar is empty">
    Check the business, the view and the filter. A month with bookings still looks empty on a day view of a free day.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot change a status or create a booking">
    Both are writes. If you connected with an API key, make sure it is **All Access**. See [Install and connect](/extension/install-and-connect).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The popup says I am not signed in">
    Reconnect from the popup.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I booked in the web app and it is not here yet">
    Close and reopen the popup to refetch.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Nothing above helped">
    Reload the extension at `chrome://extensions`, then reopen the popup.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is this the same calendar as the web app?">
    Yes. The popup shows your Cortana booking calendar, and changes you make here apply there.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the difference between Completed and Shown?">
    **Shown** records that the person turned up. **Completed** records that the meeting is finished. A no-show can still be closed out, which is why both exist.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does changing a status notify the attendee?">
    Status is internal record-keeping. Creating or rescheduling a booking is what triggers attendee notifications, according to the calendar's settings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I edit an appointment's time here?">
    Use **Rescheduled** to record a move, and manage the reschedule itself in the web app where the full editor lives.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
