## Overview

**Business Cal** is the working calendar for your whole business.

Every booking shows up here. It does not matter which booking page it came from. It does not matter who is meant to take the call. Bookings made in Cortana and bookings pulled in from GoHighLevel, Calendly or iClosed all sit on the same calendar.

Go to **Appointments → Business Cal** to open it. It is the tab you land on first.

Use it for three things. See what your week looks like. Mark down what happened after each call. Add a meeting that was arranged another way, like over the phone or by email.

<Note>
  **Before you start**

  - You need at least one booking. If you have none yet, read [1) Creating Your Booking Calendar](/cal-calendars).
  - Set your business time zone first in [2) Schedule & Working Hours](/cal-schedule). Every time on this tab is shown in that time zone.
</Note>

## 1. Read the Calendar

<Frame caption="Business Cal in Month view. Every booking, from every calendar and every team member.">
  <img src="/images/appointments/business-cal/01-month.png" alt="The Business Cal tab showing a month grid for July 2026 with appointment chips on various days, each showing a time and a contact name, plus Filter, Day, Week, Month and New Booking controls above the grid." />
</Frame>

Each booking shows up as a small coloured block. It shows the **start time** and the **contact's name**.

The colour tells you which booking page the meeting came from. That is how you spot a discovery call next to a site visit without opening either one. You choose that colour on the **Calendars** tab.

Two things to watch for:

- A block with a **line through it** is a cancelled booking. It stays on the calendar on purpose, so you keep the history.
- **+N more** shows up when a day has more bookings than fit in the box. Click it to see the rest of that day.

Switch between **Day**, **Week** and **Month** at the top right. Use the arrows next to the month name to move forward and back in time.

<Warning>
  **Day and Week views only show 07:00 to 19:00.**

  A booking before 07:00 or after 19:00 is not drawn in those two views. The booking is still there. You can still see it in **Month** view and on the **Events** tab.

  So if a booking seems to disappear when you click **Week**, check **Month** view before you worry.
</Warning>

## 2. Filter Down to What You Care About

Click **Filter** to cut the calendar down to less.

<Frame caption="The filter panel. Hide whole calendars, or hide people one by one.">
  <img src="/images/appointments/business-cal/02-filter.png" alt="The Business Cal filter panel open, listing team member names with toggles including an Unassigned entry, over the month grid." />
</Frame>

You can show or hide two things:

- **Calendars.** Switch off the ones you do not need. Now you see only your sales calls, with no site visits in the way.
- **Team members.** Switch off everyone else so one person sees only their own day. The list also has **Unassigned**. That covers bookings that were never given to anybody.

The panel also lets you **change a calendar's colour**. You pick from a small set of colours. This is the fastest way to make one type of meeting stand out.

Filters only change what you see on screen. They never change a booking itself.

## 3. Open a Booking

Click any block on the calendar to open its details.

You get the title, the status, the date, the start and end time, how long it runs, the time zone, the contact, the location or meeting link, the description, and any notes.

Did the booking come from one of your forms? Then the answers appear under **Responses**. Read them before you dial, so you know what the lead already told you.

The contact's email address and phone number are clickable. Click either one to start an email or a call from the panel.

A cancelled booking also shows the reason it was cancelled.

### Change the status

Use the status dropdown to record what actually happened on the call.

| Status | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| **Booked** | The meeting is set and has not happened yet. Every new booking starts here. |
| **Shown** | They turned up for the meeting. |
| **Not shown** | They did not turn up. |
| **Completed** | The meeting happened and you have finished dealing with it. |
| **Rescheduled** | You moved the meeting to another time. |
| **Cancelled** | The meeting is off and nobody is going to attend. |

If the booking came from GoHighLevel, your change is sent back to GoHighLevel too.

Your change is also protected. The next automatic sync will not wipe out a status you set by hand.

<Warning>
  **Setting a booking to Cancelled here is only a label. It does not cancel the meeting.**

  It marks the record, and it moves the turn to the next person in the round-robin rota. That is all it does.

  Here is what it does **not** do. It does not free the time, so nobody else can book that slot. It does not stop the reminder emails already lined up for that meeting. It does not remove the event from a linked Google or Outlook calendar.

  To really cancel, use the **Cancel** link in the booking confirmation, or the one in the guest's email. That frees the time and stops the reminders.
</Warning>

### Change who it is assigned to

The same panel lets you hand the booking to a different team member. You can also clear the name so nobody has it.

Use this when the round-robin sent a call to the wrong person.

## 4. Add a Booking by Hand

Click **New Booking** to create a meeting yourself. Nobody has to go through a booking page.

Use it for calls you agreed over the phone, by email, or in a direct message.

<Frame caption="The New Booking dialog. It puts the meeting straight on your calendar, with no booking page involved.">
  <img src="/images/appointments/business-cal/04-new-booking.png" alt="The New Booking dialog showing fields for the destination calendar, video provider, contact search, assignee, date, timezone, time, duration, title, description and location." />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose where it goes">
    Pick the calendar the meeting lands on, either **Google** or **Outlook**. Then pick how you will meet: **Google Meet**, **Zoom**, **Teams**, or none at all.

    Anything you have not linked to Cortana yet is locked. Each person links their own accounts under **Settings → Users → My Profile**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick the contact">
    Search for a contact you already have. Cortana shows you a short history of that person, so you can check you picked the right one.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the details">
    Choose who takes the meeting. Then set the date, the time zone, the time and how long it runs. Give it a title.

    Description and location are up to you. You can leave both empty.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create it">
    Cortana checks the time is free, adds the event to the linked calendar, saves the booking and emails a confirmation.

    If the calendar event cannot be added, Cortana undoes the lot. You never end up with a half-made booking.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **A booking you add by hand does not hold its time slot.**

  It shows on your calendar. But your booking pages do not know it exists. So a lead can still book that exact time and you get a clash.

  Do you add meetings this way often? Then block the same time on your linked Google or Outlook calendar as well. Booking pages do check that calendar before offering a time.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A booking is missing from Week or Day view">
    Those two views only draw 07:00 to 19:00. Click **Month** view, or open the **Events** tab. Both show every booking, whatever the time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Times look wrong by a few hours">
    Business Cal shows times in your business booking time zone. Check it first under **Appointments → Settings → Schedule**.

    The **Events** tab can show a different clock. Read the warning on [6) Events](/cal-events) for that one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cancelled a booking but the lead still got a reminder">
    Setting the status to **Cancelled** does not stop reminders that are already lined up.

    Use the **Cancel** link in the booking confirmation instead, or the one in the guest's email. That stops the reminders properly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A time I cancelled still cannot be booked">
    Same reason. Changing the status does not free the time.

    Cancel through the guest's cancel link and the time opens up again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Bookings show as Unassigned">
    Nobody was given those bookings when they were made.

    Open each one and pick a team member. Then add your team members to that booking calendar under **Calendars → Details → Distribution & Team**, so new bookings get an owner from now on.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The calendar looks empty">
    Open the **Filter** panel and check it. A calendar or a person you hid earlier stays hidden until you switch them back on.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does this show bookings from GoHighLevel, Calendly and iClosed?">
    Yes. You need to link those tools first, and copy their calendars across. [3) Calendar Sync](/cal-calendar-sync) shows you how.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can one person see only their own bookings?">
    Yes. Click **Filter** and switch everyone else off.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the difference between Completed and Shown?">
    **Shown** means the person turned up. **Completed** means the meeting is over and you have finished with it.

    Agree as a team which one you use, and stick to it. Both feed your reports, so mixing them makes the numbers hard to trust.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is a cancelled booking still on my calendar?">
    That is on purpose. Cancelled bookings stay on the calendar with a line through them, so you keep the record instead of losing it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I drag a booking to a different time?">
    No. Open the booking and use the **Reschedule** link. That moves it properly and tells the guest.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
