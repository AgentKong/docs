# Tracking Links

A tracking link is a short link that knows who you sent it to.

You save a destination once, for example your booking page, then drop it into any conversation. Cortana mints a **separate link for each contact**, so when someone clicks it, Cortana knows exactly which person clicked. A booking made on that page then attaches to the right contact instead of arriving as an anonymous lead, as long as the page carries your pixel.

This solves a specific problem. An Instagram DM carries no email address and no phone number, so when that person books a call on your scheduling page, nothing connects the booking back to the DM. A tracking link supplies the missing identity.

You will find it in **Settings → Tracking Links**.

<Note>
  **Before you start**

  - **Ad tracking must be on** for this business. If it is off, the tab shows **Ad tracking is off** instead of the list.
  - For branded links, at least one **verified tracking domain**. See [Setup Pixel](/2-setup-pixel) for domain and pixel setup. Without one, links are served from Cortana's shared shortener instead.
  - The **pixel installed on the destination page**. The click is only half the job: the pixel on the page the person lands on is what completes the match back to the contact.
</Note>

<Warning>
  **A click is matched when the person lands on a page carrying your pixel.**

  The link records the click and hands the contact's identity to the destination page, where your pixel picks it up. If someone submits a form or books without ever loading a page that has your pixel on it, there is nothing to complete the match.

  Keep tracking links pointed at real pages you control or have pixelled, such as your booking page or your checkout.
</Warning>

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings and go to the Tracking Links tab">
    In your business, open **Settings** and select the **Tracking Links** tab. You can jump straight there with `?tab=tracking-links` on your settings URL.

    The heading reads **Tracking Links**, above the line *"Save a destination once, then send it from any conversation. Every contact gets their own link, so you can see exactly who clicked, even from an Instagram DM."*

    Underneath, a line tells you which host your links are served from. If it says **"Connect your own domain for better deliverability and link previews"**, you have no verified domain yet and links will use Cortana's shared shortener.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your first tracking link">
    On an empty tab you see **No tracking links yet** and the line *"Add your booking page or checkout link once, and send it from any conversation."*

    Click **New tracking link**. The **New tracking link** dialog opens, described as *"Save a destination once, then drop it into any conversation. Each contact gets their own trackable link."*

    <Frame caption="Settings, Tracking Links. Each preset shows how many links went out and how many were clicked.">
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/settings/tracking-links/tl-01-tab.png" alt="The Tracking Links settings tab with the New tracking link button and the presets table highlighted. The table lists Name, Destination, Sent and Clicks columns with three saved presets." />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/settings/tracking-links/tl-01-tab-dark.png" alt="The Tracking Links settings tab with the New tracking link button and the presets table highlighted. The table lists Name, Destination, Sent and Clicks columns with three saved presets." />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Name it">
    Type a **Name**. This is what your team sees in the picker when they are mid-conversation, so name it for the job it does, not for the vendor. **Booking call** beats **Calendly**.

    The placeholder shows `Booking call`. Names must be unique among your active links, and the check ignores capitals, so **Booking call** and **booking call** count as the same name.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Paste the destination URL">
    Put the real page you want people to land on into **Destination URL**, for example your scheduling page. The placeholder shows `https://calendly.com/your-team/intro`.

    It must start with `http://` or `https://`. If it does not, saving fails with **"The destination must start with http:// or https://"**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the link domain">
    The **Link domain** row shows which host will serve this link. What it offers depends on how many verified tracking domains you have:

    | Verified domains | What you see |
    | --- | --- |
    | Two or more | A dropdown listing them, pre-set to your first one. |
    | Exactly one | A line reading **"Links look like `yourdomain.com/t/abc1234`"**. Cortana uses it automatically. |
    | None | A line reading **"Links look like `lnkhop.co/t/abc1234`"**, Cortana's shared shortener. |

    A dropdown only appears when you genuinely have a choice to make. Below the row, the helper text reminds you that *"A domain your prospects recognise gets clicked more than a generic shortener."* With two or more domains it changes to *"Links from this preset are served from this domain. Links you already sent keep the domain they were sent on."*

    The **+** button beside the row, labelled **Add a domain**, takes you off to verify a new one. You can do that at any point, including later.

    <Frame caption="The New tracking link dialog: Name, Destination URL and Link domain.">
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/settings/tracking-links/tl-02-create-dialog.png" alt="The New tracking link dialog with the Name field, Destination URL field and Link domain row highlighted and numbered. Below the domain row a line reads that links look like lnkhop.co slash t slash abc1234." />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/settings/tracking-links/tl-02-create-dialog-dark.png" alt="The New tracking link dialog with the Name field, Destination URL field and Link domain row highlighted and numbered. Below the domain row a line reads that links look like lnkhop.co slash t slash abc1234." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save it">
    Click **Create link**. A confirmation reads **"Tracking link created"** and the link appears in the table.

    The table lists **Name**, **Destination**, **Sent** and **Clicks**. **Sent** counts how many individual links have been minted from this preset, and **Clicks** counts how many were clicked. Both start at zero.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send one from a conversation">
    Open any conversation in **Conversations**. In the message composer, click the **link** button, tooltipped **Insert tracking link**.

    A search box appears reading **Search tracking links...**, with your saved links underneath. Search matches both the name and the destination, so typing `calendly` finds a link named **Intro call**.

    Click the one you want. Cortana mints a link for that specific contact and drops the real URL straight into your message box, where you can read it before you send. Add your own wording around it and send as normal.

    <Frame caption="The tracking-link picker in the composer, with its search field.">
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/integrations/instagram-messenger-dms/dm-06-composer-picker.png" alt="The message composer with the tracking link picker open, showing a highlighted search field reading Search tracking links and a list of saved presets with their destinations underneath." />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/integrations/instagram-messenger-dms/dm-06-composer-picker-dark.png" alt="The message composer with the tracking link picker open, showing a highlighted search field reading Search tracking links and a list of saved presets with their destinations underneath." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Re-sending the same link to the same person reuses the same short link rather than creating a second one. Your **Sent** count stays honest, and both messages report to one click record.
</Tip>

## Why a branded domain matters

Links inside an Instagram DM are judged harshly. A generic shortener host looks like spam to the person receiving it, and to the platform carrying it. A link on your own domain looks like you.

That is why the tab nudges you toward connecting a domain. If you send links inside DMs, this is worth doing before you send at volume.

Links already sent keep working from whatever host they were sent on. Verifying a domain later changes new links only, and never breaks one already in someone's inbox.

## Sub-features

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Editing a link">
    Open the **⋮** menu on the row and choose **Edit**. You can change the name, the destination, and the domain.

    <Frame caption="The row menu. Edit and Archive live here; the arrows beside it reorder.">
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/settings/tracking-links/tl-03-row-menu.png" alt="A tracking link row with its actions menu open, showing Edit and Archive options." />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/settings/tracking-links/tl-03-row-menu-dark.png" alt="A tracking link row with its actions menu open, showing Edit and Archive options." />
    </Frame>

    <Warning>
      Changing the destination does **not** redirect links you already sent. Each minted link stores its own copy of the destination, so links already in someone's inbox keep going to the old address. Only links minted after the change use the new one.

      The dialog warns you about this as soon as you edit the destination, and tells you how many links already went out from that preset. If you need everyone to land somewhere new, archive this preset and create a new one.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Archiving and restoring">
    Presets are archived, never deleted. Open the **⋮** menu and choose **Archive**, then confirm in the **Archive this tracking link?** dialog.

    **Links you already sent keep working and keep counting clicks.** Archiving only removes the preset from the picker your team sees in conversations, so nobody sends it by mistake. Nothing in anybody's inbox breaks.

    To bring one back, turn on **Show archived** in the top right, find the row marked **Archived**, then **⋮ → Restore**. A restored preset goes to the **end** of the list, because its old position stopped meaning anything while it was hidden.

      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/settings/tracking-links/tl-04-archive-confirm-dark.png" alt="The Archive this tracking link dialog explaining that links already sent keep working and keep counting clicks, and that archiving only removes the preset from the picker in conversations." />

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Reordering">
    Use the **↑** and **↓** buttons on each row. The order you set is the order your team sees in the composer picker, so put your most-sent link at the top.

    Only active links can be reordered. Archived rows have no arrows.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The duplicate name rule">
    Two active links cannot share a name. Saving a duplicate fails, inside the form rather than as a toast, with:

    > A tracking link named "Booking call" already exists.

    The check **ignores capitals**, so **Booking call** and **booking call** collide.

    It only looks at **active** links. You can archive **Booking call** and create a new active link with the same name. Restoring the archived one then fails, because that would leave two active links sharing a name:

    > Cannot restore: an active tracking link named "Booking call" already exists. Rename that one first.

    </Frame>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Creating a link without leaving the conversation">
    If the link you need does not exist yet, you do not have to go to Settings. In the composer picker, click **New tracking link** at the bottom.

    Fill in **Name** and **Destination URL**, then click **Save and insert**. Cortana saves the preset for everyone, mints a link for the contact you are talking to, and drops it into your message in one go.

    **Manage all links**, the settings icon beside it, opens the full **Tracking Links** tab.

    <Frame caption="The picker's inline create view. Save and insert does both in one click.">

    </Frame>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What the person clicking sees">
    They see a short link such as `yourdomain.com/t/abc1234`. Clicking it sends them straight to your destination page. There is no interstitial and nothing that looks like tracking.

    In the background Cortana records the click against that contact and passes their identity to the destination page, where your pixel picks it up and ties the visit, and anything they do next, back to the right person.

    Automated scanners that follow links, such as link previews in a messaging app, are filtered out so they do not register as real clicks.
  </Accordion>

## Settings reference

| Setting | What it does | Default | Recommendation | Caveat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Name** | What your team sees in the composer picker. | Empty, placeholder `Booking call` | Name it for the job, not the vendor. | Must be unique among active links. Ignores capitals. |
| **Destination URL** | Where the person lands. | Empty, placeholder `https://calendly.com/your-team/intro` | Point it at a page carrying your pixel. | Must start with `http://` or `https://`. Editing it never changes links already sent. |
| **Link domain** | Which verified domain serves the short link. | First verified domain | Use your own domain for links sent in DMs. | Only becomes a dropdown with two or more verified domains. Below that, Cortana picks the host and shows you what links will look like. |
| **Show archived** | Reveals archived presets in the table. | Off | Turn on only to restore something. | Lives in the header so it stays reachable when the list is empty. |
| Archive | Removes a preset from the composer picker. | — | Use instead of deleting. | Presets are never hard-deleted. Links already sent keep working and keep counting clicks. |
| Restore | Returns an archived preset to the picker. | — | — | Lands at the end of the list. Fails if an active link already has that name. |
| Reorder | Sets the order in the composer picker. | Newest last | Put your most-sent link first. | Active links only. |
| Composer picker | Mints a per-contact link and inserts it. | — | Read the URL before sending. | Re-inserting for the same contact and destination reuses the same link. |

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The Link domain row has no dropdown, just a line of text">
    That is expected unless you have **two or more** verified tracking domains. With one domain or none there is no choice to make, so Cortana picks the host and shows you what your links will look like instead.

    If the line reads `lnkhop.co/t/abc1234`, you have no verified domain and links use Cortana's shared shortener. Click the **+** button beside the row, or see [Setup Pixel](/2-setup-pixel), to verify your own.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot save my link, it says the name is taken">
    Another **active** link already uses that name. The check ignores capitals, so **Booking call** clashes with **booking call**.

    Either pick a different name, or turn on **Show archived** to check whether the name is on an archived preset you would rather restore than recreate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I archived a link, do the ones I already sent still work?">
    Yes. Archiving only takes the preset out of the composer picker. Every link already sent keeps resolving to its destination and keeps counting clicks. Nothing in anyone's inbox breaks.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Clicks count is zero but I know they clicked">
    Check these:

    1. **Was it a real click?** Automated link scanners are filtered out on purpose.
    2. **Did they land on a pixelled page?** The click is recorded on the redirect, but matching the visit to the contact needs your pixel on the destination page.
    3. **Are you looking at the right preset?** The count is per preset. A link minted from a pasted URL rather than this preset counts separately.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I changed the destination but people still land on the old page">
    That is by design. Links already sent keep their original destination forever, because each one stores its own copy. Only links minted after your edit use the new address.

    To move everyone, archive the old preset and create a new one, then send the new link.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The tab says Ad tracking is off">
    Tracking links depend on ad tracking being enabled for the business. Turn ad tracking on, or ask whoever administers the account to, and the tab loads normally.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why not just paste my Calendly link into the DM?">
    A plain link tells you someone booked, but not who. Since an Instagram DM carries no email address, nothing ties that booking back to the conversation. A tracking link is unique to that one person, so the booking lands on their contact record.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does every contact get a different link?">
    Yes. That is the whole point. Each contact gets their own short link to the same destination, which is what makes the click identifiable.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I see which message drove the click?">
    No. Clicks attach to the contact, not to the individual message you sent. If you send the same link twice to one person, both messages share a single link and a single click record.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I send the same link to someone twice?">
    They get the same short link both times, and it counts as one link in **Sent**. Cortana deliberately reuses an existing link for the same contact and destination rather than minting a new one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I delete a tracking link?">
    Not permanently. You archive it, which removes it from the composer picker while every link already sent keeps working. This protects you from breaking a link that is already sitting in a customer's inbox.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need my own domain?">
    No, but it helps. Without one, links come from Cortana's shared shortener. Inside an Instagram DM a generic shortener reads as spam to recipients and to the platform, so your own domain gets better delivery and better link previews.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
