## Overview

**Events** is the record side of the Appointments area. **Business Cal** shows you a calendar. **Events** shows you tables instead. You can sort them, search them and read them.

Go to **Appointments → Events**. You will see two sub-tabs.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Appointment Booked" icon="calendar-check">
    Every booking gets one row. You see its status and its lead score. Work through your calls here.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Form Submitted" icon="chart-simple">
    Everyone who opened a booking page. That includes people who started the form and gave up. Use this to see how well a page turns visitors into calls.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## 1. Appointment Booked

Each booking gets one row. Here is what each column tells you.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Contact** | The person's name and email address. |
| **Scheduled Date** | The date and time the meeting will happen. |
| **Status** | Booked, Shown, Not shown, Completed, Rescheduled or Cancelled. Click the status to change it. |
| **Lead Score** | A score from 0 to 100, worked out from their form answers. You only see a score if you set scoring up first. Hover over it to see which answers earned points. |
| **Phone** | Their phone number, if you have one. |
| **Created** | When the booking was made. This is not the day of the meeting. |

<Frame caption="Appointment Booked. Every booking with its status and lead score.">
  <img src="/images/appointments/events/01-appointment-booked.png" alt="The Appointment Booked sub-tab showing a table of bookings with columns for contact, scheduled date, status, lead score, phone and created date." />
</Frame>

Compare **Created** with **Scheduled Date**. It tells you a lot.

A big gap means people book far ahead. People who book far ahead forget more often. Send them a reminder closer to the day.

A dash in **Lead Score** means that booking has no score. Either the form has no scored questions, or you set scoring up after the booking came in. Old bookings never get scored later. See [Score Your Leads](/cal-calendars#4-score-your-leads).

<Warning>
  **This table can show a different time to Business Cal.** The two tabs get the time from two different settings. So the same booking can show two different times on the same screen.

  Say your business is set to Sri Lanka time and someone books 10:30. **Business Cal** shows `10:30`. This table shows `1:00 AM GMT-4`.

  **Trust Business Cal.** It uses the booking timezone you picked under **Settings → Schedule**. If the two tabs disagree, nobody has moved the meeting. The time on **Business Cal** is the real one.
</Warning>

## 2. Form Submitted

This sub-tab answers a different question. Not "what is booked" but "is this booking page working".

<Frame caption="Form Submitted. The funnel across the top, every lead underneath.">
  <img src="/images/appointments/events/02-form-submitted.png" alt="The Form Submitted sub-tab showing four funnel cards for page views, partial, form submission and booked, above a Booking Leads table with status, name, email, phone, calendar, outcome, created and responses columns." />
</Frame>

### The funnel

You get four cards across the top. Each one counts a different group of people.

| Card | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Page views** | Everyone who opened a booking page. |
| **Partial** | People who started typing in the form, then stopped and left. |
| **Form submission** | People who finished the whole form and sent it. |
| **Booked** | People who finished the form and then picked a time. |

Read the four numbers left to right. They should drop at each step. How fast they drop tells you what to fix.

Lots of page views but few partials? The page is not selling the call. Rewrite it.

Lots of submissions but few bookings? You are not offering enough times, or the next free time is too far away.

<Note>
  **Partial rows are real leads.** Cortana saves the name and email a couple of seconds after someone types a real email address. It does that before they finish the form. So someone who quit halfway is still a person you can call. You will only find them on this tab.
</Note>

### The Booking Leads table

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Status** | Partial, Form submission or Booked. |
| **Name**, **Email**, **Phone** | What the person typed in. A dash means they left before they got to that box. |
| **Calendar** | The booking page they were on. |
| **Outcome** | Where your rules sent this person after they answered. |
| **Created** | When this person landed on your page. |
| **Responses** | Click **View** to read every answer they gave. |

**Outcome** is the one to watch. It proves your rules are doing their job.

- **Calendar** means they saw the calendar. This is the normal path.
- **Routed** means one of your **Ending rules** matched their answers. Cortana sent them somewhere specific instead.
- A dash means no rule ran at all. That is normal for a partial lead. They left before they answered enough.

Use **All Statuses** at the top to show only one group. Use the search box to find one person by name. Click any row to open that person's full history.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The time here doesn't match Business Cal">
    This is expected. See the warning above. The two tabs get the time from two different settings. Trust **Business Cal**. Do not change the booking.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Lead Score is empty for everyone">
    No question on that form has points on it yet. You add points one question at a time. Go to **Calendars → Questions → Field settings → Lead score**. Only four question types can carry points: single select, multi-select, dropdown and number. See [Score Your Leads](/cal-calendars#4-score-your-leads).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Scores appeared for new bookings but not old ones">
    This is expected. Cortana works out a score at the moment someone sends the form. It never works it out again. So points you add today will not score yesterday's bookings. Leave the old rows as they are.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Page views are much higher than form submissions">
    Some gap is normal. A very big gap means the page is asking for too much, too soon. It can also mean you never replaced the sample text that comes with a new calendar. Open your own booking link and read it as if you had never seen it. Then rewrite anything that puts you off.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Lots of partials and almost no bookings">
    People are filling in your form and then finding no time they can take. Open the calendar and check two things. First, your open hours must overlap the hours your team actually works. Second, check **Min Notice**. If it is too high, the first free slot lands too far away and people give up.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A booking is on Business Cal but not in this table">
    Check which sub-tab you are on. **Appointment Booked** lists bookings. **Form Submitted** lists people who used a booking page. If you added the booking yourself with **New Booking**, it shows on **Appointment Booked** only. Nobody filled in a form, so there is nothing to show on the other tab.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between Partial and Form submission?">
    **Partial** means they started the form and stopped. **Form submission** means they finished it but have not picked a time yet. You can call both groups. Their phone number and email sit right there in the table.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I see what someone answered?">
    Yes. Find their row and click **View** in the **Responses** column.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does this only cover Cortana booking pages?">
    **Appointment Booked** shows everything. That includes bookings pulled in from GoHighLevel, Calendly and iClosed. **Form Submitted** shows Cortana booking pages only. It is measuring how your own pages perform, so it cannot include other tools.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I export this?">
    Not from this tab today. To get the same people out, go to **Contacts**. Each contact record there holds their booking history.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does a booking show a dash for phone?">
    Two reasons are possible. Either the form never asked for a phone number, or the person left before they reached that box. Open **Responses** and click **View** to see how far they got.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
