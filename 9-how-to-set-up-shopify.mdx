# Set up Shopify

Install Cortana from the Shopify App Store and your store is tracked end to end with nothing to paste: the pixel, the order and checkout webhooks, the conversions and the Meta and TikTok event mapping are set up for you. You then answer one question, **What do you want to track?**, and you are done.

Already tracking a store the manual way (a pasted snippet, a custom pixel and hand-made webhooks)? That setup keeps working. See [Shopify: manual setup (legacy)](/9b-shopify-manual-setup) for how to move to the app at your own pace.

## Install the app

1. In the Shopify App Store, open the **Cortana** listing and click **Install**.
2. Approve the app's access to your store, then pick a plan and approve the subscription. Billing runs through Shopify.
3. Shopify sends you to Cortana. If you already have a Cortana account under the same email, you see **This email already has a Cortana account**: sign in and the store connects to your existing business, and the Shopify subscription you just approved is cancelled so you are never billed twice.
4. Cortana signs you in and opens your business with the **What do you want to track?** screen.

<Note>
  Everything below is also reachable later: **Settings** → **Integrations** → **Shopify** → **Tracking**.
</Note>

## Watch the install finish

At the top of the screen, under **Your store**, three chips show the install as it happens. They refresh on their own for up to five minutes.

1. **Installing pixel** turns into **Pixel installed** once the Cortana web pixel is on your store.
2. **Connecting webhooks** turns into **Webhooks live** once your orders and checkouts reach Cortana.
3. **Waiting for the first event** turns into **First event received** the first time a shopper triggers an event.

If a step fails, the chip turns red with the reason and a **Retry setup** button appears. Retry resumes from the failed step; nothing already set up is repeated.

## Choose what to track

Five rows, each with a switch. Switch on what counts for your store; the defaults are the right answer for most stores.

| Row | What counts | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Purchases** | Every paid order, with its value. Refunds are booked against it. | **Always on** |
| **Checkouts started** | A shopper reaches the checkout page. | On |
| **Payment details added** | A shopper enters their payment method. | On |
| **Adds to cart** | A product goes into the cart, every time. | On |
| **Product views** | A shopper opens a product page. High volume; off unless you want it. | Off |

A switch takes effect immediately. Switching a row off stops new events from being booked; nothing already tracked is deleted. The footer counts what is on: **Tracking 4 of 5 events**.

<Tip>
  A row that reads **Not set up yet; it switches on once the install finishes** is waiting on the chips above. It becomes live on its own.
</Tip>

## Send events to your ad accounts

Purchases, checkouts started, payment details and adds to cart are sent to Meta and TikTok as the matching standard events (Purchase, InitiateCheckout, AddPaymentInfo, AddToCart) once those ad accounts are connected. If one is missing, the screen says so: **Send these events to your ad accounts: connect Meta, TikTok on the Integrations page.** Connect them under **Settings** → **Integrations**.

## Boost attribution (optional)

**Boost attribution** offers a one-line theme snippet. It keeps first-touch campaign data for two years, so more orders attribute to the ad that started them. Click **Add the snippet** and follow the instructions; the store is tracked without it.

## Finish

Click **See your conversions**. Every tracked event lands in the Conversions view with its attribution, and paid orders carry their value.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What the install sets up, exactly">
    - **The pixel.** The Cortana web pixel is installed on your store through Shopify. It sends page views and the commerce events (checkout started, payment details, add to cart, product view) with Shopify's own visitor id, so the same shopper is one visitor across every event.
    - **The webhooks.** Orders and checkouts are delivered to Cortana at the app level. Cortana also keeps a mirror of your orders, customers, line items and abandoned checkouts for the E-Commerce views.
    - **The conversions.** One conversion per row on the screen, created switched on (or off, for product views). The purchase conversion is paired with a refund conversion, so a refund appears as a negative amount against the order.
    - **The ad-platform mapping.** Each conversion is mapped to its Meta and TikTok standard event.
    - **The history.** The last 60 days of orders are imported once the install finishes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How purchases and refunds are counted">
    - A purchase is booked when the order is **paid**, dated at the order's own time, once per order. A duplicate delivery from Shopify, or the same order arriving on two paths, never books twice.
    - An order that is cancelled books nothing. A fulfilment is recorded on the order but does not add a second purchase.
    - A refund is a negative conversion on the refund pair of the purchase, for the refunded amount; partial refunds add up. A refund for an order that was placed before tracking started is ignored, and one that arrives before its order is retried until the order is in.
    - An order with no email and no phone is skipped and shown as skipped, never booked to a placeholder contact.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Every setting on the screen">
    - **Purchases**: cannot be switched off. Default on.
    - **Checkouts started**: on by default. Sent to Meta as InitiateCheckout and to TikTok as InitiateCheckout. Recommendation: leave on.
    - **Payment details added**: on by default. Sent as AddPaymentInfo. Recommendation: leave on.
    - **Adds to cart**: on by default. Sent as AddToCart. Recommendation: leave on.
    - **Product views**: off by default. Sent as ViewContent. Recommendation: on only if you optimise ads on product views; it is the highest-volume event by far.
    - **Boost attribution**: optional theme snippet. Recommendation: add it if you run campaigns with long consideration windows.
    - **Retry setup**: appears only when a step failed. Resumes from that step.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where to find this again">
    **Settings** → **Integrations** → the Shopify card. The button reads **Tracking** once the install is complete, **Finish setup** while a step is still pending or failed, **Syncing...** during the history import, and **Reconnect** if the last sync failed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Managed billing: what you see in Cortana">
    A business created by buying inside the Shopify App Store sees the ad-tracking product: the Conversions and Ad Tracking views and the E-Commerce dashboard. Features that are not part of the plan you bought are not in the sidebar. Businesses that pay Cortana directly see everything they always did, whether or not a Shopify store is connected.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I installed the app but the chips still say Installing pixel">
    The install usually completes within a minute. If a chip turns red, click **Retry setup**. If it stays pending for more than five minutes, close the screen and reopen it from **Settings** → **Integrations** → **Shopify**; the state is saved, nothing restarts from zero.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does my order show up twice in Shopify but once in Cortana">
    Shopify delivers the same order on more than one topic (created, paid, updated, fulfilled). Cortana books the purchase once, on the paid delivery, and records the others on the order without adding a conversion.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do refunds show up">
    As a negative conversion on the refund pair of your purchase conversion, with the refunded amount, on the day the refund was issued. The order's own row keeps the total refunded so far.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I track product views for one product only">
    No. **Product views** is one switch for every product page. It is off by default because of its volume.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I already had Shopify set up the manual way. Do I have to redo anything">
    No. Installing the app while the old setup runs is safe: an order that reaches Cortana on both the old webhook and the app is booked once. The screen shows a **Tidy up your old setup** card listing what you can delete, and deleting it is optional, with one exception: a custom pixel pasted before July 2026 must be deleted, because product views count twice until it is. See [Shopify: manual setup (legacy)](/9b-shopify-manual-setup).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the app see my customers' details">
    Yes, for the orders and checkouts it tracks: Cortana's protected customer data access was approved by Shopify in September 2026. Details are used to attribute orders to contacts and to send the conversions you switched on to your ad accounts.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
