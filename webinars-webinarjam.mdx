## Overview

Cortana Webinars works with WebinarJam as well as Zoom. You connect your WebinarJam account once, with its API key. Then you bring in a webinar you already scheduled in WebinarJam.

Cortana then tracks each sign-up, who came live and for how long, who watched the replay, and who bought from the webinar room. You can count each of these as a conversion and send it to your ad platforms.

This page replaces [1) Connect Zoom](/webinars-connect-zoom) and [2) Create Your Webinar](/webinars-create) for WebinarJam. Pages 3 to 6 work the same for both. They say where WebinarJam is different.

<Note>
  **Before you start.** Schedule your webinar in WebinarJam first. Cortana brings in webinars that already exist there. It never creates or moves a WebinarJam webinar.
</Note>

## 1. Find your WebinarJam API key

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your webinars in WebinarJam">
    Sign in to WebinarJam and open the **Webinars** dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Advanced">
    Click **Advanced** on any webinar. The **Advanced integration** box opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the key">
    Click **API custom integration**, then copy the **API Key**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="In WebinarJam: Advanced, then API custom integration. Every value is replaced in this picture.">
  <img src="/images/webinars/webinarjam/01-find-api-key.png" alt="WebinarJam's Advanced integration box on its API custom integration tab, with the Member ID, Webinar ID, Webinar Hash and API Key fields, and a round blue button beside the API Key. Every value is replaced in this picture." />
</Frame>

One key covers your whole WebinarJam account. Every webinar in that account can come into Cortana with it.

<Warning>
  **Do not click the round blue button next to the API Key.** It makes a new key, and the old one stops working at once. Cortana then shows **Needs a new key** until you paste the new one.
</Warning>

## 2. Connect WebinarJam in Cortana

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    In Cortana, click **Settings**, then **Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the WebinarJam card">
    Find the **WebinarJam** card and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the key">
    Paste the key into **WebinarJam API key**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect WebinarJam">
    Cortana checks the key with WebinarJam straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The account now shows as **Connected**. Under it, a line says how many webinars use it. The card's button now reads **Manage**.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is my API key safe in Cortana?">
    Yes. Cortana stores the key encrypted and never shows it again. It is never sent back to your browser.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="More than one WebinarJam account">
    Click **Connect another account** and paste that account's key. Each account shows on its own row.

    One WebinarJam account can belong to one Cortana business only.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What the account states mean">
    - **Connected**: WebinarJam accepts the key.
    - **Needs a new key**: WebinarJam stopped accepting the key, usually because it was regenerated. Click **Paste a new key** and paste the current one.
    - **Disconnected**: you disconnected it. Click **Paste a new key** to bring it back.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Disconnect an account">
    Click **Disconnect** on the account's row. Cortana asks first and says how many webinars use it.

    Disconnecting deletes the key in Cortana. Sign-ups and attendance stop for its webinars until you connect it again. Their history stays.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## 3. Add your WebinarJam webinar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open New webinar">
    Click **Webinars** in the menu on the left, then **New webinar**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick WebinarJam">
    Under **Where does it run?**, click **WebinarJam**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the account">
    Under **Which WebinarJam account?**, pick the account. With one account, it is picked for you. You can also connect one here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the webinar">
    Under **Which webinar?**, pick the webinar you scheduled in WebinarJam. Each one shows its next date in its own timezone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Set webinar details">
    This takes you to the details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which webinars show in the list">
    Your scheduled WebinarJam webinars, newest first. A webinar marked **Repeats** has more than one date.

    "Right Now" and "Always On" webinars do not show. Cortana needs a scheduled date to track.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My webinar is not in the list">
    Check that it is scheduled in WebinarJam, with a date still to come. If the account has no scheduled webinar, the box says *Create a scheduled webinar in WebinarJam first*.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## 4. Choose where people register

Under **Where do people register?**, pick one answer.

### With a Cortana form or one of my connected forms

People sign up on your own form. Cortana registers each of them in WebinarJam, with their own personal join link.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the form">
    Under **What form feeds into this?**, pick the form. It can be a Cortana form, or a GoHighLevel, Typeform, Tally, HubSpot or ClickFunnels form you connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the rest">
    The rest is the same as for Zoom: how people register for a repeating webinar, and the pitch minute. See section 4 of [2) Create Your Webinar](/webinars-create#4-gate-the-form-people-sign-up-with).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add webinar">
    Cortana adds the webinar and shows **Your webinar is ready**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Where do people register? With your own form, Cortana registers each person in WebinarJam.">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/webinars/webinarjam/07-register-form.png" alt="The New webinar box asking Where do people register, with With a Cortana form or one of my connected forms picked, the form list under What form feeds into this, and the Add webinar button." />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/webinars/webinarjam/07-register-form-dark.png" alt="The New webinar box asking Where do people register, with With a Cortana form or one of my connected forms picked, the form list under What form feeds into this, and the Add webinar button." />
</Frame>

### On WebinarJam's own registration page

People sign up on WebinarJam's page. Your Cortana pixel tracks them there.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the pixel">
    Click **Copy pixel**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the webinar in WebinarJam">
    In WebinarJam, click **Edit** on the webinar. Click **Next** until you reach the **Integrations** step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on tracking">
    Next to **3rd party tracking systems**, click the pencil. Pick **Embed a 3rd party tracking token**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it five times">
    Paste the same pixel into all five boxes: **Registration page tracking**, **Registration form tracking**, **Post-registration Thank You page tracking**, **Webinar live room tracking** and **Replay page tracking**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save in WebinarJam">
    Click **Save**, then finish the webinar's steps in WebinarJam.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the pitch minute">
    Back in Cortana, type the minute your offer starts in **When does the pitch start?** Leave it empty if there is none.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add webinar">
    Cortana adds the webinar and shows **Your webinar is ready**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which way should I pick?">
    Pick your own form if you already have a sign-up page you like, or a form in another tool. Cortana then registers each person in WebinarJam itself, so you paste nothing into WebinarJam.

    Pick WebinarJam's own page if you want to keep WebinarJam's sign-up page. Then the pixel in the five boxes is how Cortana sees those people.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Cortana keep the WebinarJam room password?">
    No. WebinarJam puts a room password and private room links in its thank you page address. Cortana never stores them, from the pixel or anywhere else.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## 6. Get replays and purchases within minutes (optional)

After each session, Cortana checks WebinarJam every 6 hours for 7 days for replay views and in-room purchases. WebinarJam's **Custom Webhook** tells Cortana to check right away.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the WebinarJam box">
    In Cortana, click **Settings**, **Integrations**, then **Manage** on the **WebinarJam** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Faster replay and purchase tracking">
    Click **Faster replay and purchase tracking** under the account, then **Set up the webhook**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the webhook in WebinarJam">
    In WebinarJam, open **Profile**, **Integrations**, **Autoresponder**, and pick **Custom Webhook**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the URL">
    Copy the **Webhook URL** from Cortana and paste it as the webhook URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the token">
    For authentication, choose **Bearer token**. Copy the **Bearer token** from Cortana and paste it. Cortana shows it only once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the rules">
    In each webinar's rules in WebinarJam, send **Attends replay** and **Purchases** to this webhook.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="The webhook URL and token, with where each one goes in WebinarJam. Both are hidden here.">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/webinars/webinarjam/11-webhook.png" alt="The WebinarJam accounts box with the Faster replay and purchase tracking panel open, showing the Webhook URL and the Bearer token, each with a Copy button, in four numbered steps saying where they go in WebinarJam." />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/webinars/webinarjam/11-webhook-dark.png" alt="The WebinarJam accounts box with the Faster replay and purchase tracking panel open, showing the Webhook URL and the Bearer token, each with a Copy button, in four numbered steps saying where they go in WebinarJam." />

The account now shows **Webhook on**. To replace the token, click **Make a new webhook token**. The old token stops working.

## How WebinarJam differs from Zoom

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When attendance shows up">
    Zoom tells Cortana as people join and leave. WebinarJam does not, so Cortana asks WebinarJam once the room has certainly closed: about 4 hours after the start. WebinarJam closes a live room after 4 hours at most.

    Until then, the **Attendance** tab says *Attendance arrives once WebinarJam's room has closed, about four hours after the start.*

    Each person's time in the room comes from WebinarJam. Watched percentages use the session's real length: from the start to the last person leaving.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Replay views and in-room purchases">
    Only WebinarJam webinars have these. After a session, Cortana checks WebinarJam every 6 hours for 7 days, or within minutes with the webhook in section 6.

    - **Webinar Replay Watched**: watched any of the replay.
    - **Webinar Replay Watched 50%**: watched at least half of the replay, measured against the session's length.
    - **Webinar In-Room Purchase**: bought from the webinar room, live or on the replay.

    Each counts once per person per session. The **Registrants** tab shows *Watched the replay* and *Bought in the room*.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why the in-room purchase has no dollar value">
    WebinarJam says someone bought, not what they paid. Stripe, or your checkout, stays the record of the money. So **Webinar In-Room Purchase** carries no value, and never counts the same sale twice.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Questions, polls and chat">
    WebinarJam does not share them with Cortana. A WebinarJam webinar shows attendance but no questions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Emails">
    WebinarJam sends its own confirmation and reminder emails. Set them up in WebinarJam. Zoom's email switches do not show for a WebinarJam webinar. Cortana's own calendar invites work the same.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Meetings and past sessions">
    WebinarJam webinars only, no meetings. Cortana tracks a webinar from the moment you add it. Past sessions are not brought in.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Settings Reference

| Setting | What it does | Default | We recommend | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **WebinarJam API key** | Connects your WebinarJam account. | Empty | Copy it from **Advanced**, **API custom integration**. | One account belongs to one Cortana business. |
| **Paste a new key** | Brings back an account that needs a new key, or one you disconnected. | Not applicable | Paste the key from the same WebinarJam account. | A key from another account is refused. |
| **Disconnect** | Deletes the key in Cortana. | Not applicable | Only when you stop using the account. | Sign-ups and attendance stop for its webinars. |
| **Where does it run?** | Picks WebinarJam or Zoom for a new webinar. | Nothing picked | **WebinarJam** for this page. | None. |
| **Which WebinarJam account?** | The account the webinar lives in. | Your only account | Your main account. | Only connected accounts show. |
| **Which webinar?** | The WebinarJam webinar to bring in. | Nothing picked | One with a date still to come. | Right Now and Always On webinars do not show. |
| **What is the webinar called?** | The webinar's name in Cortana. | The name from WebinarJam | Keep it. | None. |
| **How long does it run?** | The length Cortana uses until the real end is known. | 60 minutes | The real length of your webinar. | WebinarJam does not share one. |
| **Who hosts?** | The team member whose calendar sends invites. | Nothing picked | Someone with a connected calendar. | No calendar means no calendar invites. |
| **Where do people register?** | Your own form, or WebinarJam's own page. | Nothing picked | Your own form, if you have one. | WebinarJam's page needs the pixel in five boxes. |
| **Copy pixel** | Copies your Cortana pixel for WebinarJam's boxes. | Not applicable | Paste it into all five boxes. | A box without it is a page Cortana cannot see. |
| **When does the pitch start?** | The minute your offer starts. | Empty | Set it if you pitch. | Counted from the start. |
| **Faster replay and purchase tracking** | Shows the webhook URL and token for WebinarJam. | Off | Turn it on if you use replays or room offers. | The token shows only once. |

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="It says WebinarJam refused this key">
    The message is *WebinarJam refused this key. Copy it again from WebinarJam: Webinars dashboard, Advanced, API custom integration.* Copy the whole key again, with no spaces, and paste it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says that does not look like a WebinarJam API key">
    The message is *That does not look like a WebinarJam API key.* You pasted something else. Copy the key from **API custom integration**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The account says Needs a new key">
    WebinarJam stopped accepting the key, usually because someone regenerated it. Click **Paste a new key** and paste the current key.

    Sign-ups that came in meanwhile are sent to WebinarJam once the key works again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says WebinarJam is busy">
    The messages are *WebinarJam is busy. Try again in a minute.* and *WebinarJam did not answer as expected. Try again in a minute.* Wait a minute and try again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says the account is held by a deleted workspace">
    The message is *This WebinarJam account is still held by a workspace that was deleted. Contact Cortana support to release it.* Click the support button and we will release it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A sign-up shows Failed on the Registrants tab">
    The **Registrants** tab shows WebinarJam's reason. A common one is a field WebinarJam requires that your form does not ask, such as a last name. Turn off that requirement in WebinarJam's registration settings, or add the question to your form.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Attendance is empty after my webinar">
    Attendance arrives about 4 hours after the start, once WebinarJam's room has closed. If WebinarJam lists nobody a day later, the **Attendance** tab says *WebinarJam listed nobody as attending this event within a day of it, so no attendance is recorded.*
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Cortana use OAuth for WebinarJam?">
    No. WebinarJam only offers an API key. You paste it once. Zoom uses a sign-in instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is the API key for one webinar or the whole account?">
    The whole account. One key brings in any webinar in that WebinarJam account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change the webinar's date in Cortana?">
    No. WebinarJam keeps the schedule. Change dates and repeats in WebinarJam, and Cortana follows.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to paste the pixel if people sign up with my own form?">
    No. With your own form, Cortana registers people in WebinarJam itself. The five boxes are only for WebinarJam's own registration page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does a replay view not show up right away?">
    Without the webhook, Cortana checks every 6 hours for a week after the session. Set up the webhook in section 6 to see it within minutes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I bring in my past WebinarJam webinars?">
    No. Cortana tracks a webinar from the moment you add it, the same as for Zoom.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
