## Overview

The research looks at your market for you. It finds the keywords your buyers search for, scores them, and drafts a **keyword plan**. You remove what does not fit, add your own, and approve it.

Once you approve the plan, Cortana starts tracking those keywords every day. See [3) Track Your Rankings](/seo-rank-radar).

<Note>
  **Before you start.** SEO needs your product brief. If the page says **Set up your business brain first**, go to **Settings**, **Business Info**, and click **Start Onboarding**.
</Note>

---

## 1. Run the research

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open SEO">
    Click **SEO** in the menu on the left.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the product">
    If your business has more than one product, pick one in the product menu at the top right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Run market research">
    Your first run is free. Cortana says *Research started. This first run is free.*
  </Step>
</Steps>

The card **Researching your market…** shows six steps:

1. Reading your current search footprint
2. Mapping competitor rankings
3. Mining how your market talks
4. Building the keyword universe
5. Checking each keyword fits your business
6. Scoring opportunities & drafting the plan

It takes a few minutes. You can leave the page. The research keeps running, and the page shows it when you come back.

When it finishes, the page shows two tabs: **Keyword plan**, which opens first, and **Research**.

---

## 2. Read what it found

Open the **Research** tab. **What the research found** shows:

- **How many keywords** it looked at across your market.
- **Quick wins**: the easiest keywords with the strongest buying intent, and why each one.
- **Competitor footprint**: the sites that rank in your market, and where you can beat them.
- **How your market asks**: real questions people ask, with links to where they asked them.

## 3. Edit and approve your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Remove what does not fit">
    On the **Keyword plan** tab, click the **×** at the end of a keyword's row. It moves to **Removed**, where the round arrow brings it back, or **Bring all back** brings back every one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your own">
    Type a keyword in **Search or add a keyword**. When it is not in the plan, the list's first row says **Add "your keyword" to your plan**: click it, or press Enter. Yours get a **yours** label.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve it">
    Click **Approve plan**. The button shows how many keywords you keep. Until you approve, the **Keyword plan** tab has a dot.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Cortana says *Plan approved* and how many keywords are locked in, and opens the **Movement** tab. The checks start on the next daily run, and your content calendar plans articles from the new plan straight away.

The plan shows 25 keywords at a time. For each keyword you see its topic, its intent, its monthly searches, like *1.2k*, and its difficulty, like *12*, with a green, amber or red dot. Lower difficulty means easier to rank. Click **Searches / mo** or **Difficulty** to sort by it, and pick a topic in **All topics** to see only that topic.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Run the research again">
    Open the **Research** tab and click **Run the research again**. Your approved plan keeps tracking while it runs, and the **Research** tab shows a spinner.

    When it finishes, the new plan is a draft on the **Keyword plan** tab, with a dot on the tab. Approve it to switch tracking to it. Until then, Cortana keeps tracking the plan you approved before.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Change an approved plan">
    On the **Keyword plan** tab, click the **×** on a keyword to remove it. A tracked keyword stops being checked. Add more in **Search or add a keyword**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long does the research take?">
    A few minutes. It runs on Cortana's side, so you can close the page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A research step says skipped.">
    One data source did not answer during this run. The research goes on without it, and scores those keywords carefully. Run it again later to fill the gap.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says Insufficient wallet balance.">
    Your first research run is free. Later runs are paid from your Cortana wallet. Add funds, then run it again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says A research run is already in progress.">
    One run at a time. Wait for it to finish. The page shows its progress.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I add a keyword that is not in the research?">
    Yes. On the **Keyword plan** tab, type it in **Search or add a keyword** and press Enter.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
