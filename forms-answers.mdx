## Overview

Every answer a visitor gives is saved onto that person's contact record.

You read them in the contact window, the same window you open from **Contacts**.

Each question becomes a field named after the question you asked.

<Note>
  **Before you start**

  - You need a live form. See [Sharing Your Form](/forms-share).
  - Fill your own form in once. Everything below is easier to follow when there is a real answer to look at.
  - Use a real email address you can recognise later, so you can find the contact again.
</Note>

---

## 1. What Happens When Someone Presses The Button

<Steps>
  <Step title="Cortana works out who they are">
    A form recognises a person by their email address or their phone number.

    If a contact with that email or phone already exists, the answers go onto that contact. Nobody is duplicated.

    Only when nothing matches does Cortana make a new contact.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Their answers are saved onto them">
    Every answer is written onto that contact, straight away.

    **Name**, **Email**, **Phone** and **Website** questions fill the contact's own Name, Email, Phone and Website.

    Every other question becomes a field of its own, under **Custom Fields**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The visit is counted">
    Every form gets its own tracking pixel. Cortana makes it, so the form's page views reach your ad reporting with full detail.

    You do not install it, name it or manage it. It does not appear in your list of tracking pixels.

    There is no code to paste either. The form's own page already carries it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 2. Read The Answers On The Contact

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Contacts">
    Click **Contacts** in the menu on the left of your business.

    Search for the person's email address or their name.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the contact">
    Click their row. The contact window opens.

    Their name, email and phone sit at the top, under **Contact Information**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Scroll to Custom Fields">
    **Custom Fields** is further down the left side of the window, under the line *Additional information for this contact*.

    Each question you asked is a field here, named after the question. The answer sits in the box under it.

Two form questions appear as fields, one a dropdown holding the choice the visitor picked and one a text box holding what they typed." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **The list holds every field your business has, not only this form's.**

  Fields from your booking calendars and from a connected CRM sit in the same list. A field a person has never answered is simply empty.
</Note>

<Note>
  **One person, many forms, one record.**

  If the same person fills in two different forms, both forms' answers appear on that one contact.

  Nothing is overwritten unless the same question is answered twice. Then the newer answer wins.
</Note>

## 3. How Each Kind Of Answer Looks

Most answers are shown exactly as the visitor typed them. A few are tidied up so they read well.

| Question | How the answer looks |
| --- | --- |
| **Single choice**, **Dropdown**, **Picture choice** | The choice they picked, in a list you can change. |
| **Multiple choice** | Each choice as its own chip. |
| **Matrix** | One line per row, written as `Row: choice`. |
| **Date range** | The two dates, written as `start to end`. |
| **Ranking** | The choices in the order the visitor put them. |
| **File upload** | The files they attached. |
| **Address** | The whole address on one line. |

## 4. Rename A Question And The Field Follows

The field takes its name from the question.

Rename the question in the builder and the field is renamed with it, so your answers stay together in one place.

<Warning>
  **Rename the question before you share the form, not after.**

  A question renamed once answers are in keeps its answers. But renaming is not something to do casually on a live form, because the name is what you and your team will look for.
</Warning>

## 5. Answers And Your CRM

Answers are also sent to a connected CRM as custom fields, when those fields are linked.

**GoHighLevel** and **HubSpot** both work this way.

A field has to be linked on both sides before anything is sent. Nothing is pushed to a CRM field that is not linked.

## 6. Count Form Answers As Conversions

You can tell Cortana that finishing your form counts as a conversion. Then it shows up in your ad reports next to everything else.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Conversion Setup">
    Go to **Ad Tracking** in the menu on the left, then the **Conversion Setup** tab.

    You'll see your list of conversion events. Each row is one thing you count, such as a lead or a purchase.

    <Frame caption="Conversion Setup. Each row is one thing you count in your ad reports.">
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/answers/02-conversion-setup.png" alt="The Ad Tracking area with the Conversion Setup tab highlighted, showing a table of conversion events with their status, name, recent activity, last event and number of sources." />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/answers/02-conversion-setup-dark.png" alt="The Ad Tracking area with the Conversion Setup tab highlighted, showing a table of conversion events with their status, name, recent activity, last event and number of sources." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a source to a conversion event">
    Click the name of the conversion event you want your form to feed. Use **Add Custom Event** first if you need a new one.

    In the box that opens, scroll to **Sources** and click **Add Source**.

    <Frame caption="A conversion event holds sources. A source is one place its conversions come from.">
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/answers/03-add-source.png" alt="The Edit Conversion box with the Add Source button highlighted in its Sources section, above a line reading no sources created yet." />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/answers/03-add-source-dark.png" alt="The Edit Conversion box with the Add Source button highlighted in its Sources section, above a line reading no sources created yet." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Cortana Forms">
    In **Create Conversion Source**, open the **Integration** list and pick **Cortana Forms**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick the event and the forms">
    **What counts as a conversion?** offers two things.

    - **Partial Submission**, described as *started, never finished*.
    - **Form Submission**, described as *completed and sent*.

    Each source tracks one of them. Make a second conversion event if you want both.

    Then tick the forms this event should count under **Which forms to track?**. A form that is still off is marked **draft**, and counts nothing until you turn it on.

    Click **Submit**.

    <Frame caption="Pick what counts, then tick the forms. Only ticked forms feed this event.">
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/answers/04-cortana-form-source.png" alt="The Create Conversion Source box with Cortana Forms chosen as the integration, Form Submission chosen as the event, and the Which forms to track list highlighted, showing tickboxes beside each form name with draft labels on the ones that are off." />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/answers/04-cortana-form-source-dark.png" alt="The Create Conversion Source box with Cortana Forms chosen as the integration, Form Submission chosen as the event, and the Which forms to track list highlighted, showing tickboxes beside each form name with draft labels on the ones that are off." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 7. What A Payment Step Does

A **Payment** question charges the card inside the form, on your own Stripe or Whop account. Cortana takes 0%.

The money goes to your own account. So you see it in your Stripe or Whop dashboard, like any other charge.

## 8. What A Signature Step Does

A **Signature** question keeps more than the drawing.

Cortana stores four things. The picture of the signature. The wording you set under **What are they agreeing to?**. The date. And a fingerprint of the exact questions and answers that were on screen.

That fingerprint cannot be changed afterwards, and neither can the record. It exists so "I never agreed to that" can be answered.

## Settings Reference

The controls that decide what happens to an answer. None of them live in the form builder.

| Setting | Where it lives | What it does | Default | We recommend | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Custom Fields** | The contact window, below **Location** | Holds every answer that is not the person's name, email, phone or website. | Empty until someone answers | Read them here. This is where a form's answers live. | The list shows every field your business has, so fields nobody has answered look empty. |
| **Cortana Forms** source | Ad Tracking → Conversion Setup → a conversion → **Add Source** | Makes form answers count towards that conversion event. | Not set up | Set one up for **Form Submission** on the forms that really mean a lead. | Only ticked forms count. |
| **What counts as a conversion?** | The same box | **Partial Submission** *(started, never finished)* or **Form Submission** *(completed and sent)*. | Nothing chosen | **Form Submission** unless you are chasing people who gave up. | One source tracks one of them. Make a second conversion event for the other. |
| **Which forms to track?** | The same box | The forms this event counts. | None ticked | Tick only the forms that really mean a lead. | A form marked **draft** is off, so it can never fire. |
| **Form pixel** | Nothing to open. It is kept out of your tracking pixel list | Counts visits to the form's page and ties them to the ad they came from. | Made for you, with the form | Nothing to do. It looks after itself. | There is nothing to copy, rename or install, and no snippet to paste. |
| **Scheduler question** | The form builder, **Add a question → Scheduler** | Puts a booking calendar inside the form. Booking there also makes an appointment. | Not on a form | Add one when you want the person to pick a time as well. | The booking page brings its own questions. Yours are separate from them. |
| **Payment question** | The form builder, **Add a question → Payment** | Charges the card inside the form, on your own account. | Not on a form | Only Stripe and Whop. Nothing else can be used. | The money lands in your own account, so check it there. |

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I see the answers people gave?">
    On the person's contact. Open **Contacts**, find them, and scroll the contact window to **Custom Fields**.

    There is no separate list of submissions, and no export.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Nobody appeared in my Contacts after a submission">
    A form only knows who someone is from an email address or a phone number.

    Check your form actually asks for one. Add an **Email** question if it does not.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I expected a new contact and it went onto an old one">
    That is on purpose. The email or phone matched a contact you already had, so the answers went there.

    Cortana would rather add to the person you know than make a second copy of them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A question is missing from Custom Fields">
    Two things to check.

    1. **Nobody answered it.** An unanswered question has nothing to store.
    2. **It cannot be answered.** Text blocks, images, videos and dividers hold no answer, so they get no field.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I renamed a question and cannot find the old field">
    The field was renamed with the question. Look for the new name.

    The answers already collected are still there under it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot see any numbers for my form">
    Forms itself shows none. No submission count, no conversion rate and no funnel for a single form.

    Its page views do reach your ad reporting, through a pixel Cortana runs for you. Nothing in Forms adds them up for you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Which forms to track list shows my form as draft">
    The form is off. Turn it on from its card in the Forms list, or from the switch at the top of the builder.

    A form that is off has no page, so nothing can ever be submitted on it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I connected Fanbasis or PayPal but Payment will not offer it">
    Forms can only take money through **Stripe** and **Whop**.

    Any other processor you have connected is used elsewhere in Cortana, but not by a form.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Payment question says no processor is connected">
    The business has no connected Stripe or Whop account.

    Connect one in **Settings**, then reopen the question. Only accounts that are really connected are offered, so a half-finished setup will not show.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where exactly do the answers end up?">
    On the person's contact record, under **Custom Fields**.

    **Name**, **Email**, **Phone** and **Website** questions fill the contact's own fields instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if the same person fills in two of my forms?">
    Both forms' answers appear on that one contact.

    They are matched by their email or their phone, so you get one person, not two.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get an email when someone fills my form in?">
    No. A form sends nothing to you.

    A **Scheduler** step does send a confirmation email, but to the person who booked, not to you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I export my answers to a spreadsheet?">
    No. There is no export in Forms.

    The answers are on each contact, so anything that reads your contacts reads them too.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do answers reach my CRM?">
    Yes, as custom fields, when the field is linked on both sides.

    **GoHighLevel** and **HubSpot** both work this way.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the form know which ad someone came from?">
    Yes. The form's page carries its own pixel, and the website code carries your ad link's tracking tags across.

    So the visit is attributed to the ad that brought them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is a partial submission?">
    Someone who started answering and never pressed the button at the end.

    You can count those as their own conversion event, separately from finished ones.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does a payment through a form cost me anything extra?">
    No. The builder says *Charged on your own Stripe account. Cortana takes 0%.*

    Your processor's own fees still apply, as they would anywhere else.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
