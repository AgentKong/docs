Every answer a visitor gives is saved onto that person's contact record. You read them in the contact window, the same window you open from **Contacts**.

Each question becomes a field named after the question you asked. There is no separate submissions table and no per-form analytics.

<Note>
  **Before you start.** You need a live form. See [Share your form](/forms-share).

  Fill your own form in once, using a real email address you can recognise later. Everything below is easier to follow when there is a real answer to look at.
</Note>

## 1. What happens when someone presses the button

**Cortana works out who they are.** A form recognises a person by their email address or their phone number. If a contact with that email or phone already exists, the answers go onto that contact, so nobody is duplicated. Only when nothing matches does Cortana make a new contact.

**Their answers are saved onto them.** Every answer is written onto that contact straight away. **Name**, **Email**, **Phone** and **Website** questions fill the contact's own fields. Every other question becomes a field of its own, under **Custom Fields**.

**The visit is counted.** Every form gets its own tracking pixel, which Cortana makes for you, so the form's page views reach your ad reporting with full detail. You do not install it, name it or manage it, and it does not appear in your list of tracking pixels. There is no code to paste either: the form's own page already carries it.

## 2. Read the answers on the contact

Click **Contacts** in the menu on the left of your business, and search for the person's email address or their name. Click their row and the contact window opens, with their name, email and phone at the top under **Contact Information**.

**Custom Fields** is further down the left side of the window, under the line *Additional information for this contact*. Each question you asked is a field here, named after the question, with the answer in the box under it.

<Note>
  **The list holds every field your business has, not only this form's.**

  Fields from your booking calendars and from a connected CRM sit in the same list, and a field a person has never answered is simply empty.

  If the same person fills in two different forms, both forms' answers appear on that one contact. Nothing is overwritten unless the same question is answered twice, and then the newer answer wins.
</Note>

### How each kind of answer looks

Most answers are shown exactly as the visitor typed them. A few are tidied up so they read well.

| Question | How the answer looks |
| --- | --- |
| **Single choice**, **Dropdown**, **Picture choice** | The choice they picked, in a list you can change. |
| **Multiple choice** | Each choice as its own chip. |
| **Matrix** | One line per row, written as `Row: choice`. |
| **Date range** | The two dates, written as `start to end`. |
| **Ranking** | The choices in the order the visitor put them. |
| **File upload** | The files they attached. |
| **Address** | The whole address on one line. |

The field takes its name from the question. Rename the question in the builder and the field is renamed with it, so your answers stay together in one place. Rename before you share the form rather than after, because the name is what you and your team will look for.

## 3. Count form answers as conversions

You can tell Cortana that finishing your form counts as a conversion. Then it shows up in your ad reports next to everything else.

### Open Conversion Setup

Go to **Ad Tracking** in the menu on the left, then the **Conversion Setup** tab. You see your list of conversion events. Each row is one thing you count, such as a lead or a purchase.

### Add a source to a conversion event

Click the name of the conversion event you want your form to feed. Use **Add Custom Event** first if you need a new one. In the box that opens, scroll to **Sources** and click **Add Source**.

### Choose Cortana Forms

In **Create Conversion Source**, open the **Integration** list and pick **Cortana Forms**.

Three Cortana sources sit in that list, and it is worth knowing which is which.

| Integration | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Cortana Forms** | A form was started or sent. Any form: one at its own address, or the form a booking calendar shows. |
| **Cortana Calendars** | Someone booked a time on a booking calendar. |
| **Cortana CRM** | A deal moved in your pipeline. |

### Pick the event

**What counts as a conversion?** is required. Its placeholder reads *Choose an event to track*, and it offers two things.

- **Partial submission**, described as *started, never finished*.
- **Form submission**, described as *completed and sent*.

Under it sits the line *Each source tracks one event. Create a separate conversion for the other stage. A booking is its own event, under Cortana Calendars.*

### Pick the forms

**Which forms to track?** appears once you have chosen an event, with the line *Your forms and the forms on your booking calendars. Only the selected ones count toward this event.*

The list is in two groups.

- **Forms**: your standalone forms, each with its own address. One that is off carries a **draft** pill, and counts nothing until you turn it on.
- **Booking calendar forms**: the form each booking calendar shows. One that is switched off carries an **inactive** pill.

Tick what you want, then click **Submit**. With nothing to pick from you see *You have no forms yet. Create a form or a booking calendar first, then come back and track it.*

Your new source is named after what you picked, such as `Form: Form submission (Contact Us)`. Pick a booking calendar as well and Cortana writes the rows it needs, without you having to know there are two kinds.

<Note>
  **Booking calendar forms are counted here, not under Cortana Calendars.**

  **Cortana Calendars** counts one thing only: **Appointment booked**, *someone books a time on a calendar*. Its own panel says *To count a calendar's form being started or sent, create a Cortana Forms source instead.*

  Pick **Partial submission** with a booking calendar ticked and two extra controls appear: a checklist of the fields that must be filled for a partial to count, and a **Lead quality** filter read from that calendar's own questions.
</Note>

## 4. Answers and your CRM

Answers are also sent to a connected CRM as custom fields, when those fields are linked. **GoHighLevel** and **HubSpot** both work this way.

A field has to be linked on both sides before anything is sent. Nothing is pushed to a CRM field that is not linked.

## 5. What the Payment and Signature steps do

A **Payment** question charges the card inside the form, on your own Stripe or Whop account. Cortana takes 0%. The money goes to your own account, so you see it in your Stripe or Whop dashboard like any other charge.

A **Signature** question keeps more than the drawing. Cortana stores four things: the picture of the signature, the wording you set under **What are they agreeing to?**, the date, and a fingerprint of the exact questions and answers that were on screen. That fingerprint cannot be changed afterwards, and neither can the record. It exists so "I never agreed to that" can be answered.

  <Accordion title="Troubleshooting">
    **Where do I see the answers people gave?** On the person's contact. Open **Contacts**, find them, and scroll the contact window to **Custom Fields**. There is no separate list of submissions, and no export.

    **Nobody appeared in my Contacts after a submission.** A form only knows who someone is from an email address or a phone number. Check your form actually asks for one.

    **I expected a new contact and it went onto an old one.** That is on purpose. The email or phone matched a contact you already had, so the answers went there.

    **A question is missing from Custom Fields.** Either nobody answered it, or it cannot be answered. Text blocks, images, videos and dividers hold no answer, so they get no field.

    **I renamed a question and cannot find the old field.** The field was renamed with the question. Look for the new name. The answers already collected are still there under it.

    **Where do I see how my form is performing?** In your ad reporting. The form's page views go there through a pixel Cortana runs for you, and finished forms count there too once you set up a **Cortana Forms** conversion source.

    **My form shows as draft in the tracking list.** The form is off. Turn it on from its card in the Forms list, or from the switch at the top of the builder.

    **My booking calendar shows as inactive in the tracking list.** That calendar is switched off, so nothing can be submitted on it. Turn it back on in the Appointments area.

    **The Payment question says no processor is connected.** The business has no connected Stripe or Whop account. Connect one in **Settings**, then reopen the question. Only accounts that are really connected are offered.
  </Accordion>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where exactly do the answers end up?">
    On the person's contact record, under **Custom Fields**. **Name**, **Email**, **Phone** and **Website** questions fill the contact's own fields instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if the same person fills in two of my forms?">
    Both forms' answers appear on that one contact. They are matched by their email or their phone, so you get one person, not two.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I spot new answers?">
    Open **Contacts** and sort by **Created**. Everyone who filled your form in is there, newest first. A **Scheduler** step also sends a confirmation email to the person who booked.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get my answers out of Cortana?">
    They live on each contact, so anything that reads your contacts reads them too. That includes your connected CRM, which gets them as custom fields.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is a partial submission?">
    Someone who started answering and never pressed the button at the end. You can count those as their own conversion event, separately from finished ones.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I count the form on my booking calendar too?">
    Yes, in the same place. **Cortana Forms** lists your booking calendar forms under their own heading, alongside your standalone forms.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does Cortana charge on a payment?">
    Nothing. The builder says *Charged on your own Stripe account. Cortana takes 0%.* Your processor's own fees still apply, as they would anywhere else.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
