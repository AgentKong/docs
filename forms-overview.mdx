## What Cortana Forms Does

**Forms** is where you build the forms people fill in.

A form asks questions. Someone answers them and presses a button. That is it.

Every form you build here can have its own web page. You can also paste it into your own website.

You'll find it in the menu on the left of your business, under **Appointments**.

By the end of these pages you will have a live link. It looks like `https://app.usecortana.ai/f/your-business-id/contact-us`.

## The Two Screens

Forms has two screens and nothing else. Everything else is a box that opens on top of them.

| Screen | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **The list** | Every form in the business, one card each. Turn a form on or off. Add one, copy one, share one, or put one away. |
| **The builder** | Where you write the questions, pick the look, and set what people see after they press the button. It saves by itself. |

---

## What A Form Can Do

- **Ask 31 kinds of question.** Text, choices, star ratings, dates, file uploads and more. See [Building Your Form](/forms-build).
- **Split questions over pages.** People answer a few at a time instead of all at once.
- **Show a question only sometimes.** Ask about budget only when someone picked "Sales question".
- **Take a payment.** Card details appear inside the form, on your own Stripe account.
- **Let people book a call.** One of your booking calendars can sit inside the form.
- **Change the ending.** Show a message, or send people to a page of your own.

---

## What Forms Does Not Do Yet

We would rather tell you now than let you look for these.

- There is **no list of answers** in Cortana. Nothing in the app shows you what people typed. See [Where Your Answers Go](/forms-answers).
- **Forms shows no numbers of its own.** No views, no submission count and no conversion rate on a form's card. Page views are still tracked behind the scenes.
- There is **no delete**. You archive a form instead, and archiving cannot be undone from the app.
- There are **no ready-made forms** to start from. You start blank or you import.
