Forms is where you build the forms people fill in. A form asks questions, someone answers them and presses a button.

Every form can have its own web page, and you can paste the same form into your own website. Either way Cortana ties each answer to the ad that brought that person in, and files it on their contact record.

## The two screens

Forms has two screens. Everything else opens on top of them.

| Screen | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **The list** | Every form in the business, one card each. Turn a form on or off. Add one, copy one, share one, or put one away. |
| **The builder** | Where you write the questions, pick the look, and set what people see after they press the button. It saves by itself. |

## What a form can do

- **Ask 31 kinds of question.** Text, choices, star ratings, dates, file uploads and more.
- **Split questions over pages.** People answer a few at a time instead of all at once.
- **Show a question only sometimes.** Ask about budget only when someone picked "Sales question".
- **Take a payment.** Card details appear inside the form, on your own Stripe or Whop account.
- **Let people book a call.** One of your booking calendars can sit inside the form.
- **Look like your brand.** Pick the colours, the font and the size of the writing.
- **Change the ending.** Show a message, or send people to a page of your own.
- **Save every answer onto the person.** Answers land on their contact record, under **Custom Fields**.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where is Forms in the menu?">
    Click **Forms** in the menu on the left of your business. It sits under **Appointments**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a domain or a paid plan?">
    No. Forms has no plans, tiers or quotas. Every form gets a web page on Cortana, and you do not need a domain to use it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many forms can I have?">
    As many as you like. There is no limit, and no limit on questions either.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do the answers end up?">
    On each person's contact record, under **Custom Fields**. There is no separate submissions table. [Where your answers go](/forms-answers) shows you how to read them.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
