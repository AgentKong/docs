    # Editing Conversion Attribution

    Alright, so if you ever need to manually change the attribution for a conversion event, we've made it easy to do that from two different spots. Here's how you can get it done.

    ## From the Main Table

    If you're looking at your conversion events—like a Purchase—you can edit the attribution directly from the main list.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Conversion Modal">
        Click into the event type you want to view (e.g., Purchase).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Locate the Attribution Column">
        Find the specific conversion you want to change and click the pencil icon next to the attribution label.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Update Attribution Details">
        You have two ways to do this. **Top-Down:** Select a specific Ad — if you know the ad, selecting it will automatically resolve the Medium, Campaign, and Source for you. **Bottom-Up:** If you don't know the specific ad, start at the Source and work your way up to manually set the campaign and medium.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Save Changes">
        Once you're happy with the changes, just hit **Save**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    ## From the Webhook Data View

    There's also another spot where you can make these changes if you're already deep-diving into the data.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="View Data">
        Click on the **View Data** link on the far right of any conversion row.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Edit Attribution Data">
        In the **Webhook Data Details** window, find the **Attribution Data** section and click **Edit**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Adjust and Save">
        This gives you the exact same ability to select an ad or manually adjust the source and campaign. Just click **Save** when you're finished.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
      **Pro Tip:** Selecting the specific Ad is the fastest way to edit attribution, as it auto-fills all the other tracking parameters for you!
    </Note>

    Hopefully, that makes sense! If you have any questions as you're working through this, please feel free to reach out to our team.

    # Setting Up Lead Filtering & Qualification

    Learn how to filter and qualify lead conversions inside Cortana using integration forms and surveys. This allows you to differentiate between a standard lead and a highly qualified lead for better reporting and ad optimization.

    ## Overview

    By default, Cortana tracks every form submission or appointment booking as a standard conversion. However, by enabling **Lead Filtering / Qualification**, you can set custom rules based on form responses (like budget, MRR, or business type) to isolate your highest-value leads.

    > ⚠️ **Best Practice:** Never completely filter out unqualified leads without tracking them elsewhere. Instead, use a **Dual-Event Setup** to track all leads under one event and only qualified leads under a second event. This preserves your baseline data while giving you deeper insights into lead quality.

    ## Step 1: Create the "All Leads" Tracked Event

    Before setting up your qualification filters, create a baseline event to capture every single lead submission.

    1. Navigate to **Conversion Setup** \> **Conversion Events**.
    2. Click **\+ Add Custom Event**.
    3. Set the **Display Name** to something recognizable, such as `Lead (All)`.
    4. Click **Create Event**.
    5. Inside the newly created event, click **\+ Add Source**.
    6. Select your preferred **Integration** (e.g., `GoHighLevel`, `Tally Forms`, or `Typeform`).
    7. Select the **Event Type** (e.g., `Form Submission`, `Survey Submission`, or `Appointment Booked`).
    8. Choose the specific form or calendar you want to track from the dropdown list.
    9. Click **Submit** and then **Save Changes**.

    ## Step 2: Create the "Qualified Leads" Event & Apply Filters

    Now, create the companion event that will filter out the low-value leads based on their form responses.

    1. Click **\+ Add Custom Event** again.
    2. Set the **Display Name** to `Lead (Qualified)`. Click **Create Event**.
    3. Inside this event, click **\+ Add Source** and select the **exact same integration, event type, and form/calendar** used in Step 1.
    4. Toggle on **Enable Qualification Filter** (or **Lead Filtering / Qualification**).
    5. Choose your rule logic:
       - **ALL:** The lead must meet every single condition to qualify.
       - **ANY:** The lead qualifies if they meet at least one of the conditions.
    6. Click **\+ Add Rule** to build your qualification criteria:
       - Cortana automatically pulls custom fields from your connected form.
       - Select a field (e.g., `Current MRR`), choose a condition (e.g., `equals`), and select or type the qualifying value (e.g., `$20k-40k/Mo`, `$40k-60k/Mo`, `$60k+/Mo`).
    7. Leave unselected any answers that represent unqualified leads (e.g., `$0-20k/Mo`). Leads who select these answers will be completely filtered out of this specific event.
    8. Click **Submit** and then **Save Changes**.

    ## Step 3: Map Events to Meta CAPI for AI Ad Optimization

    To supercharge your ad platforms, send both events back to the Meta Conversions API (CAPI) with different target values. This teaches the Meta algorithm to hunt for higher-quality leads.

    1. Navigate to the **AI Ad Optimization** tab.
    2. Click **Manage** under your Meta Conversions API setup.
    3. Go to **Mappings** and find your tracking events:
       - **Map the standard booking/lead event** (e.g., `Appointment Booked`) to the standard Meta event type `Schedule`. Assign it a baseline value (e.g., `$50.00`).
       - **Map your qualified event** (e.g., `Appointment Qualified`) to a custom Meta event type like `qualified Appointment`. Assign it a significantly higher value (e.g., `$150.00`).
    4. Save your mappings.
