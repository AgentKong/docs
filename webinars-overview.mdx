Webinars runs your Zoom webinars and Zoom meetings, or your WebinarJam webinars, through Cortana. People sign up with a form. Each of them gets a personal join link.

Cortana then sees who joined, how long they stayed, and how much they watched. You can count all of it as conversions, and send it to your ad platforms.

## The journey in five lines

1. **Connect Zoom or WebinarJam** once.
2. **Make a webinar**: a name, a host and a sign-up form. With Zoom you also pick the time. With WebinarJam you pick a webinar you already scheduled there.
3. **Share its own link**. People sign up and get their personal join link.
4. **Run it in Zoom or WebinarJam**. Cortana sees who joined and how long they stayed.
5. **Count it**. Pick which steps count as conversions for your ads.

## Zoom or WebinarJam?

**New webinar** asks first: **Where does it run?** Pick the one you host your webinars on.

- **Zoom**: Cortana makes the webinar or meeting in Zoom for you, or uses one you already made. Connect it with your own Zoom app.
- **WebinarJam**: you schedule the webinar in WebinarJam, and Cortana brings it in. Connect it with your WebinarJam API key. WebinarJam also gives you replay views and in-room purchases.

Sign-ups, attendance and conversions work the same for both. [Use WebinarJam](/webinars-webinarjam) lists every difference.

## Webinar or meeting?

This is for Zoom only. In Cortana, both work the same: sign-ups, attendance and conversions.

The difference is in Zoom. A Zoom webinar needs the Webinars plan on your Zoom account. A Zoom meeting does not. If your Zoom account has no Webinars plan, pick **Meeting**.

## What Cortana records

- **Each person who signed up.** Name, email, which event, when, and where they came from.
- **Their personal join link.** So Cortana knows who each person is when they join.
- **Each person who joined.** Total time in the room and how much they watched. With Zoom, every join and leave too.
- **Whether they saw your pitch.** If you set a pitch minute.
- **Each event.** How many people came, the most at one time, the show rate, and who stayed minute by minute.
- **Replays and purchases, with WebinarJam.** Who watched the replay, and who bought from the webinar room.

[4) After Your Webinar](/webinars-after) lists everything in full.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where is Webinars in the menu?">
    Click **Webinars** in the menu on the left of your business. It sits right after **Forms**, with a megaphone icon.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need a paid Cortana plan for Webinars?">
    No. Webinars has no plan of its own in Cortana. You need a Zoom or a WebinarJam account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Cortana host the webinar?">
    No. Your webinar runs in Zoom or WebinarJam, as usual. Cortana handles the sign-ups and the tracking around it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Cortana Webinars without Zoom?">
    Yes, with WebinarJam. See [Use WebinarJam](/webinars-webinarjam). Other webinar tools are not supported yet.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Zoom and WebinarJam in the same business?">
    Yes. You pick Zoom or WebinarJam for each new webinar.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Cortana record or replay my webinar?">
    No. Cortana does not bring in recordings, transcripts, chat or questions. With WebinarJam, it does track who watched WebinarJam's own replay.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
