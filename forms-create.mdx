There are two ways to make a form. Start blank, or bring one over from another tool. Both land you in the same place: the builder, with your form open and ready to edit.

This page also covers the other things you do to a whole form. Copy it, and put it away when you are finished with it.

<Note>
  **Before you start.** You need a business you are signed in to, and nothing else. No domain, no pixel, no paid plan.

  Bringing a form over from another tool? You need that tool's own copy of the form, as text. Section 3 explains where to get it.
</Note>

## 1. Open Forms

Click **Forms** in the menu on the left of your business. It sits under **Appointments**.

The page opens with the heading **Forms** and the line *Turn a form on or off, or add a new one*.

### What each card tells you

One card is one form. It shows four things.

- **The name**, at the top. Click it to open the builder.
- **The on and off switch**, on the right of the name. Green means the form is live.
- **The counts**: how many questions it has, how many booking calendars use it, and the day it was last changed.
- **The ending of its web address**, such as `/f/contact-us`.

A form that is off looks faded, and has no web address line. An off form has no page to open.

<Frame caption="One form card. The web address line only appears once the form is live.">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/create/02-form-card.png" alt="A single form card highlighted in the Forms list, showing the form name, a green on and off switch, the question count, the day it was last updated and the ending of its web address, with Edit and an actions button along the bottom." />

The count is questions, not answers. Answers are saved onto each person's contact, and [Where your answers go](/forms-answers) shows you where to read them.

## 2. Add a blank form

**Add form** sits at the top right of the Forms list, next to **Import**. There is no dialog and nothing to fill in. The form is made the moment you click.

<Frame caption="Add form makes the form straight away. There is nothing to confirm.">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/create/03-add-form.png" alt="The top of the Forms list with the Add form button highlighted at the top right, beside the Import button." />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/create/03-add-form-dark.png" alt="The top of the Forms list with the Add form button highlighted at the top right, beside the Import button." />
</Frame>

Cortana opens the builder for you. Your new form is called **Untitled form** and it is **Off**.

The name sits at the top left, in big text. Type over it. Your name is stored when you click somewhere else, or when you press **Enter**.

<Frame caption="A brand new form. The big name at the top left is a box you can type in.">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/create/04-untitled-form.png" alt="The form builder for a brand new form, with the form name box reading Untitled form highlighted at the top left. The header also shows Off, Share and Saved, and the middle column reads Add a question and it appears here." />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/create/04-untitled-form-dark.png" alt="The form builder for a brand new form, with the form name box reading Untitled form highlighted at the top left. The header also shows Off, Share and Saved, and the middle column reads Add a question and it appears here." />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  **Name it properly now, because the name sets the web address.**

  The first real name you give a form becomes the ending of its link. `Contact Us` becomes `/f/contact-us`.

  That happens once, while the form is still called **Untitled form** and still off. After that the address is fixed: rename the form as often as you like and every link you already sent out keeps working.

  A new form is off, so nobody can reach it yet. [Share your form](/forms-share) turns it on.
</Warning>

## 3. Bring a form over from another tool

Cortana can rebuild a form you already have somewhere else. Four tools are supported and no others.

### Click Import

**Import** sits at the top right of the Forms list, to the left of **Add form**.

A box opens, headed **Import a form**, with the line *Bring a form over from another platform. It arrives as a draft so you can check it first.* Three step names sit under it: **Platform**, **Paste** and **Review**.

<Frame caption="Import is beside Add form. It walks you through three steps.">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/create/05-import-button.png" alt="The top of the Forms list with the Import button highlighted at the top right, to the left of the Add form button." />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/create/05-import-button-dark.png" alt="The top of the Forms list with the Import button highlighted at the top right, to the left of the Add form button." />
</Frame>

### Pick the tool

Four tools are offered. Each tile carries a short line of its own on screen.

| Tool | Where the wizard gets your form |
| --- | --- |
| **Typeform** | Their own copy of the form, from `api.typeform.com/forms/{id}`. |
| **Tally** | Their own copy of the form, from `api.tally.so/forms/{id}`. |
| **HubSpot** | The form definition, from the HubSpot Forms API. |
| **GoHighLevel** | Answers people have already sent you. The wizard works the questions out from those, rather than from the form itself. |

Click one and Cortana moves you to **Paste**.

<Frame caption="The four tools Cortana can read a form from. There are no others.">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/create/06-import-platform.png" alt="The Import a form box on its Platform step, showing Typeform, Tally, HubSpot and GoHighLevel as four tiles, with the Typeform tile highlighted. Each tile carries a line saying where that tool's copy of the form comes from." />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/create/06-import-platform-dark.png" alt="The Import a form box on its Platform step, showing Typeform, Tally, HubSpot and GoHighLevel as four tiles, with the Typeform tile highlighted. Each tile carries a line saying where that tool's copy of the form comes from." />
</Frame>

### Paste the form

The box now reads **Paste the form definition**. This is the one hard part. Cortana does not connect to the other tool for you, so you paste the other tool's own copy of the form, as text.

That text is called JSON. You get it by asking the other tool's API, which is the technical door into that tool. Whoever set it up for you can fetch it in a minute.

Paste the whole thing, including the outer `{` and `}`. Then click **Review it**.

<Frame caption="Paste the other tool's own copy of the form here. Nothing is stored yet.">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/create/07-import-paste.png" alt="The Import a form box on its Paste step, with the large text area highlighted. Above it the label reads Paste the form definition and below sit a Back button and a Review it button." />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/create/07-import-paste-dark.png" alt="The Import a form box on its Paste step, with the large text area highlighted. Above it the label reads Paste the form definition and below sit a Back button and a Review it button." />
</Frame>

Paste something that is not JSON and you see **That is not valid JSON. Copy the whole response, including the outer &#123; &#125;.**

### Read the warnings

Cortana shows you what it understood. Nothing has been stored yet.

You get a **Name** box, a question count, and every question in a list with its kind. Above the list sits **Worth checking**, in orange. Read it. It is the reason this step exists.

A warning tells you something did not come across perfectly. A question we do not have becomes a plain text box. Branching rules are never brought over.

<Frame caption="Worth checking lists everything that did not come across perfectly.">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/create/08-import-review.png" alt="The Import a form box on its Review step, with the orange Worth checking panel highlighted. It lists three warnings, and below it every imported question appears with its kind and whether it is required." />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/create/08-import-review-dark.png" alt="The Import a form box on its Review step, with the orange Worth checking panel highlighted. It lists three warnings, and below it every imported question appears with its kind and whether it is required." />
</Frame>

Change the **Name** if you want, then click **Import as a draft**. A draft simply means a form that is off.

### Open it and check it

You see how many questions were brought over, and the line *It is saved as a draft called "your form name". Open it to check the questions and publish when you are happy.*

Click **Open the form**. An imported form is always off. Read every question before you switch it on.

<Frame caption="The form is made, and it is off. Open it and read every question.">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/create/09-import-done.png" alt="The Import a form box showing a green tick, the line six questions imported, a note that the form is saved as a draft, and the Open the form button highlighted." />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/create/09-import-done-dark.png" alt="The Import a form box showing a green tick, the line six questions imported, a note that the form is saved as a draft, and the Open the form button highlighted." />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  **A GoHighLevel form is a guess, not a copy.**

  The wizard builds it from answers people have already sent you, not from the form itself. A question nobody has answered yet will be missing, and a question with a small set of answers may come across as free text.

  Check a GoHighLevel import question by question, against the real form.
</Warning>

<Note>
  **The wording on the GoHighLevel tile is out of date.**

  On screen it reads *"Rebuilt from your submissions, since GHL exposes no form schema"*. A schema is the form's design.

  The first half is right. The reason is not. GoHighLevel does hand out a form's design. This wizard just does not read it.
</Note>

## 4. Copy a form

On the card, click the button with the three dots, to the right of **Edit**. Then click **Duplicate**.

The copy appears straight away, named after the first with **(copy)** on the end. It is always off, even when the form you copied is live. There is no name box and nothing to confirm.

<Frame caption="Duplicate copies the whole form, its questions and its look. The copy is off.">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/create/10-duplicate.png" alt="A form card's actions menu open on the Forms list, showing Share, Open form, Duplicate and Archive, with Duplicate highlighted." />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/create/10-duplicate-dark.png" alt="A form card's actions menu open on the Forms list, showing Share, Open form, Duplicate and Archive, with Duplicate highlighted." />
</Frame>

<Tip>
  **Copy a form before you change a live one.**

  A live form has no draft and no history. Every edit you make is on the page people are filling in right now.

  So if you want to try something, copy the form first and change the copy.
</Tip>

## 5. Put a form away

Click the three dots on the card, then **Archive**. The form disappears from the list at once. There is no "are you sure".

<Frame caption="Archive is the only way to remove a form. There is no delete.">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/create/11-archive.png" alt="A form card's actions menu open on the Forms list, showing Share, Open form, Duplicate and Archive, with Archive highlighted in red at the bottom." />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/create/11-archive-dark.png" alt="A form card's actions menu open on the Forms list, showing Share, Open form, Duplicate and Archive, with Archive highlighted in red at the bottom." />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  **Archive only when you are finished with a form.**

  Once a form leaves the list you cannot bring it back yourself. Turn the form **off** instead if you only want to stop people filling it in: an off form stays on the list and switches back on any time.

  If you archive something by mistake, message support before you rebuild it.
</Warning>

If a booking calendar still uses the form, you see **This form is still used by 1 booking calendar. Archiving it here would not stop those calendars from showing it, unlink it there first.** Go to that booking calendar's **Questions** tab, point it at a different form, then come back and archive.

  <Accordion title="Troubleshooting">
    **I clicked Add form and nothing seemed to happen.** It did happen. Cortana takes you straight into the builder for the new form, and the name at the top left reads **Untitled form**.

    **The import says That is not valid JSON.** You pasted something the other tool did not give you, or only part of it. Copy the whole response, from the first `{` to the last `}`. Do not paste the web address of your form.

    **My imported form is missing questions.** **GoHighLevel** imports are rebuilt from answers people already sent you, so a question nobody has answered cannot be seen. Add those by hand. For the other three, read the **Worth checking** list again: nothing is ever dropped silently.

    **My multiple choice question came across with no answers to pick from.** That is the **options missing** warning, shown in red on the review step. Open the question in the builder and type the choices in.

    **Archive did nothing and a message appeared.** A booking calendar is using this form. Open that calendar, go to its **Questions** tab, and give it a different form. Then archive.

    **I archived the wrong form.** Message support using the button in the bottom right, before you rebuild it. The form and its answers are still stored. Include the form's name so they can find it quickly.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I rename a form?">
    Yes, but only inside the builder. Click the name at the top left and type over it. There is no rename on the list.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will renaming change my link?">
    Only the very first time, while the form is still called **Untitled form** and still off. After that the address is fixed, and every link you sent out keeps working.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I take a form away?">
    Use **Archive** on the card's three-dot menu. To stop people filling it in without archiving it, turn the form off instead. It stays on the list, ready to switch back on.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which tools can I bring a form over from?">
    **Typeform**, **Tally**, **HubSpot** and **GoHighLevel**. For anything else, click **Add form** and type your questions in.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do I find the JSON the import asks for?">
    It comes from the other tool's API, not from its normal screens. Typeform and Tally both return it at a web address that ends in your form's id. If you do not know how to get it, ask whoever set that tool up.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When does my imported form go live?">
    When you turn it on. It arrives off, whatever it was in the other tool. Read every question first.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
