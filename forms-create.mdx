## Overview

There are exactly two ways to make a form. You start blank, or you bring one over from another tool.

Both land you in the same place: the builder, with your form open and ready to edit.

This page also covers the other things you can do to a whole form. Copy it, share it, and put it away.

<Note>
  **Before you start**

  - Your business must already exist, and you must be signed in to it.
  - Nothing else is needed. You do not need a domain, a pixel, or a paid plan to make a form.
  - Bringing a form over from another tool? You will need that tool's own copy of the form, as text. Section 3 explains where to get it.
</Note>

## 1. Open Forms

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Forms">
    Click **Forms** in the menu on the left of your business. It sits under **Appointments**.

    The page opens with the heading **Forms** and the line *Turn a form on or off, or add a new one*.

      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/create/01-forms-list-dark.png" alt="The Forms list with the page heading and the line reading Turn a form on or off, or add a new one, highlighted at the top left. Form cards fill the rest of the page." />
    </Frame>

    If you have never made a form, you'll see **No forms yet** instead of the cards.

    <Frame caption="The empty state. Both ways to start a form are on it.">
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/create/12-empty-state.png" alt="The Forms empty state, reading No forms yet, with a line explaining that a form can collect leads on its own page, be embedded on a site or be attached to a booking calendar, and two buttons, Create your first form and Import one instead." />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/create/12-empty-state-dark.png" alt="The Forms empty state, reading No forms yet, with a line explaining that a form can collect leads on its own page, be embedded on a site or be attached to a booking calendar, and two buttons, Create your first form and Import one instead." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Read a card">
    Each card is one form. It tells you four things.

    - **The name**, at the top. Click it to open the builder.
    - **The on and off switch**, on the right of the name. Green means the form is live.
    - **The counts**: how many questions it has, how many booking calendars use it, and the day it was last changed.
    - **The ending of its web address**, such as `/f/contact-us`.

    A form that is off looks faded. It also has no web address line, because an off form has no page to open.

    <Frame caption="One form card. The web address line only appears once the form is live.">

    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **The card counts questions, not answers.**

  There is no submission count on this page, and nothing in Cortana lists what people typed. [Where Your Answers Go](/forms-answers) explains what you can see instead.
</Note>

## 2. Add A Blank Form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add form">
    **Add form** sits at the top right of the Forms list, next to **Import**.

    There is no dialog and nothing to fill in. The form is made the moment you click.

    <Frame caption="Add form makes the form straight away. There is nothing to confirm.">
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/create/03-add-form.png" alt="The top of the Forms list with the Add form button highlighted at the top right, beside the Import button." />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/create/03-add-form-dark.png" alt="The top of the Forms list with the Add form button highlighted at the top right, beside the Import button." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Give it a name">
    Cortana opens the builder for you. Your new form is called **Untitled form** and it is **Off**.

    The name sits at the top left, in big text. Type over it.

    Your name is stored when you click somewhere else. Press **Enter** to do the same thing.

    <Frame caption="A brand new form. The big name at the top left is a box you can type in.">
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/create/04-untitled-form.png" alt="The form builder for a brand new form, with the form name box reading Untitled form highlighted at the top left. The header also shows Off, Share and Saved, and the middle column reads Add a question and it appears here." />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/create/04-untitled-form-dark.png" alt="The form builder for a brand new form, with the form name box reading Untitled form highlighted at the top left. The header also shows Off, Share and Saved, and the middle column reads Add a question and it appears here." />
    </Frame>

    Naming it now matters. While the form is still called **Untitled form** and still off, the name you type also becomes its web address. Rename it later and the web address stays as it was.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **You cannot change the web address yourself.**

  Cortana makes it from the name, the first time you name the form. `Contact Us` becomes `/f/contact-us`.

  There is no box anywhere to edit it. So pick the name you want before you share anything.
</Warning>

<Note>
  **A new form is off, so nobody can reach it yet.**

  It stays off until you switch it on. That is covered in [Sharing Your Form](/forms-share).
</Note>

## 3. Bring A Form Over From Another Tool

Cortana can rebuild a form you already have somewhere else. Four tools are supported and no others.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Import">
    **Import** sits at the top right of the Forms list, to the left of **Add form**.

    A box opens, headed **Import a form**, with the line *Bring a form over from another platform. It arrives as a draft so you can check it first.*

    Three step names sit under it: **Platform**, **Paste** and **Review**.

    <Frame caption="Import is beside Add form. It walks you through three steps.">
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/create/05-import-button.png" alt="The top of the Forms list with the Import button highlighted at the top right, to the left of the Add form button." />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/create/05-import-button-dark.png" alt="The top of the Forms list with the Import button highlighted at the top right, to the left of the Add form button." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick the tool">
    Four tools are offered. Each says where its copy of the form comes from.

    | Tool | What the box says |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Typeform** | *Their API returns your form as JSON at api.typeform.com/forms/&#123;id&#125;* |
    | **Tally** | *Their API returns your form as JSON at api.tally.so/forms/&#123;id&#125;* |
    | **HubSpot** | *The form definition from the HubSpot Forms API* |
    | **GoHighLevel** | *Rebuilt from your submissions, since GHL exposes no form schema* |

    Click one and Cortana moves you to **Paste**.

    <Frame caption="The four tools Cortana can read a form from. There are no others.">
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/create/06-import-platform.png" alt="The Import a form box on its Platform step, showing Typeform, Tally, HubSpot and GoHighLevel as four tiles, with the Typeform tile highlighted. Each tile carries a line saying where that tool's copy of the form comes from." />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/create/06-import-platform-dark.png" alt="The Import a form box on its Platform step, showing Typeform, Tally, HubSpot and GoHighLevel as four tiles, with the Typeform tile highlighted. Each tile carries a line saying where that tool's copy of the form comes from." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Paste the form">
    The box now reads **Paste the form definition**.

    This is the one hard part. Cortana does not connect to the other tool for you. You paste the other tool's own copy of the form, as text.

    That text is called JSON. You get it by asking the other tool's API, which is the technical door into that tool. Whoever set it up for you can fetch it in a minute.

    Paste the whole thing, including the outer `{` and `}`. Then click **Review it**.

    <Frame caption="Paste the other tool's own copy of the form here. Nothing is stored yet.">
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/create/07-import-paste.png" alt="The Import a form box on its Paste step, with the large text area highlighted. Above it the label reads Paste the form definition and below sit a Back button and a Review it button." />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/create/07-import-paste-dark.png" alt="The Import a form box on its Paste step, with the large text area highlighted. Above it the label reads Paste the form definition and below sit a Back button and a Review it button." />
    </Frame>

    Paste something that is not JSON and you'll see **That is not valid JSON. Copy the whole response, including the outer &#123; &#125;.**
  </Step>

  <Step title="Read the warnings">
    Cortana shows you what it understood. Nothing has been stored yet.

    You get a **Name** box, a question count, and every question in a list with its kind.

    Above the list sits **Worth checking**, in orange. Read it. It is the reason this step exists.

    A warning tells you something did not come across perfectly. A question we do not have becomes a plain text box. Branching rules are never brought over.

    <Frame caption="Worth checking lists everything that did not come across perfectly.">
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/create/08-import-review.png" alt="The Import a form box on its Review step, with the orange Worth checking panel highlighted. It lists three warnings, and below it every imported question appears with its kind and whether it is required." />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/create/08-import-review-dark.png" alt="The Import a form box on its Review step, with the orange Worth checking panel highlighted. It lists three warnings, and below it every imported question appears with its kind and whether it is required." />
    </Frame>

    Change the **Name** if you want. Then click **Import as a draft**. A draft simply means a form that is off.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open it and check it">
    You'll see how many questions were brought over, and the line *It is saved as a draft called "your form name". Open it to check the questions and publish when you are happy.*

    Click **Open the form**.

    <Frame caption="The form is made, and it is off. Open it and read every question.">
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/create/09-import-done.png" alt="The Import a form box showing a green tick, the line six questions imported, a note that the form is saved as a draft, and the Open the form button highlighted." />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/create/09-import-done-dark.png" alt="The Import a form box showing a green tick, the line six questions imported, a note that the form is saved as a draft, and the Open the form button highlighted." />
    </Frame>

    An imported form is always off. Read every question before you switch it on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **A GoHighLevel form is a guess, not a copy.**

  GoHighLevel does not hand out the design of a form. So Cortana works backwards from answers people already sent you.

  That means a question nobody has answered yet will be missing. A question with a small set of answers may come across as free text.

  Check a GoHighLevel import question by question, against the real form.
</Warning>

## 4. Copy A Form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use the card menu">
    On the card, click the button with the three dots, to the right of **Edit**. Then click **Duplicate**.

    The copy appears straight away, named after the first with **(copy)** on the end. It is always off, even when the form you copied is live.

    There is no name box and nothing to confirm.

    <Frame caption="Duplicate copies the whole form, its questions and its look. The copy is off.">
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/create/10-duplicate.png" alt="A form card's actions menu open on the Forms list, showing Share, Open form, Duplicate and Archive, with Duplicate highlighted." />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/create/10-duplicate-dark.png" alt="A form card's actions menu open on the Forms list, showing Share, Open form, Duplicate and Archive, with Duplicate highlighted." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Copy a form before you change a live one.**

  A live form has no draft and no history. Every edit you make is on the page people are filling in right now.

  So if you want to try something, copy the form first and change the copy.
</Note>

## 5. Put A Form Away

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use the card menu">
    Click the three dots on the card, then **Archive**.

    The form disappears from the list at once. There is no "are you sure".

    <Frame caption="Archive is the only way to remove a form. There is no delete.">
      <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/forms/create/11-archive.png" alt="A form card's actions menu open on the Forms list, showing Share, Open form, Duplicate and Archive, with Archive highlighted in red at the bottom." />
      <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/forms/create/11-archive-dark.png" alt="A form card's actions menu open on the Forms list, showing Share, Open form, Duplicate and Archive, with Archive highlighted in red at the bottom." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Archiving cannot be undone from the app.**

  There is no archived view and no restore button. Once a form leaves the list, you cannot bring it back yourself.

  Turn the form **off** instead if you only want to stop people filling it in. An off form stays on the list and can be switched back on any time.

  If you archive something by mistake, message support before you rebuild it.
</Warning>

<Note>
  **A form a booking calendar uses cannot be archived.**

  You'll see **This form is still used by 1 booking calendar. Archiving it here would not stop those calendars from showing it, unlink it there first.**

  Go to that booking calendar's **Questions** tab and point it at a different form. Then come back.
</Note>

## Settings Reference

Every control on the Forms list.

| Setting | What it does | Default | We recommend | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Add form** | Makes a blank form called **Untitled form** and opens it. | Not applicable | Rename it before you do anything else. | No dialog. The form exists the moment you click. |
| **Import** | Rebuilds a form from Typeform, Tally, HubSpot or GoHighLevel. | Not applicable | Read every **Worth checking** warning before importing. | You must paste the other tool's own copy of the form as text. No file upload, no link. |
| **On and off switch** | Puts the form's page online, or takes it down. | Off for a new form | Leave it off until the form is finished. | Nothing checks the form first. A form with no questions can be switched on. |
| **Edit** | Opens the builder. Same as clicking the name. | Not applicable | Use it. It is the only way to edit anything. | The builder has no Save button. It saves by itself. |
| **Share** *(menu)* | Opens the link, short link, single-use link and website code. | Not applicable | Turn the form on first, or there is nothing to share. | On an off form it only says *Publish the form first*. |
| **Open form** *(menu)* | Opens the form's public page in a new tab. | Not applicable | Always look at your own form before you send it out. | Only shows on a live form. An off form has no page. |
| **Duplicate** *(menu)* | Copies the form, its questions and its look. | Not applicable | Copy before changing anything on a live form. | The copy is named **(copy)** and is always off. No name box. |
| **Archive** *(menu)* | Removes the form from the list. | Not applicable | Only when you are sure. Turn it off instead if you are not. | No confirmation, and no way back from the app. |

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I clicked Add form and nothing seemed to happen">
    It did happen. Cortana takes you straight into the builder for the new form.

    The name at the top left will read **Untitled form**. That is your new form.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The import says That is not valid JSON">
    You pasted something the other tool did not give you, or only part of it.

    Copy the whole response, from the first `{` to the last `}`. Do not paste the web address of your form. Do not paste a screenshot's text.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My imported form is missing questions">
    Check which tool you imported from.

    **GoHighLevel** imports are rebuilt from answers people already sent you. A question nobody has answered cannot be seen, so it is not brought over. Add those by hand.

    For the other three, read the **Worth checking** list again. Nothing is ever dropped silently, so any problem is written there.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My multiple choice question came across with no answers to pick from">
    That is the **options missing** warning, shown in red on the review step.

    Open the question in the builder and type the choices in. [Building Your Form](/forms-build) covers how.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Archive did nothing and a message appeared">
    A booking calendar is using this form. Cortana refuses, because archiving here would not stop that calendar showing the form.

    Open the booking calendar, go to its **Questions** tab, and give it a different form. Then archive.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I archived the wrong form">
    There is no restore button and no archived view in the app.

    Message the support team using the button in the bottom right, and do it before you rebuild the form.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I delete a form?">
    No. There is no delete anywhere in Forms.

    **Archive** is the only way to remove one, and it cannot be undone from the app. If you only want to stop people filling a form in, turn it off instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are there any ready-made forms to start from?">
    No. There is no template gallery.

    You start blank with **Add form**, or you bring a form over with **Import**. Those are the only two ways.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How many forms can I have?">
    As many as you like. There is no limit and no paid tier for Forms.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I rename a form?">
    Yes, but only inside the builder. Click the name at the top left and type over it.

    There is no rename on the list.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will renaming change my link?">
    Only once, and only in one case: while the form is still called **Untitled form** and still off.

    After that the web address is fixed. Rename all you like and every link you sent out keeps working.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I import from Google Forms or Jotform?">
    No. Only **Typeform**, **Tally**, **HubSpot** and **GoHighLevel** are supported.

    For anything else, add a blank form and type the questions in.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do I find the JSON the import asks for?">
    It comes from the other tool's API, not from its normal screens.

    Typeform and Tally both return it at a web address that ends in your form's id. If you do not know how to get it, ask whoever set that tool up. It is a one minute job for them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is an imported form live straight away?">
    No. It is always off, whatever it was in the other tool.

    Read every question, then turn it on yourself.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
