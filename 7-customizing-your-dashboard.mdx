## Overview

Our attribution platform provides deep customization options, allowing you to tailor your data views to mirror your precise marketing funnel. This guide covers how to set up custom presets, define conversion logic, construct advanced formulas, and select your attribution models.

---

## Column Presets

Column presets allow you to quickly pivot between different views of your data (e.g., comparing a "1-Call Close" funnel against a "Free Trial" funnel).

### Creating a New Preset

1. Click the **Column Presets** dropdown menu at the top right of your Attribution Table.
2. Select **Create New Preset** from the menu.
3. In the modal that appears, select your desired metrics from the **Available Columns** list on the left to move them to the **Selected Columns** list on the right.
4. Provide a name in the **Preset Name** field at the bottom.
5. Click **Save**.

> 💡 **Tip:** You can reorder columns at any time within your active preset by clicking and dragging them into your preferred layout order. Default presets can also be edited or deleted as needed.

---

## Configuring Columns & Conversion Events

The platform gives you granular control over what metrics pull into your table, separating them into **Conversions**, **Custom Metrics**, and **Ad Platforms**.

### 1. Conversion Events

Track events pushed directly from your tracking setup (e.g., _Unique Visitors_, _Page Views_, _Lead (Qualified)_, _Schedule_, _Purchase_, _Showed_).

### 2. De-duplicating Conversions with "Show Unique"

By default, the platform can track multiple actions from the same contact. To prevent repetitive actions from skewing your KPIs, configure the **Show Unique** filter on any conversion column:

- **Unique Per Attribution Fingerprint:** _(Recommended)_ Tracks conversions distinctly based on their source attribution path. If a contact books an appointment from "Ad #1", fails to show up, and your team manually reschedules them under that same ad track, it filters out the second event to maintain an accurate 1:1 count for that ad's performance.
- **Count Only 1 Per Contact:** Strictly registers the very first time a contact triggers the event, ignoring all subsequent duplicates indefinitely.
- **Time-Based Windows (7, 14, 30, 90 Days):** De-duplicates events triggered by the same contact within a specified rolling timeframe.

### 3. Toggling Cost and Revenue

For each conversion event column, you can independently toggle two display parameters:

- **Show Cost per \[Conversion Name\]** (e.g., Cost Per Lead / CPL)
- **Show Revenue per \[Conversion Name\]** ---

## Adding Ad Platform Columns

To tie creative performance indicators directly to your conversion metrics, switch to the **Ad Platform** tab inside the column customization modal. From here, you can append platform-native stats such as:

- **Video Performance:** Hook Rate, Hold Rate, Completion Rate, ThruPlays, Video Views (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%).
- **Delivery & Engagement:** Reach, Frequency, CPM, CTR, Link Clicks, Likes, Comments, Shares, Saves.

## Creating Custom Metrics

If you need a localized KPI that isn't calculated out-of-the-box, you can write custom math formulas directly into the interface.

1. Open the column customization modal and select the **Custom Metrics** tab.
2. Click **\+ Create Custom Metric**.
3. Fill in the required configurations:

| Field | Description |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Name** | The label that will display at the top of your column (e.g., `Show Rate`). |
| **Formula** |  |
| **Format** | Choose how the calculated value displays (`Numeric`, `Currency`, or `Percent`). |

### Example Formula: Show Rate

To display your rolling appointment attendance percentage, construct the following formula:

$\text{Show Rate} = \frac{\text{Showed}}{\text{Schedule}}$

_Set the output format to **Percent (%)** to see real-time updates directly in your attribution table._

## Attribution Models

You can change how data credit is shared among touchpoints by using the **Attribution Model** toggle at the top of the report dashboard.

### Available Models

- **Paid Priority** _(Default System setting)_ Gives 100% of the conversion credit to the most recent **paid ad** the customer clicked before converting. If the user’s journey didn’t include a paid ad click, the platform safely falls back to a standard **Last Click** model so your data remains complete.
- **Last Click** Gives total credit to the final touchpoint in the customer's journey before conversion, regardless of organic or paid origin.
- **Scientific** Utilizes a rolling 30-day window. Credit goes to the _first click_ that occurred within 30 days of the conversion. If no touchpoints exist inside that window, it looks for the most recent paid ad; if none are found, it records the absolute first click of the journey.
- **First Click** Gives 100% of the conversion credit to the very first documented interaction the customer had with your brand, even if it took place months prior.

## Conditional Formatting

Highlight your winning or underperforming channels visually using conditional coloring rules.

1. Access **Conditional Formatting** via your Column Presets dropdown menu.
2. Click **\+ Create Rule**.
3. Select whether you want to build a **Single Color** rule or a **Color Scale**.
4. Define your rule logic (e.g., If `Cost Per Purchase` is `Greater Than` `100`, Apply Color: **Red**).
5. Chain secondary logic if required (e.g., If `Cost Per Purchase` is `Less Than` `100`, Apply Color: **Green**).

## Advanced Display Settings

Click the **Gear Icon** next to your metrics bar to manage structural data behavior across your views.

### General Settings

- **Ad Accounts:** Toggle single or multiple ad accounts simultaneously to combine data feeds or isolate platform reports.
- **Currency:** Unify your metrics. If you spend your budget in CAD or AUD but your revenue processing accounts receive USD, the platform handles currency conversion automatically to output a clean, standardized ROAS metric.
- **Timezone:** Shift your report display timestamp grid to coordinate perfectly with your localized business operations timezone (e.g., GMT-5 Eastern Time).

### Breakdowns

Segment your main rows into layered sub-rows using inputs pulled directly via native two-way syncs with your CRM (e.g., HubSpot, GoHighLevel) or intake tools (e.g., Typeform, Calendly).

- **By Custom Field:** Drill into metric groups based on properties like customer revenue tiers (e.g., separating pipelines into \$0–\$10k vs. \$10k–\$20k cohorts).
- **By Tag:** Slice your attribution table via applied CRM organization tags.

### Count Conversions On

This setting fundamentally shifts the date to which conversions are back-dated, resolving common reporting discrepancies:

- **Date of Conversion (Default):** Places the conversion metric entirely on the calendar day it occurred.
  - _Note:_ This can cause data to appear uneven week-over-week (e.g., tracking 10 leads one week that result in 0 sales, then registering 6 sales the next week against only 1 new lead because prior leads finally closed).
- **Date of Click:** Shifts the conversion marker backwards to the exact calendar date the user clicked the ad. If a customer clicks an ad today but buys 45 days later, the revenue metric maps back to today's date to give you a true look at the ad's immediate day-of placement strength.
- **Date of Lead Qualified:** Back-dates your downstream funnel activities (like product purchases or call attendance) to the exact day that the user originally opted into your system as a qualified lead.
