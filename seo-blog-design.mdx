## Overview

Your blog has two pages you can design: **Blog home**, the list of your posts, and **Article**, the page every post shows in. They are pages of your Cortana website. You edit them in the Studio, by hand or with AI, just like any website page.

At the end you have a blog in your own design, live on your domain.

## 1. Make your blog's pages

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Content tab">
    Open **SEO**, then the **Content** tab. Click **Design your blog**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a design">
    Choose **Journal**, **Magazine** or **Minimal**. You can change it later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make the pages">
    Click **Make my blog's pages**. The Studio opens on your blog. It uses your website's style. If you have no Cortana website yet, Cortana makes one for you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Next time, the button reads **Edit your blog's design**, with **Live** or **Not published** beside it.

---

## 2. Edit the two pages

<Frame caption="Your blog in the Studio: its two pages, Layout and Publish design.">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/seo/blog-design/02-studio.png" alt="The Studio on the blog. The switch between Blog home and Article is marked 1, Layout is marked 2, and Publish design is marked 3." />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/seo/blog-design/02-studio-dark.png" alt="The Studio on the blog. The switch between Blog home and Article is marked 1, Layout is marked 2, and Publish design is marked 3." />
</Frame>

1. Switch between **Blog home** and **Article** at the top.
2. Edit them like any website page: text, sections, images, colors, buttons, forms and calendars.
3. On **Article**, the article element shows each post. You can move it and style around it. It always stays on the page.

To start from another design, click **Layout**, pick one, and click **Use** with its name. The Studio says how many of your own elements leave the pages first. Your live blog keeps its design until you publish again.

---

## 3. Publish the design

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Publish design">
    Click **Publish design** at the top of the Studio.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check what goes live">
    Cortana checks both pages and says which ones go live. If nothing changed, it says so.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish design**. Every post and list page shows the new design within about two minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your posts are not touched. Only their look changes.

## 5. Your blog on your website

When your Cortana website goes live on your domain, your blog goes live at `yourdomain.com/blog`. You do not need to set anything up.

Your website's menu shows **Blog** after your pages. To hide it, select the menu in the Studio, open its settings, and switch **Blog** off.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When does my blog not move to my website's /blog?">
    Your blog stays where it is when it already lives on another address, like `blog.yourdomain.com`. It also stays when you are connecting another address, when your website has its own page at `/blog`, or when your articles go to WordPress.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What the article element shows">
    The post's title, author, date, picture, words and FAQ. In the Studio, pick which post it shows while you design, in the article's settings. Your live blog always shows the post a reader opened.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Custom code on your blog">
    A custom block's look (its HTML and CSS) shows on your blog. Its script does not run there. **Publish design** tells you when your design has one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Checkouts on your blog">
    A checkout or an upsell goes on a website or funnel page, never on a blog page. Link to that page from your blog instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Edit your blog with AI">
    Connected AI tools (like Claude or Codex) see your blog's two pages and can edit and publish them, just like your website. The article element always stays.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why does my blog still look the old way after I changed it?">
    Your changes are a draft until you click **Publish design**. After you publish, give it about two minutes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I delete the article from the Article page?">
    No. Every post shows in it. Move it anywhere on the page and style around it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I see an article in my design before I publish it?">
    Open the article in the **Content** tab and click **Preview on your blog**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is there a Blog link in my website menu?">
    Your blog is live, so your website links to it. Switch **Blog** off in the menu's settings to hide it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I go back to the plain blog?">
    A blog with no published design looks the way it always did. Once you publish a design, your blog uses it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
