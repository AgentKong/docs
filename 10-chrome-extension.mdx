# Getting Started with the Cortana AI Extension

The Cortana AI Chrome extension adds your conversion data to Meta Ads Manager, so you can see the leads, bookings and payments each campaign produced next to Meta's own numbers. It also gives you a toolbar popup for sending payment links, managing appointments and minting tracking links.
