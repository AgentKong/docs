## What is a recipe?

A recipe is a portable agent setup: a bundle of skills, tool connectors, and scheduled tasks, optionally with a persona and model. Instead of configuring an agent by hand, you apply a recipe and get a working setup in one step.

Find recipes in the **Recipes** view of the agent workspace.

## Creating a recipe

Recipes are composed from things you already have:

1. Open **Recipes** and start a new recipe.
2. Pick from your existing **skills**, **connected tools**, and **schedules** (buttons take you straight to each creation surface if you need to make something new first).
3. Optionally include a **persona prompt** and **model**, so the recipe can hatch a complete agent.
4. Save. The recipe is private to your business until you publish it.

Limits per recipe: 20 skills, 10 connectors, 10 schedules.

## Applying a recipe

There are two ways to use a recipe:

- **Apply to an existing agent** — adds the recipe's skills and schedules to an agent you already have.
- **Hatch a new agent** — creates a fresh agent from the recipe, using its persona and model.

What happens on apply:

- **Skills** are copied into the target agent.
- **Tool connectors** are set up business-wide. Ones that need no sign-in go live immediately; ones that need authentication are added disabled, and a connect card appears so you can finish sign-in.
- **Schedules** are created **paused** — nothing recurring starts running (or spending) until you review and enable it in Automations.

## The marketplace

The marketplace has two sections:

- **Verified recipes** — published by the Cortana team.
- **Community recipes** — published by other Cortana users, reviewed and approved by Cortana before they appear.

All marketplace listings are anonymous — your business name is never shown on a recipe you publish.

## Publishing your own recipe

1. Open one of your recipes and choose **Publish**.
2. It enters review; once approved it appears in Community recipes. You can withdraw it at any time.
3. Editing a published recipe unpublishes it — you'll need to publish the new version again.

## Safety

Recipes never contain credentials — publishing a recipe with connected tools shares the tool's address and auth method, never your tokens. Every recipe is re-validated when applied, and connectors must use secure `https://` URLs.
