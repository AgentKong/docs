## Overview

A **recipe** bundles skills, tools, and loops so a whole job can be set up in one click — on one of your agents, or on a brand-new one. The **Recipes** tab has two parts:

- **My recipes** — recipes you've built, ready to apply or publish.
- **Recipe marketplace** — ready-made setups shared across Cortana. **Verified** recipes are published by Cortana; **Community** recipes come from other businesses (always anonymous, reviewed before going live).

<Note>
  **Prerequisites** — to build a recipe you need something to bundle: at least one [skill](/claw/skills), [tool](/claw/connectors), or [loop](/claw/automations). To apply one, you need an agent to apply it to (or let it create one).
</Note>

## Apply a recipe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Recipes tab">
    In the CortanaClaw workspace rail, click **Recipes**. Your own recipes are under **My recipes**; shared ones are under **Recipe marketplace**.

    <Frame caption="The Recipes tab: your recipes up top, the shared marketplace below.">
      <img src="/images/claw/recipes/01-recipes-tab.png" alt="The Recipes tab. A 'My recipes' section with a highlighted 'New recipe' button and a recipe named 'stock — 1 tool — check stock market' with a highlighted 'Use' button and a menu. Below, a highlighted 'Recipe marketplace' heading explains that Verified recipes are published by Cortana and Community recipes come from other businesses, reviewed before going live." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Use and choose where it goes">
    Click **Use** on any recipe. You can either **attach** it to an existing agent, or **hatch** a brand-new agent whose persona and model come from the recipe.

    When you apply a recipe: its **skills are copied** into the target agent; its **tools are provisioned business-wide**; and its **schedules are created paused**, so nothing runs until you turn it on.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish any sign-ins">
    If a recipe includes a tool that needs authentication, a **connect card** appears after applying — finish the sign-in there and the tool goes live. Tools that need no auth are live immediately. See [Connectors](/claw/connectors).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Recipe **schedules always arrive paused**. Open the [Loops](/claw/automations) tab and turn one on when you're ready — this prevents a shared recipe from silently spending your credit.
</Warning>

## Build your own recipe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New recipe">
    In **My recipes**, click **New recipe** to open the composer.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Compose it from what you already have">
    Give the recipe a **Recipe name** and a **Description**, and optionally a **Persona for new agents** (used when someone hatches a new agent from it). Then tick the **Skills**, **Tools (MCPs)**, and **Loops** you want to include — recipes are composed **from existing** items. Use **Create a skill**, **Connect a tool**, or **Create a loop** to add something new first. You must pick at least one skill, tool, or loop.

    Click **Save recipe**.

    <Frame caption="The recipe composer. Tick existing skills and tools to bundle them.">
      <img src="/images/claw/recipes/02-recipe-composer.png" alt="The New recipe composer with a Recipe name field, a Description field, an optional 'Persona for new agents' box, a highlighted Skills and Tools (MCPs) area with checkboxes for each existing skill and connected tool (coingecko, context7, deepwiki, githubcopilot, gitmcp, linear and others), a Loops section reading 'No loops yet', and a highlighted 'Save recipe' button with the hint 'Pick at least one skill, tool or loop.'" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **No secrets ever travel in a recipe.** Tool credentials live in the encrypted vault and are never included in a recipe by design — a shared recipe carries the tool's address and how it authenticates, not your keys.
</Note>

## Publish to the marketplace

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How publishing works" icon="upload">
    From a recipe's **…** menu, publish it. A recipe moves **private → pending → published**. Recipes published by Cortana go live instantly as **Verified**; recipes you publish require **superuser approval** before they appear under **Community**. Any edit to a pending or published recipe reverts it to private, so you re-publish after changes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Anonymity and review" icon="user-secret">
    Marketplace listings are **anonymous** — your business identity is stripped from a published recipe. Community submissions are reviewed before going live.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Safety of installing shared recipes" icon="shield-check">
    Every applied recipe is re-validated before it runs: only `https://` tool URLs are allowed, and a recipe can carry at most 20 skills, 10 tools, and 10 schedules. Because secrets can never be part of a recipe, installing one never exposes another business's credentials — or yours.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Settings reference

| Field | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Recipe name** | Composer | The recipe's title. |
| **Description** | Composer | What the recipe sets up, and for whom. |
| **Persona for new agents** | Composer | Persona applied when someone hatches a new agent from the recipe (optional). |
| **Skills / Tools (MCPs) / Loops** | Composer | The existing items to bundle. At least one required. |
| **Use** | Recipe row | Apply the recipe — attach to an existing agent or hatch a new one. |
| **… menu** | Recipe row | Edit, publish, or manage the recipe. |

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A tool from a recipe isn't working">
    It probably needs sign-in. Look for the connect card that appears after applying, and complete authentication. Until then the tool stays disabled. See [Connectors](/claw/connectors).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The recipe's schedule didn't run">
    Recipe schedules are created **paused**. Turn it on from the [Loops](/claw/automations) tab.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My published recipe went back to private">
    Editing a pending or published recipe reverts it to private. Re-publish it from the **…** menu after your changes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't save my recipe">
    A recipe must include at least one skill, tool, or loop — the composer shows *"Pick at least one skill, tool or loop."* Tick something first.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between Verified and Community recipes?">
    **Verified** recipes are published by Cortana and go live instantly. **Community** recipes are submitted by other businesses, published anonymously, and reviewed before appearing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does applying a recipe overwrite my agent?">
    No. It **adds** the recipe's skills and tools, and creates its schedules paused. Choose to attach to an existing agent or hatch a new one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will installing a Community recipe expose my data or theirs?">
    No. Recipes never contain secrets, listings are anonymous, and every applied recipe is re-validated (https-only tools, size caps) before it runs.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change a recipe after publishing?">
    Yes — edit it, which returns it to private, then re-publish.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
