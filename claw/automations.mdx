## The Automations view

Open **Automations** in the agent workspace to see everything your agents do on their own — recurring schedules and standing goals in one list, sorted by what's running now, what's due next, and what's idle.

## Scheduled tasks

A schedule fires an agent task on a recurring cadence.

**Create one from:**

- The **Automations** view (**New automation**).
- Slack: `/schedule` (or `/cortana schedule`) opens the scheduling form.
- The agent itself — you can just ask it to "check this every morning at 8" and approve its proposal.
- A [recipe](/claw/recipes) — recipe schedules arrive **paused** so nothing runs until you turn it on.

You describe the timing in plain language ("every weekday at 9am", "every 2 hours") and Cortana converts it to a precise schedule in your timezone.

**How schedules behave:**

- Each schedule runs a specific agent with your task text, on the cloud by default, or pinned to your desktop.
- If the previous run is still going, the next one waits — runs never overlap.
- A schedule that fails 5 times in a row pauses itself so it can't burn credit in a broken loop.

**Limits:**

- Minimum cadence: **15 minutes**.
- Up to **50 active schedules** per business (paused schedules don't count; the cap is re-checked when you re-enable one).

## Goals

A goal is a standing objective with success criteria — "get our help-center articles updated for the new pricing", "find and fix broken UTM links". Instead of firing once, the agent:

1. Works an iteration toward the goal.
2. Judges its own progress against your success criteria.
3. Keeps notes on what it tried and what's next.
4. Loops on the cadence you set until the goal is **achieved**, it runs out of attempts, or you stop it.

While iterating, the agent records procedures that worked as [skills](/claw/skills), so progress compounds.

**Controls and limits:**

- You set the cadence, the max number of attempts, and whether iterations run gated (approvals) or unattended.
- Up to **20 active goals** per business.
- Pause, resume, or stop a goal any time from the Automations view.

## Billing note

Every scheduled run and goal iteration is a normal agent run and bills like one. The cadence floor and the caps above exist to protect your wallet — see [Approvals and billing](/claw/approvals-and-billing).
