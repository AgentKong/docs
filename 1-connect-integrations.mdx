# Integrations Setup

Connect the tools you already use. Cortana then gets your sales, leads, bookings and ad data. Each tool has its own short guide below.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    In your business, click **Settings** in the menu on the left.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Integrations tab">
    Click the **Integrations** tab. It is the first tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your tool">
    Type its name in the **Search integrations...** box. Its card appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Click **Connect** on the card. Then follow that tool's guide below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How connecting works

Every card connects in one of three ways:

- **Sign in.** You go to the tool's own website. You sign in and approve. Then you come back to Cortana.
- **Paste a key.** You copy a key from the tool. You paste it into Cortana. Each guide says where the key lives.
- **Install or paste a script.** A few stores need an app or a small script. The guide walks you through it.

When a tool is connected, its button changes. It often says **Manage**. With several accounts it says, for example, **Manage (2)**.

<Note>
  Most tools then need one more step: tell Cortana what counts as a conversion. You do that in [**4.2) Conversion Sources**](/4-conversion-tracking). Each guide below links to its part.
</Note>

## Connecting Your Data Sources

Data sources bring in your leads, bookings and sales. Find your tool in the groups below:

- **CRMs:** GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Salesforce, Close.io.
- **Payments and stores:** Stripe, PayPal, Shopify, WooCommerce, Whop, Kajabi, Commas (Fanbasis), Shopier.
- **Forms and funnels:** Typeform, Tally Forms, ClickFunnels, iClosed.
- **Calendars, calls and meetings:** Calendly, Cal.com, CallRail, Fathom.
- **Webinars:** Zoom, WebinarJam.
- **Affiliates and imports:** FirstPromoter, Hyros Data Import.

<Note>
  **Coming from Hyros?** Use the **Hyros Data Import** card. It copies your past Hyros data into Cortana once. See [Connecting Hyros Data Import](#connecting-hyros-data-import).
</Note>

## CRMs

### Connecting GoHighLevel

GoHighLevel brings in contacts, messages, calendars and conversions from one sub-account.

**How it connects:** sign in to GoHighLevel. Then paste one token.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Click **Connect** on the **GoHighLevel** card. GoHighLevel opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your sub-account">
    Choose the sub-account for this business. Approve the access. You come back to Cortana.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make a Private Integration Token">
    A box opens. It says **Sorry, one last step.** The token keeps your connection steady.

    In GoHighLevel, go to **Settings → Private Integrations → Create Private Integration**. Add all the scopes, or just the 36 required ones. Copy the token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the token and Location ID">
    Paste the token. It starts with `pit-`.

    Then fill in **Location ID**. It is in your GoHighLevel web address. It is also in **Settings → Business Profile**. Click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Cortana reads your GoHighLevel users. Invite them, so their deals and bookings show as theirs. Then click **Done**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The card now shows **Manage (1)**. Click it to see your **GoHighLevel Subaccounts**.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The card button turns amber or red">
    The connection needs attention. Open **Manage**. A sub-account may say the token is invalid. Click **Reconnect** on it and paste a fresh Private Integration Token.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

**Next:** count GoHighLevel events as conversions. See [**4.2) Conversion Sources**](/4-conversion-tracking) under **GoHighLevel**.

### Connecting PayPal

PayPal brings in payments, subscription payments and refunds.

**How it connects:** paste two keys. PayPal has no sign-in button for this. So you make a small app in PayPal and copy its keys. It takes about three minutes.

Click **Connect** on the **PayPal** card. The **Connect PayPal** box opens.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the PayPal Developer dashboard on Live">
    Go to [developer.paypal.com](https://developer.paypal.com/dashboard/applications/live). Check it says **Live**, not **Sandbox**. Sandbox keys connect, but no real payment ever shows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your app, or create one">
    Open an app you have. Or click **Create App** and pick the type **Merchant**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tick Transaction Search, then Save">
    In the app's features, tick **Transaction Search**. Click **Save**. This step is required. Without it, nothing imports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the Client ID and Secret into Cortana">
    Paste the **Client ID** into **Client ID**. Paste the **Secret** into **Secret**. Click **Connect PayPal**.

    You see **PayPal connected**. Your payment history starts importing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="It says: PayPal connected, but a permission is missing">
    Your keys work, but **Transaction Search** is not on yet. Tick it in your PayPal app and save. PayPal can take up to 9 hours to turn it on. You do not need to reconnect. The account shows **Enabling access** until PayPal is ready.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says: These credentials can also take payments">
    It still works. Cortana only reads your payments. A read-only app is just safer to share.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Some payments show up late">
    Payments through a Cortana payment link show in seconds. Invoices and anything else paid inside PayPal take 20 minutes to 3 hours. That delay is PayPal's. Nothing is lost.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

**Next:** count PayPal payments as conversions. See [**4.2) Conversion Sources**](/4-conversion-tracking) under **PayPal**.

### Connecting WooCommerce

WooCommerce brings in your orders, carts and checkout steps.

**How it connects:** paste your store keys, then paste a tracking script. No WordPress plugin from Cortana is needed.

Click **Connect** on the **WooCommerce** card. Then click **Connect WooCommerce Store**. There are four steps: **Store**, **Keys**, **Pixel** and **Verify**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Store">
    Type your **Store URL**, like `https://yourstore.com`. Click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keys">
    In WordPress, go to **WooCommerce → Settings → Advanced → REST API**. Click **Add key**. Set permissions to **Read/Write**. Click **Generate**. Copy both values now. They show only once.

    Paste them into **Consumer Key** and **Consumer Secret**. Click **Connect Store**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pixel">
    Click **Copy pixel script**. In WordPress, install the free **WPCode Lite** plugin. Go to **Code Snippets → Header & Footer**. Paste the script into **Header**. Save.

    Back in Cortana, click **I've installed it → Verify**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Open your store in a new tab. Cortana waits for the first visit. You can also click **Send test event**. When it all works, click **Finish**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Next:** count WooCommerce orders as conversions. See [**4.2) Conversion Sources**](/4-conversion-tracking) under **WooCommerce**.

### Connecting Kajabi

Kajabi brings in every payment on your site. Every offer is covered, even new ones.

**How it connects:** paste two scripts, then paste two keys. You need a tracking pixel in Cortana first, under **Tracking → Pixels**.

Click **Connect** on the **Kajabi** card. The **Connect Kajabi** box has three steps: **Scripts**, **API Keys** and **Verify**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scripts">
    Copy **Paste 1: Site pages**. In Kajabi, go to **Settings → Site Details → Page Scripts (Header)**. Paste it.

    Copy **Paste 2: Checkout pages**. In Kajabi, go to **Settings → Checkout → Checkout Tracking Code → Header tracking code**. Paste it. This one is required. Checkout pages do not get the first script.

    Click **Next · API Keys**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="API Keys">
    In Kajabi, go to **Settings → Public API → Create User API Key**. You must be the account **Owner**. You need the Pro plan, or the API add-on.

    Paste the **Client ID** and **Client Secret**. Click **Connect & Create Webhooks**. If you have several Kajabi sites, pick one under **Which Kajabi site?**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Load any page of your Kajabi site. Then make a test purchase. A 100% coupon works. Click **Re-check** to see both arrive. Click **Done**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Next:** count Kajabi payments as conversions. See [**4.2) Conversion Sources**](/4-conversion-tracking) under **Kajabi**.

### Connecting LinkedIn Ads

LinkedIn Ads brings in your LinkedIn spend, results and Lead Gen Form leads.

**How it connects:** sign in to LinkedIn. Then pick your ad accounts. Nothing syncs until you save that choice.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect LinkedIn">
    Click **Connect** on the **LinkedIn Ads** card. Sign in to LinkedIn and approve. Cortana reads your ads, reports and lead forms. It also sends conversions back to LinkedIn. It cannot change your ads or spend money.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the LinkedIn Ads settings">
    Click **Manage** on the card. The **LinkedIn Ads** panel opens. It shows a **LinkedIn Ads Accounts** table. Every account starts as **Not Selected**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how far back to sync">
    Pick a **Sync period**: **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 3 months**, **Last 6 months** or **Last 12 months**. The default is **Last 7 days**. Longer periods take a few minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tick the ad accounts you want">
    Tick each account to sync. Use **Search accounts...** to find one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow to specific campaigns (optional)">
    Click the number in the **Campaigns** column. The **Select Campaigns** box opens. Tick the ones you want. Click **Save selection**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Changes">
    Click **Save Changes**. The first sync starts right away. After that, LinkedIn refreshes every hour.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="An account says No analytics access">
    Your LinkedIn user has no analytics role on that account. You can tick it, but no results will come in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The campaign picker says: Tick this account and save it first">
    Tick the account. Click **Save Changes**. Then pick its campaigns.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A dash shows instead of a campaign count">
    Cortana could not read the count from LinkedIn. It does not mean zero campaigns.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says: Your LinkedIn connection has expired">
    LinkedIn logins last 365 days. They do not renew by themselves. Reconnect LinkedIn Ads once a year. Your data stays safe.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An amber banner sits on the account list">
    Some accounts did not load, or the list is an older copy. Click **Reload list** before you save.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Breaking LinkedIn down by campaign and ad">
    Clicks count as **LinkedIn Ads** straight away. To split them by campaign and ad, tag your LinkedIn links. See [**5) URL Parameters**](/5-url-parameters) under **LinkedIn Ads**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

**Next:** count LinkedIn Lead Gen Form leads as conversions. See [**4.2) Conversion Sources**](/4-conversion-tracking) under **LinkedIn Ads**.
