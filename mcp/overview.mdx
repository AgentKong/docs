The Cortana MCP connector gives an AI assistant a live connection to your business data, and lets it act for you: build and run Meta ads, set up tracking, and run your AI Media Buyer. Ask in plain English and the assistant picks the right tools.

<Note>
  The standout: Cortana ties ad spend to actual revenue and closed customers, not just platform metrics. Your assistant can compare Meta spend to Stripe revenue in one conversation.
</Note>

## What is MCP?

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open standard that lets AI assistants connect to external data and tools. Instead of copy-pasting exports into Claude, the assistant queries your Cortana data directly, then acts on it.

## The cross-domain superpower

Each source is useful on its own, but the value is combining them in a single question. Because Cortana already connects your ad platforms, revenue tools, and CRM, the assistant can answer things no single dashboard can, like which campaigns brought in customers who actually stuck around.

- Compare Meta ad spend to Stripe revenue this month, by campaign.
- Find the ads that brought in your highest-LTV customers, then build more like them.
- Pull the objections from last week's sales calls and the ad that sourced each lead.

## Read by default, act by opt-in

Read tools are available as soon as you connect. Tools that change things sit behind opt-ins on the consent screen: Meta ad management (anything that creates, launches, or spends) and the AI Media Buyer's actions (approvals, new ads, posting, settings, Slack, and licenses). Meta ads are created paused, and going live or changing budgets always needs your confirmation. See [Connect your AI](/mcp/setup).

## Manage Ad Tracking licenses from chat

{/* last-verified: 2026-10-06 · tools: get_ad_tracking_licenses, set_ad_tracking */}

Your assistant can check how many Ad Tracking licenses your plan has left and switch Ad Tracking on or off for your businesses, so new client accounts get rolling without opening Cortana.

1. Make sure the connection is allowed to act. On the Cortana consent screen, keep **Let (your assistant) act on your media buyer decisions** ticked (it is on by default). On an API key, tick **Act on media buyer decisions**.
2. Ask your assistant, for example "How many tracking licenses do I have left?" or "Turn Ad Tracking on for Acme Windows and Bolt Roofing."
3. The assistant answers with one line per business: on, already on, off, already off, or the reason it could not.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How Ad Tracking licenses are counted">
    A business uses one license while its Ad Tracking is on. Your plan sets how many licenses you have. The count covers your whole account, not only the businesses on this connection. When the plan is full, the assistant tells you the limit is reached and that you can add licenses in Billing. Adding licenses is done in Billing, not from chat.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What switching Ad Tracking off does">
    Switching Ad Tracking off frees the license. It also switches that business's AI Media Buyer off, because the media buyer cannot run without tracking. Cortana asks your assistant to tell you this and get your yes before it switches anything off. Nothing already tracked is deleted.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who can switch Ad Tracking from chat">
    The connection needs the opt-in from step 1, and your Cortana login needs the **Manage ad tracking** permission (account owners always have it). Each business must be on this connection and be one you can open in Cortana. One request can switch up to 100 businesses.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Businesses shared with you">
    A business shared with you from another account uses the owner's plan, so its license comes from the owner's count, not yours.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My assistant says the Ad Tracking limit is reached">
    Every license on your plan is in use. Add licenses in Billing, or ask your assistant to switch Ad Tracking off on a business you no longer track.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My assistant says I need the Manage ad tracking permission">
    Your account admin turned it off for your login. They can turn it on from **Control Centre** > **Users** > your name > **Manage Permissions** > **Ad Tracking**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My assistant says I have no Ad Tracking subscription">
    Your plan has no Ad Tracking licenses, so no business can be switched on. Add an Ad Tracking plan in Billing, then ask again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why did my AI Media Buyer turn off when I switched tracking off?">
    The AI Media Buyer needs Ad Tracking to run, so switching tracking off switches it off too and frees its license. Switch Ad Tracking back on first, then switch the AI Media Buyer back on in Cortana.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Run your Slack channels from chat

{/* last-verified: 2026-10-13 · tools: list_slack_channels, create_slack_channel, connect_slack_channel, list_slack_teammates, invite_to_slack_channel */}

Your assistant can create Slack channels for a business, choose the channel where the AI Media Buyer posts its proposals, and add your teammates to a channel, all by asking. At the end the business has its channel with the right people in it, without opening Slack's settings.

1. Make sure the connection is allowed to act. On the Cortana consent screen, keep **Let (your assistant) act on your media buyer decisions** ticked. On an API key, tick **Act on media buyer decisions**. The business needs Slack connected in Cortana and the AI Media Buyer switched on.
2. Ask for a channel, for example "Make Acme a Slack channel and put the media buyer in it" or "Create a channel called acme-creative." The assistant answers with the channel's name as Slack saved it.
3. To use a channel you already have, ask "Connect the media buyer to #acme-ads." The assistant says where the media buyer posted before and where it posts now.
4. Ask it to add people, for example "Add Sarah and Mike to #acme-ai-media-buyer." It answers one line per person: added, already in the channel, a guest Slack keeps out, several people with that name, or not found.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What the assistant can do in Slack">
    List the business's channels and see which one the AI Media Buyer posts into. Create public channels. Make a new or existing channel the AI Media Buyer's channel. List the people in the workspace. Add up to 50 people to a public channel in one request, by name or Slack ID.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How names are matched">
    A name matches a person's full name, display name, or username, ignoring capitals. If nobody matches exactly, a first name works ("Sarah" finds "Sarah Connor"). When several people match, nobody is added for that name: the assistant lists them and asks which one you mean. Cortana never reads email addresses from Slack.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The AI Media Buyer's channel">
    Each business has one AI Media Buyer channel, and a channel can belong to only one business. A channel another business's AI Media Buyer already uses cannot be picked. Creating a new channel does not move the AI Media Buyer off the channel it already has unless you ask to replace it. Connecting an existing channel does move it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Channel names">
    Slack channel names are lowercase, use dashes instead of spaces, and are at most 80 characters, so "Acme Creative" becomes `acme-creative`. A channel created for the AI Media Buyer without a name uses your agency's naming template, the same one the **Create Slack channel** button uses in the media buyer settings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Private channels">
    The assistant creates public channels and adds people to public channels only. To use a private channel as the AI Media Buyer's channel, add the Cortana app to it in Slack first, then ask the assistant to connect it. Add people to a private channel in Slack.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My assistant says to reconnect Slack">
    Your Slack was connected before Cortana could create channels and add people. Reconnect Slack once on the business's **Integrations** page, then ask again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My assistant says Slack is not connected for this business">
    Connect Slack on the business's **Integrations** page in Cortana, then ask again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why won't it add someone to my private channel?">
    Adding people to private channels is not available yet. Add them in Slack, or use a public channel.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says several people match the name">
    More than one person in your workspace has that name. Tell the assistant which one you mean (it shows each person's full name, display name, and username) and it adds that person.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says to invite the Cortana app to the channel">
    The channel is private and Cortana's Slack app is not in it. In Slack, add the Cortana app to the channel, then ask again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says Slack is busy">
    Slack limits how fast apps can work. Wait a minute and ask again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
