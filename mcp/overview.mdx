## What is MCP?

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open standard that lets AI assistants connect to external data and tools. Instead of copy-pasting exports into Claude, the assistant queries your Cortana data directly, then acts on it.

## The cross-domain superpower

Each domain is useful on its own, but the value is combining them in a single question. Because Cortana already connects your ad platforms, revenue tools, and CRM, the assistant can answer things no single dashboard can, like which campaigns brought in customers who actually stuck around.

A few examples:

- Compare Meta ad spend to Stripe revenue this month, by campaign.
- Find the ads that brought in your highest-LTV customers, then build more like them.
- Pull the objections from last week's sales calls and the ad that sourced each lead.

## Read by default, manage by opt-in

Read tools are available as soon as you connect. Ad management tools (anything that creates, launches, or spends) require a separate opt-in checkbox on the consent screen. Everything is created paused, and going live or changing budgets always requires your confirmation.
