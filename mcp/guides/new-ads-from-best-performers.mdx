---
title: "Make new ads from your best performers"
description: "Ask your AI which ads are winning and why, shape a direction together, and have Cortana write a new batch built on them."
---

Your AI can show you which ads are winning and why, help you pick what to lean on, and order a new batch from Cortana built on those winners. At the end, the new concepts are written and waiting in Review for your yes.

<Note>
  Needs the AI Media Buyer on for the business, and the media buyer opt-in on the connection (**Let (your assistant) act on your media buyer decisions**). See [Connect your AI](/mcp/setup).
</Note>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask what's winning">
    Ask: *"What are my best ads for Acme Windows, and why did they win?"* Your AI reads your winning ads with the angle, the hook, the offer and who each one speaks to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See what's working across the account">
    Ask: *"Which angles and formats are converting, and where is the spend going?"* Your AI reads the account by angle, hook, format and offer, with cost per result against your target.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shape a direction">
    Ask: *"Help me plan the next batch."* Your AI reads the creative brief (your offer, your winners, what prospects say on calls) and brainstorms with you. Settle on a direction in your own words, for example *"more like my top 3, leaning on the warranty."*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order the batch">
    Ask: *"Make 6 ads with that direction."* Before anything starts, Cortana reads your direction against the account and answers go, caution or stop, with why and what it recommends, plus what the order includes. Say yes and the order starts. An order with no direction starts at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for it">
    Your AI reports progress ("4 of 6 written") until every concept is written. Where Cortana makes the images, they follow within about 10 minutes, and each ad lands in Review once all of its variations are ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and approve">
    Ask: *"Show me what's in Review."* Approve or deny each variation, for example *"approve 1A and 1C, deny 1B, too salesy."* Approved variations launch at your next posting slot.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you get back

- Your winners, with why each one won.
- A read of your direction before you spend anything.
- A batch of new concepts, each with its variations, waiting in Review.

<Tip>
  Want exact words on the ad? Say *"use exactly this hook: ..."* and Cortana keeps it word for word.
</Tip>
