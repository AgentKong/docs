---
title: "Ask about your sales calls"
description: "Ask your AI what prospects say on your calls: their pains, objections and questions, in their own words, and what happened on any one call."
---

Your AI can read your recorded calls and meetings and tell you what prospects actually say: the pains, dreams, objections, questions and competitors that come up most, with the exact words behind each. At the end you have the patterns across your calls, and any single call's transcript when you want it.

<Note>
  Needs your call recordings connected in Cortana (meeting recordings, or calls through Cortana). Call insights need recorded calls in a call group.
</Note>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask what comes up most">
    Ask: *"What objections come up most on our sales calls?"* Your AI reads your call insights: the most-mentioned pains, dreams, objections, questions and competitors, each with how many calls it came up on and a few verbatim quotes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get the prospect's own words">
    Ask: *"Give me the exact words prospects use about price."* Your AI pages through every line in that bank with the customer's verbatim sentences behind it. Use them in ads and scripts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at recent meetings">
    Ask: *"Summarize last week's demos and their next steps."* Your AI lists meetings with attendees, outcome and summary, and reads a full transcript when you ask.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dig into one call">
    Ask: *"Read me the transcript of yesterday's call with John."* Your AI finds the call and reads the conversation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Combine it with your ads: *"Which objections come up on calls from leads that came from Meta?"* or *"Write three hooks from what prospects say about the warranty."*
</Tip>

## What you get back

- The top lines in each bank, with counts and quotes.
- Meeting summaries, action items and next steps.
- Full transcripts on request.
