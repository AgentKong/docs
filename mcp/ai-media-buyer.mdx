The Cortana MCP connector gives your own Claude or ChatGPT a live line to the AI Media Buyer. Every morning it reads your ad accounts and tells you what needs you. During the day you answer in plain words: "approve the kills in Acme", "keep 1 and 3, retire 2", "make six more", "post the approved ones now", "run the review for Acme now". And you make ads with it: brainstorm from what Cortana knows about your account, then order them ("make me 20 for Acme, lean on the warranty angle") and Cortana writes every hook and every prompt, reading your direction against the account data first.

You will end up with one connection, three scheduled tasks (the morning review, an order, and rendering), one project that knows how to be your media buyer, and a report for every business you run.

<Note>
  The analysis itself runs on Cortana's servers on Cortana's usage. Your assistant reads the results and carries your decisions back. It never sees how Cortana decides.
</Note>

## Before you start

1. **The AI Media Buyer is switched on for your account.** Cortana staff enable it per business after implementation. If a business has no media buyer page in Cortana, it is not on yet. {/* TODO: verify the sidebar label that opens /business/{id}/media-buyer */}
2. **A media buyer Slack channel is connected** for any business you want to launch ads from. Launch instructions can only be saved once a channel exists, because the Go live and Undo buttons live on those Slack cards.
3. **A paid Claude plan** if you want Claude to run its scheduled tasks. ChatGPT scheduled tasks work with connected apps. {/* TODO: verify: live test T1/T2, a ChatGPT Task calling the connector */}

## Connect once

1. In Cortana, open a business's media buyer page {/* TODO: verify the sidebar label */}, then the **Settings** tab, then the **Delivery** tab. Under **Analysis & Notifications**, next to the Slack channel, the **Claude / ChatGPT** block shows what is connected on your account. Click **Connect Claude / ChatGPT** (or **Manage connections** once one exists).
2. **Step 1 · Show steps for.** Choose **Claude** or **ChatGPT** and the steps below change to match. It only changes the steps: the connector URL is the same for both, and you can connect both.
3. **Step 2 · Connect Claude** (or **Connect ChatGPT**). Copy the connector URL from the page and add it in your assistant: in Claude, **Settings**, then **Connectors**, then **Add custom connector** {/* TODO: verify; Claude's help centre today says Customize, then Connectors, then +, then Add custom connector */}; in ChatGPT, **Settings**, then **Connectors**, then **Create**. {/* TODO: verify ChatGPT's current labels (Connectors may now be labelled Apps or Plugins; OpenAI's developer-mode page links chatgpt.com/plugins and enables Developer mode under Settings, then Security and login) */} Sign in with your Cortana login.
4. On the Cortana consent screen, choose what the connection may reach:
   - **All my businesses, including ones I add later** is one switch. Leave it on and every business you can access today, and every one you add later, is covered without reconnecting. Turn it off to pick businesses one by one.
   - **Let Claude act on your media buyer decisions** (or ChatGPT) is on by default. With it on, the assistant can approve the kills and scales you ask for, manage creatives, order ads with your direction and wait for them, edit your launch instructions, arm and post creatives, and start billed renders, generation runs and reviews when you ask. Turn it off for a read-only connection that reports and never acts.
5. Click **Approve**. Back in Cortana the page updates by itself and lists the connection with its date and last use.

On first connect the assistant presents your report once, unprompted, then tells you what it can do.

## The scheduled tasks

**Step 3 · Three scheduled tasks, unprompted** on the Connect page carries the recipe and the three prompts: the morning review, an order, and rendering (ChatGPT only). The one-page setup with every prompt is [Set up your AI media buyer in Claude or ChatGPT](/mcp/ai-media-buyer-schedule).

1. In your assistant, open **Scheduled tasks** (Claude) or **Tasks** (ChatGPT) and create a task for each prompt.
2. Set the morning review to repeat every morning at a time after your Cortana review time, so the report is fresh. The review time is in each business's media buyer settings. Set the order task to the rhythm you want new ads written, one per business. Set the render task daily for a business that renders on your own subscription.
3. Paste each prompt from the Connect page (click **Copy** on its card) as the task's instructions, fill in the placeholders, and turn the Cortana connector on for the task. The morning prompt is:

```text
Call get_review_report for all my businesses and present it: what needs me first, then what's on watch, then one line per healthy business. Show each card's tables as tables, then the call. Collapse healthy businesses after 8. If nothing needs me, say so in one line. When I say kill, keep, approve or undo, do it at once, no confirmation.
```

4. Save them. The first run lands at the next time. You can run a task once by hand to see it today.

The report always reads in this order: what needs you (open kills, raises, trims, a judgment call, a failed action, cards in In production waiting for their image), then what was done and is undoable, then ads on watch, then one line per healthy business. A card that needs a call, or had an action taken, carries the same 3, 7, 14, 21 and 30 day tables as your Slack card, shown as tables, then the call. A quiet morning is one line. Every report says what time it is "as of"; if it is older than your review cadence the assistant says so and offers to run the review now.

## Make it your media buyer

**Step 4 · Make it your media buyer** on the Connect page carries the instructions that turn a plain assistant into your media buyer: how to present the morning report, how to act on your decisions, how to order ads with your direction, and how to render on your own subscription. No code, nothing to install.

1. In your assistant, create a new project for your media buyer.
2. On the Connect page, under **Step 4 · Make it your media buyer**, click **Copy** and paste the text as the project's instructions.
3. In each chat, turn the Cortana connector on before you ask: in Claude, the **+** button, then **Connectors**; in ChatGPT, the **+** menu, then **Developer mode**, and pick Cortana. {/* TODO: verify in the live UIs; vendor wording as of 2026-09-20: Claude "enable connectors for individual conversations via the + button, then Connectors", OpenAI "Choose Developer mode from the Plus menu and select the apps for the conversation" */} Your chats there follow the instructions; the scheduled tasks keep working either way. On a Claude Team or Enterprise plan, connectors work in private projects only. {/* TODO: verify against Claude's current help centre */}

The page shows the kit's version under the text. When a newer version appears, paste it again.

## Write the ads yourself with the playbook

**Step 5 · Give it the playbook** on the Connect page offers the three files Cortana writes with: the media buyer playbook, the image designer, the video scripter. Download them into your project's knowledge and the chat can write ads itself, on your own subscription, from the engine's plan (`plan_ads`), the winners as stamps, the conversion snapshot and the sales calls, then post the ones you like to Meta paused. The full walk is [Make ads in your own chat with the playbook](/mcp/ai-media-buyer-playbook). Ordering with `make_ads` stays the way to have Cortana write them.

## What you can ask

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Review and approve">
    "What needs me?" gives the report for every business, or one business by name. "Why is that a kill?" opens the detail behind one card: the numbers and the reasoning. "What are my best ads and why?" lists the winners.

    "Approve the kills", "reject the Beta scale", "undo the Acme raise" act at once on the items you name. Executed kills and scales can be undone for 72 hours. When the same request matches several businesses, the assistant asks which one first. A judgment call in the report (an Ask) takes one of its options or your own words, such as "set the target to $80".

    In Claude and ChatGPT the report can open as a panel: the same cards and tables, with a **Kill** / **Keep** button on each open card, **Undo** on an executed one, and the judgment call's options as buttons. A tap does exactly what the words do, at once, with no confirmation. Where your assistant does not show panels, the report is the same text and tables. The panel needs the acting switch on. {/* TODO: verify: live test, the panel rendering in claude.ai and in ChatGPT */}
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Make ads with your assistant">
    Say "help me make ads" or "what should we make next". The assistant asks Cortana for the creative brief once: your offer as you wrote it, the ads that won and why, the angles Cortana has not covered lately, what prospects say on your sales calls, your launch rules, and how past concepts actually played out. It brainstorms with you from that. Every idea names its angle and the winner or prospect line it builds on.

    When you know what you want, order it: "make me 20 for Acme and lean on the warranty angle", or "use exactly this hook: ..." for words to keep verbatim. Cortana reads your direction against the account data first and answers go, caution or stop with the reason and a recommendation, plus the quote (how many image concepts and scripts, who renders); it writes only on your yes. Then the assistant waits with "N of M written" until the batch is done. Cortana writes every hook and every prompt; your words are the direction, never the copy. An order with no direction starts at once.

    Where the images come from depends on **Who renders the images**. Set it once for all your businesses in Control Center, Media Buyer, Overview: every business follows it unless its own row in the fleet table, or its own media buyer settings, says otherwise. Cortana renders them on its side (about $0.04 to $0.08 per image, billed as usage) and they land in **Review**; on your own subscription the cards wait in **In production** with their prompts, and one ChatGPT render task (or you, by hand) renders and uploads them for every business at once. Once launched, its ads feed Cortana's ledger like any other ad.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Creatives">
    Iterate an image with your assistant, then say "that one": it lands in the **Review** column of your **Ad Pipeline**. "What's new in review?" numbers the board so "keep 1, 3, kill 2" works. A note on an image ("make the headline bigger on 3") depends on who renders for that business: where Cortana renders, it re-renders that image on Cortana's side and the previous version stays in the card's history; where the business renders on your own subscription, Cortana writes a new prompt and your render task (or you, with Claude) renders it again.

    "Make six more for Acme" starts at once: the concepts arrive within minutes and the images with the overnight batch, or wait for your render task on your own subscription. "What would six cost?" answers the quote (how many image concepts and scripts, who renders, the minutes) without starting anything. There is no cap on an order.

    "Show me what's ready" in Claude or ChatGPT opens the Review column as a wall of images: every card as a tile with its images, and under each **Approve**, **Deny** with a one-line why, and **Iterate** with a note on one image. Approve arms the card for the next posting slot, Deny retires it and keeps your reason, Iterate re-renders or re-prompts that image as a note in chat would. Where panels are not shown, "what's new in review" lists the same cards with their image links. {/* TODO: verify: live test, the wall in claude.ai and in ChatGPT */}
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What it learns from you">
    Two things you say become instructions for the next batch. A reason on a rejection ("kill 2, it looks desperate", or the why under Deny on the wall) is kept as your taste: the writer and the designer see the newest reasons and steer away from them. A standing rule ("from now on, never mention a competitor's price", "always show the founder") is binding on every ad Cortana writes from then on, above any winner: an ad that once won by breaking the rule is not proposed again. "What are my rules" lists them; "drop the rule about prices" removes one; each business keeps up to ten. The rules are also a box you can type into under **Standing creative rules** in that business's media buyer settings: add one, reword one in place, delete one. A rule saved there and a rule said in chat are the same rule. The morning report says once what Cortana learned that week: "learned: 3 standing rules, 2 rejections this week".
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Posting">
    "From now on, launch new ads into my CBO campaign, one ad per ad set, US and UK only" rewrites your **Launch** instructions; **Scaling** and **Kill** work the same way. The assistant reads the saved text back so you see exactly what is stored. "What's armed?" lists the board. "Post the approved ones now" runs the posting slot right away under your per-slot limit and counts as today's slot. If the ads would go live with real spend, the assistant tells you so first and posts only on your yes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Run the review now">
    "Run the review for Acme now" starts a full review on Cortana's side at the usual usage price. One review runs at a time per business. It takes a few minutes; ask "is it done?" for the status, the ads reviewed, and the cost line. Once done, the report is fresh.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What it will and won't do

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Read-only connections">
    With the acting switch off, the assistant reports, explains, lists the review board and the posting setup, and never approves, posts, renders, orders or runs anything. Decisions stay in Slack or the app; ordering ads from chat needs the acting switch, so connect again with it on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What the assistant can see">
    Verdicts, costs against your targets, the reasoning behind each call, counts, names, your own instructions and settings, and image links. It has no access to how Cortana decides, and it says so if asked.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Slack and the app stay in step">
    A decision made in chat updates the Slack card ("Approved by you via Claude") and the app. There is one record of every decision, whichever door you used.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Costs">
    Talking to your assistant is on your assistant's plan. Orders, renders, generation runs and reviews started from chat run on Cortana and are billed as usage to the wallet that pays for the business, the same as from the app: one usage line per run, naming what it wrote and rendered. On your own subscription no render is billed by Cortana: the writing is, the rendering is your assistant's.

    The wallet is the only limit. An order is checked against it before anything is written: when the wallet cannot cover it, the assistant says "Your wallet is empty, no more Cortana usage credits until it is topped up" with the top-up link, and nothing runs. A review or a re-render is refused the same way. If the wallet runs out during an order, Cortana stops at the next step with what is already written and says why; nothing goes below zero. A business whose wallet is suspended or empty is skipped by the overnight review, and its morning report starts with "paused: wallet suspended" (or empty) and the top-up link; reviews and orders resume by themselves once it is topped up. A normal order shows no price; ask "what would six cost?" for the quote.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Disconnecting">
    On the Connect page, click **Disconnect** on a connection and confirm. It loses access to every business it covered, and every scheduled task it runs stops. You can connect again from the same page.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The morning report says it is older than my review cadence. What do I do?">
    The last review ran before your cadence would expect a new one. Ask the assistant to run the review now, or move your scheduled task to a time after the review time in that business's media buyer settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did it ask me which business?">
    The items you named exist in more than one business. Say the business and it acts at once.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why won't it save my launch instructions?">
    That business has no media buyer Slack channel yet. Launches need the channel for the Go live and Undo buttons, so connect one in the media buyer settings first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did 'post now' refuse?">
    Nothing is armed, no posting schedule exists for that business, a posting slot is already running, or the launch would be held anyway (a template import waiting for your confirmation, or a Slack channel that cannot be reached). The answer says which.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it spend money without asking?">
    Kills and scales you approve execute at once and can be undone for 72 hours. "Post now" from chat reads a launch that would go live with real spend back for your yes first. Ads you keep in chat launch at the next posting slot under your launch mode: Draft waits for your Go-live tap in Slack; Live, or an empty Launch box, goes live. Renders, generation and reviews start only when you ask and cost usage, not ad spend.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I added a new business. Do I need to reconnect?">
    Not if you left **All my businesses, including ones I add later** on. It is covered on the next call. If you picked businesses one by one, connect again and add it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did Cortana push back on my direction?">
    Cortana read your direction against your account's own history: the winners, the angles the last cycles covered, and how past concepts on that angle actually did. "Caution" or "stop" names the reason and recommends how to unify what is working with your idea. You can still say yes; the read is advice, not a veto. Changed words are read again before they run.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A card says it is waiting for its image. What now?">
    That business renders on your own subscription, so Cortana wrote the prompt and the image has not come home yet. In ChatGPT, run the render task (or say "render everything waiting across my businesses"): it renders every waiting prompt exactly as written, for every business that renders on your own subscription, and uploads each to its wrap. {/* TODO: verify: live test T1/T2, a ChatGPT scheduled task rendering and uploading through the connector */} In Claude, ask for the queue, render each prompt where you like, then share the image and ask Claude to upload it for that wrap; if Claude says it cannot send files, the card keeps waiting and the morning report reminds you. Setting **Who renders the images** to Cortana makes Cortana render every order from then on; the cards already waiting stay in **In production** until you ask your assistant to re-render one, by its card, or upload an image for it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It said my wallet is empty. What happens to the order?">
    Nothing ran and nothing was billed: an order is checked against the wallet before it starts. Top up from the link in the answer (your business's billing page, or the agency wallet when that is the one paying) and place the order again. An order that ran out mid-way kept what was written; the cards are on the board and the run's note says where it stopped.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I stop it from using an angle I hate?">
    Tell your assistant a standing rule: "from now on, never compare us to the competitor's price". It is saved in your words, binds every ad from then on, and beats a winner. You can add, reword and delete rules under **Standing creative rules** in the business's media buyer settings. A one-off "no, not that" with a reason (a Deny with a why on the wall, or "kill 2, too salesy") is kept as taste for the next batch without becoming a rule.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I connect both Claude and ChatGPT?">
    Yes. The connector URL is the same for both; connect each one from its own settings and paste each its own instructions from the Connect page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says it can't do something and will send it to Cortana's engineers.">
    That is a real request filed with Cortana's team, with your words. It returns a reference; there is no promised date.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
