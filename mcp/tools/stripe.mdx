---
title: "Stripe"
description: "Payments, subscriptions, customers, SaaS metrics and the rest of your Stripe account."
---

`get_stripe_financials` lets your AI read your Stripe data the way Cortana stores it: revenue, MRR and churn, customers with their lifetime value, and your products, prices and invoices. One tool, many modes: pick the mode for the question.

<Note>
  Needs Stripe connected in Cortana. Your Stripe integration's MCP permissions decide which modes your AI sees: a mode you switched off never appears. Every tool takes an optional `businessId` when your connection reaches more than one business.
</Note>

### `get_stripe_financials`

| Parameter | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `mode` | enum | Yes | `payments`, `subscriptions`, `customers`, `metrics`, `products`, `invoices`, `disputes`, `payment_intents`, `payment_links`, `prices` or `promotion_codes` |
| `from` / `to` | string | For `metrics` and `payments` | Date range, ISO 8601 |
| `status` | string | No | Payments: `succeeded` or `failed`. Invoices: `draft`, `open`, `paid`, `uncollectible` or `void`. |
| `isRecurring` | string | No | `true` for recurring payments only |
| `stripeCustomerId` | string | No | Only this Stripe customer |
| `customerId` | string | No | Invoices for this Stripe customer |
| `hasActiveSubscription` | string | No | `true` or `false`, for customers |
| `periodType` | enum | No | `daily` or `monthly`, for metrics |
| `active` | string | No | `true` or `false`, for payment links, prices and promotion codes |
| `productId` | string | No | Prices for this product |
| `type` | enum | No | `recurring` or `one_time`, for prices |
| `sort` | string | No | e.g. `-totalRevenue`, `-amount`, `-paymentDate` |
| `limit` | number | No | Page size, max 100 |
| `page` | number | No | Page number |

## Revenue

### `payments` mode

Payment history with amounts, status and the customer. Ask for `status: "succeeded"` when you mean revenue.

**Ask it:** "How much did we collect in Stripe last month?"

### `payment_intents` mode

Each payment intent: its id, amount, status and customer.

**Ask it:** "Show me the failed payment attempts this week."

### `invoices` mode

Invoices with status, amounts and due dates. Filter by customer or status.

**Ask it:** "Which invoices are still open?"

### `disputes` mode

Open and recent disputes with amounts, reasons and evidence deadlines.

**Ask it:** "Do we have any disputes due soon?"

## Subscriptions and SaaS metrics

### `subscriptions` mode

Active and past subscriptions, each with its MRR contribution.

**Ask it:** "How many active subscriptions do we have on the annual plan?"

### `metrics` mode

Daily or monthly KPIs: MRR, ARR, churn, net and gross revenue retention, ARPU, LTV and quick ratio. Needs `from` and `to`.

**Ask it:** "What was our MRR and churn each month this year?"

## Customers

### `customers` mode

Your customer roster with lifetime value, MRR and churn dates.

**Ask it:** "Who are our top 10 customers by lifetime value?"

## Catalog

### `products` mode

Your product catalog with its pricing.

### `prices` mode

Prices with amounts, currencies, intervals and the product each belongs to.

### `payment_links` mode

Your payment links with their URLs and products.

### `promotion_codes` mode

Promotion codes and coupons with their discounts and usage.

**Ask it:** "Which promo codes were used the most?"
