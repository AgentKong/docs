description: "Run your AI Media Buyer from chat: the review, decisions, new ads, creative review, posting, schedules and settings."

These tools let your AI work your AI Media Buyer the way you would in Slack and in the app: read the review, approve or undo what it proposes, order new ads, review creatives, decide when ads post, and set up a new client. Everything your AI does here is the same action as the matching button in Slack or the app.

<Note>
  Needs the AI Media Buyer switched on for the business. Reading needs **AI Media Buyer** ticked on the connection. Anything that changes your account (approving, ordering ads, posting, settings) also needs **Let (your assistant) act on your media buyer decisions** ticked when you connect.
</Note>

Every tool takes an optional `businessId` when your connection reaches more than one business.

## Reports & decisions

### `get_review_report`
The media buyer's review: what needs you (kills, budget raises and trims, a judgment call, a failed action, concepts waiting for an image), what it did and can still undo, what is on watch, and how each business is doing in your own event names.

No parameters. Leave out `businessId` to get every business on the connection as a summary; pass one for that business in full, with every card's numbers and reasoning.

**Returns:** the review, sorted by what needs you first.

**Ask it:** "What happened overnight across my accounts?"

### `approve_proposals`
Approve open cards from the review by their ids. The action runs on Meta right away, exactly like the Approve button in Slack, and stays undoable for 72 hours. Changes your account.

| Parameter | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `proposalIds` | string[] | Yes | The cards to approve, from `get_review_report` (up to 20 per call). |

**Returns:** one line per card: executed, skipped (no longer waiting) or failed with the reason.

**Ask it:** "Approve the two kills in Acme."

### `reject_proposals`
Reject open cards. Nothing runs and the card closes as rejected, like the Reject button in Slack. Changes your account.

| Parameter | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `proposalIds` | string[] | Yes | The cards to reject (up to 20 per call). |

**Returns:** rejected or skipped, per card.

**Ask it:** "Don't kill Hook B, leave it running."

### `undo_actions`
Undo actions that already ran (a kill, a pause, a budget raise or trim) within 72 hours, like the Undo button in Slack. If the budget moved since, the undo declines and says so. Changes your account.

| Parameter | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `proposalIds` | string[] | Yes | The executed actions to undo, from the report (up to 20 per call). |

**Returns:** undone or declined, per action, with the reason.

**Ask it:** "Undo the scale in Beta."

### `answer_ask`
Answer a judgment call from the review: kill on target, keep the winners, adjust the target, wait one cycle, or keep the target. Your own words work too. Changes your account.

| Parameter | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `askId` | string | Yes | The judgment call, from `get_review_report`. |
| `option` | enum | No | `KILL_ON_TARGET`, `KEEP_WINNERS`, `ADJUST_TARGET`, `WAIT_ONE_CYCLE` or `KEEP_TARGET`. |
| `text` | string | No | Your answer in your own words, when you don't name an option. |
| `newTarget` | number | No | With `ADJUST_TARGET`: the new target. Leave out to use the suggested one. |

**Returns:** what was done. If your words can't be matched, the options to choose from.

**Ask it:** "Keep the winners and wait a week on the rest."

## Ordering new ads

| Parameter | Type | Required | Description |

**Ask it:** "Make 10 ads for R&G and lean on the warranty angle."

**Ask it:** "What would 20 new ads take?"

## Posting & launches

### `get_posting_setup`
How this business posts and creates ads: the Launch, Scaling and Kill instructions in your words, Draft or Live, the posting days and next slot, the review and ad creation schedules, the floor, and how many ads each creation run makes.

No parameters.

**Returns:** the full posting setup.

**Ask it:** "When do new ads go up next, and what's my floor?"

### `set_launch_instructions`
Write one instruction box in your own words. **Launch** decides where new ads go (campaign, ad sets, budgets) and can name a base ad to copy text from, or how to name the ads ("name each ad its file name"). **Scaling** says how winners are scaled. **Kill** adds your own stop rules. Replaces the whole box. Changes your account.

| Parameter | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `box` | enum | Yes | `LAUNCH`, `SCALING` or `KILL`. |
| `text` | string | Yes | The whole box in your words. Empty clears it. |

**Returns:** the box as saved.

**Ask it:** "From now on launch new ads into the Broad campaign and name each ad its file name."

### `set_posting_schedule`
Set when queued ads post: the days, the time, on or off, the most ads per slot, and Draft (paused drafts you switch on in Slack) or Live (real spend at the slot, no tap). Live is only saved after you confirm. Changes your account.

| Parameter | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `days` | string[] | No | e.g. `["MON","THU"]`. |
| `timeOfDay` | string | No | `HH:MM`, 24-hour. |
| `timezone` | string | No | e.g. `America/Toronto`. |
| `enabled` | boolean | No | Posting on or off. |
| `maxPerSlot` | number | No | The most ads one slot posts (1–100), or null for no cap. |
| `launchMode` | enum | No | `DRAFT` or `LIVE`. |
| `confirmLive` | boolean | No | `true` once you confirmed Live. |

**Returns:** the schedule as saved and what changed.

**Ask it:** "Post Monday and Thursday at 8am, 3 ads a slot, Live. Confirmed."

### `list_board`

| Parameter | Type | Required | Description |

| `limit` | number | No | Rows per page (default 25, max 100). |
| `cursor` | string | No | The previous page's cursor. |

**Returns:** the cards.

**Ask it:** "What's queued to post?"

### `arm_cards`

### `queue_roster_ads`
Put your best ads from your agency's Ad Library into a client's pipeline, ranked by how they did across your other accounts. Ads the client already has, ads whose file is missing, and ads a client in the same market runs are skipped with the reason. Changes your account.

| Parameter | Type | Required | Description |

| `preview` | boolean | No | Show the picks without adding them. |

**Returns:** what was added and what was skipped, with reasons.

**Ask it:** "Launch the 4 best ads from our Ad Library for Acme."

### `post_now`
Run the posting slot now instead of waiting. Queued ads go out under the business's Launch instructions. If they'd go live with real spend, your AI asks you first. Changes your account.

| Parameter | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Schedules & output

### `set_review_and_creation_schedule`
Set when the media buyer reviews your ads, when it creates new ones, and the floor. Scheduled ad creation skips its run while at least the floor's number of ads wait in Ready to publish (ads in Review don't count). Changes your account.

| Parameter | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `reviews` | object | No | `days`, `timeOfDay`, `timezone`, `enabled` for the review. |
| `creatingAds` | object | No | The same, for ad creation. |
| `floor` | number | No | 1–500, or null for the default (one week of posting). |

**Returns:** the schedules and floor as they now run, and what changed.

**Ask it:** "Create new ads Mondays at 9am, and set the floor to 5."

### `set_creative_output`
Set what each scheduled creation run makes: image ads and video scripts per batch, the longest a script runs, and how many image versions per concept. Changes your account.

| Parameter | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `imageAds` | number | No | Image ads per batch (0–30). |
| `videoScripts` | number | No | Video scripts per batch (0–30). 0 means no scripts. |
| `maxScriptSeconds` | number | No | 10–180 seconds. |
| `maxVariantsPerConcept` | number | No | 1–20 image versions per concept. |

**Returns:** the output before and after.

**Ask it:** "5 image ads per batch, no scripts."

### `create_business`
Create a new client business from a template: its tracking setup, media buyer setup and Ad Library ads come with it (the ads wait in Review). Changes your account.

| Parameter | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `name` | string | Yes | The client's business name. |
| `templateId` | string | Yes | From `list_business_templates`. |
| `ownership` | enum | Yes | `client` or `us`. |
| `industry` | enum | Yes | The closest industry. |
| `industryOther` | string | No | The industry in words, when it's `OTHER`. |
| `timezone` | string | Yes | e.g. `America/Toronto`. |
| `website` | string | No | The client's website. |
| `serviceArea` | string | No | Where they serve, e.g. "Greater Toronto Area" or a ZIP list. |
| `turnOn` | boolean | Yes | Switch on ad tracking and the AI Media Buyer. |
| `destinationUrl` | string | No | The landing page new ads send people to. |

**Returns:** the new business and what came with it.

**Ask it:** "Onboard Acme Windows from our main template."

### `get_ad_tracking_licenses`
Your plan's Ad Tracking and AI Media Buyer licenses (total, in use, free) and which businesses have each on.

| Parameter | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `limit` | number | No | Rows per page (default 25, max 100). |
| `cursor` | string | No | The previous page's cursor. |

**Returns:** the license counts and each business's status.

**Ask it:** "How many media buyer licenses are free?"

### `set_ad_tracking`
Switch Ad Tracking on or off for up to 100 businesses. Switching off also switches the AI Media Buyer off for that business. Changes your account.

| Parameter | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `businessIds` | string[] | No | Businesses to switch (up to 100). |
| `businessId` | string | No | One business instead. |
| `on` | boolean | Yes | On or off. |

**Returns:** one line per business.

**Ask it:** "Switch tracking on for these three clients."

### `set_ai_media_buyer`
Switch the AI Media Buyer on or off for up to 100 businesses (switching on turns tracking on too, where it's off), and set who's emailed when Meta disables an ad account (up to 5 addresses). Changes your account.

| Parameter | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `businessIds` | string[] | No | Businesses to switch (up to 100). |
| `businessId` | string | No | One business instead. |
| `on` | boolean | No | On or off. Leave out to change only the emails. |
| `billingAlertEmails` | string[] | No | Who gets the payment and disabled-account alerts. Replaces the list; empty clears it. |

**Returns:** one line per business.

**Ask it:** "Turn the media buyer on for Acme and send payment alerts to billing@acme.com."
