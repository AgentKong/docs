---
title: "History"
description: "Who changed what, and from where: a record's history, or everything that changed in your business lately."
---

Since History arrived in Cortana, every change to an appointment, a calendar, a deal, a pipeline or a custom metric is kept with who made it and from where: a teammate in the app, a lead on your booking page, your own app through an API key, Claude or ChatGPT acting for someone, or a CRM's sync. Changes made before then have no history, except a conversion's value, attribution and time edits, which Cortana already kept on each conversion. This tool reads it back in the same words as the **History** panel in Cortana.

<Note>
  On every connection, with nothing extra to tick on the consent screen. It reads what the connection's other tools read: appointments, calendars, deals and pipelines on a connection that reads your CRM contacts, custom metrics on one that reads attribution, and conversions on one that reads conversion events. Each is on every connection by default. A custom metric's or a conversion's history also needs your Ad Tracking **View** permission. Takes an optional `businessId` when your connection reaches more than one business.
</Note>

### `get_history`

One record's history, or your business's recent changes. Newest first, 25 lines a page.

| Parameter | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `record` | object | No | One record: `{ "kind": "deal", "id": "…" }`. `kind` is `appointment`, `calendar`, `deal`, `pipeline`, `custom_metric` or `conversion`. Leave out for your business's recent changes. |
| `kind` | enum | No | Recent changes to one kind of record only |
| `actorKind` | enum | No | Recent changes by one kind of actor only: `person` (a teammate in the app or the Chrome extension), `contact` (a lead on the booking page), `api_key` (an app using an API key), `ai` (Claude, ChatGPT or another AI, for a person), `crm_sync`, `automation`, `system` |
| `cursor` | string | No | The `nextCursor` of the previous page, for older lines |

**One record** reads its whole history. A pipeline's includes its stages' changes. A conversion's folds in its own value, attribution and time edits: who changed the value of a sale, its attribution, or its time.

**Your business's recent changes** cover the last 30 days across every record you may see, each line naming its record so your AI can read that record's history next. A conversion's value, attribution and time edits are kept on each conversion, so ask about that conversion to see them.

**Returns:** each line as the History panel reads it, with its time on the business's clock and each changed field before and after:

```text
Moved to Won · Sarah Lee, in the app
Booked for Fri, Nov 20, 3:00 PM · Acme Scheduler, via API
Value $4,000.00 → $3,500.00 · Shopify sync
Rescheduled to Wed, Nov 25, 4:30 PM · Claude, for Sarah Lee
```

**Ask it:** "Who moved the Acme deal to Lost?" · "What did Claude change today?" · "Who edited the value of that $4,000 sale?" · "What changed on my 30-minute calendar this week?"
