## What Cortana Calendar Does

Cortana Calendar lets people book calls with you. You put your booking page online. Someone opens it, answers a few short questions, picks a time, and books.

The booking then shows up in your dashboard. It is not only a diary entry. It is counted as a **tracked conversion**, which means Cortana ties it to the ad the person came from.

That is the part a normal booking tool cannot do. Your booking page also does the tracking. So for every call in your diary, you can see which ad, which campaign and which channel brought that person in.

To open it, click **Appointments** in the menu on the left of your business.

## The Four Tabs

The **Appointments** area has four tabs across the top. Here is what each one does.

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Business Cal** | Your day-to-day diary. It shows every booking from every booking page. Switch between month, week and day view. Click any booking to mark what happened on the call, or to hand it to someone else. |
| **Events** | The lists behind the diary. **Appointment Booked** shows every booking you have taken. **Form Submitted** shows how many people opened the page, how many started answering the questions, and how many finished and booked. |
| **Calendars** | Where you build and edit your booking pages. Set the questions you ask, the hours you are free, who takes each call, the reminder emails, and the link you share. |
| **Settings** | Your business settings. Pick the timezone your bookings run on. Set the hours each person on your team can take calls. Connect Google, Outlook, Zoom and other booking tools. Choose the web address your confirmation emails come from. |
