## What Cortana Calendar Does

Cortana Calendar is the booking system built into your business. You publish a branded scheduling page, someone answers a few questions, picks a time, and books — and the appointment lands in your dashboard as a **tracked conversion**, not just a calendar entry.

That last part is what makes it different from a standalone scheduling tool. Because the booking page is also a tracking page, you can see which ad, campaign or channel produced each booked call.

You'll find it under **Appointments** in the left sidebar of your business.

</Card>

## The Four Tabs

The Appointments area is split into four tabs.

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Business Cal** | Your working calendar. Every appointment across every booking page, in month, week or day view. Click one to change its status or reassign it. |
| **Events** | The records behind the calendar. **Appointment Booked** lists every booking; **Form Submitted** shows the funnel from page view to partial answer to completed booking. |
| **Calendars** | Where you build and edit booking pages — questions, hours, routing, reminders and the shareable link. |
| **Settings** | Business booking timezone and team working hours, connections to Google, Outlook, Zoom and external booking systems, and the domain your confirmation emails send from. |
